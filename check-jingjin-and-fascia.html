经筋和筋膜：原文怎么说

Sinew channels and fascia: what the sources say · 2026-11-23 · v1

本文为出处核查，不评价疗效，不提供诊疗建议。 This is a source check. It makes no claim about effects and gives no medical advice.

经筋原文讲什么 · What the sinew channels are

《灵枢·经筋》十二条，每条只写「起于某处、结于某处」，不写属哪一脏、络哪一腑。这是经筋和经脉最根本的不同。例：

「手心主之筋，起于中指，与太阴之筋并行，结于肘内廉，上臂阴结腋下，下散前后挟胁；其支者，入腋，散胸中，结于臂。」（通行本作「结于臂」，另有本作「结于贲」。）

Each of the twelve gives only an origin and the places it reaches. None is attached to an organ: this is the basic difference from the meridians.

「结」是走向，不是疙瘩 · Jie means "reaches", not a lump

篇中「结、聚、散、布、着」都是讲路线的字：走到、汇到、铺开、贴着。例：「上结于踝」「结于腘」「聚于阴器」「散胸中」「着于脊」。原文没有「筋结」一词。

Jie, ju, san, bu and zhuo all describe routes: reaching, converging, spreading, attaching. The term "sinew knot" does not appear in the chapter.

「以痛为输」 · "Take the painful spot as the point"

意思是拿痛的地方当腧穴。在《灵枢·经筋》出现 13 次：十二经筋各一次，手太阳之筋两次（通行本，两个来源对读一致）。前后是针刺操作，这里不引。

It appears 13 times in the received text: once in each of the twelve channels and twice in the hand taiyang channel in the chapter. The surrounding words are needling instructions and are not quoted here.

分层讲的是刺法，不是病位 · The layers are about needling, not about where illness lies

《素问·刺要论》分七层（毫毛腠理、皮肤、肌肉、脉、筋、骨、髓），《素问·刺齐论》《灵枢·官针》分五层，语境都是针刺手法。「病在筋不在脉」这个判断，原文里没有。《灵枢·九针十二原》还说，穴位（节）「神气之所游行出入也，非皮肉筋骨也」。

The Suwen divides the body into seven layers in one chapter and five in another, and the Lingshu into five; all three passages concern needling technique. None says illness lies in the sinew rather than the vessels. The Lingshu also says the points are "where spirit and qi travel in and out; not skin, flesh, sinew or bone".

筋膜 · Fascia

现代定义的共同点是：片状、连续、包被、遍布全身。综述支持力可以沿肌筋膜链传导，至少 3 条链证据良好；传导量受哪些因素影响，还有争议。没有研究把十二经筋和肌筋膜链逐条对应。

Fascia is defined as sheet-like, continuous, enveloping and body-wide. Reviews support force transmission along at least three myofascial chains; what affects how much force is transmitted is still debated. No study maps the twelve sinew channels onto them one to one.

可以并列的，和不能等同的 · Side by side, not the same

  • 可以并列：走向、汇聚、按痛处取。
  • 不能等同：脏腑配属、字义，以及「经筋就是筋膜」。《说文》：「筋，肉之力也。」更接近肌腱，不是筋膜。

They can be set side by side on routes, convergences and taking the painful spot. They cannot be equated: organ links, the meaning of the character (the Shuowen defines jin as "the strength of flesh", closer to tendon than to fascia), and "sinew channels are fascia".

流传的说法 · Popular sayings

「筋松一寸，气通三尺」找不到古书出处，疑为现代口诀。有原文的是《素问·生气通天论》「骨正筋柔，气血以流」（常被改作「气血自流」）。

A popular saying about loosening the sinews by an inch has no classical source. The genuine line is 「骨正筋柔，气血以流」 from the Suwen, often misquoted.
