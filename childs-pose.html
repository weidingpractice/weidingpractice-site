婴儿式Child's Pose

Balasana

额头点地，慢慢左右转动额头。Rest your forehead on the floor and roll it slowly from side to side.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

印
印堂 Yintang
GV29
有体会 · felt

两眉头连线的正中。

Midway between the inner ends of the eyebrows.

怎么做How I practise it

我的发现（2026-11-13）。头部有压力的这几天，在婴儿式里额头点地，慢慢转动额头，感觉有缓解的作用。
My discovery (13 Nov 2026).On days when my head felt pressure, resting my forehead on the floor in Child's Pose and rolling it slowly brought relief.
转的时候碰到哪里。正中是印堂；往两边转，压过眉毛上方的阳白（胆经），再往外接近太阳穴。额头往上一点，是神庭、上星（督脉）。
What it touches as it rolls.At the centre, Yintang; rolling to either side passes over Yangbai above the eyebrows (Gallbladder channel) and towards the temples. A little higher on the forehead lie Shenting and Shangxing (Governing Vessel).
