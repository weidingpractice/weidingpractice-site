婴儿式

Child's PoseBālāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →✓ 有体会 · felt1 个穴位 · 1 条经络
额头点地，慢慢左右转动额头。Rest your forehead on the floor and roll it slowly from side to side.
口令 · Cue

往阴，往阳Toward yin, toward yang

更阴 More yin

  • 手往后放，掌心向上。Arms back by the sides, palms up.

再阴，就躺下来。More yin: lie down.

本式 The pose

臀部坐向脚跟，额头落地。Hips toward the heels, forehead to the floor.

更阳 More yang

  • 双手向前伸：力臂长了，更阳一点。Arms reaching forward: a longer lever, a little more yang.

再阳，起来到四足着地，再到下犬式。 下犬式 Downward Dog →More yang: come up onto all fours, then into Downward Dog.

怎么做Practice note

我的发现（2026-11-13）。头部有压力的这几天，在婴儿式里额头点地，慢慢转动额头，头觉得轻了一些。My discovery (13 Nov 2026).On days when my head felt pressure, resting my forehead on the floor in Child's Pose and rolling it slowly, my head felt a little lighter.

转的时候碰到哪里。正中是印堂；往两边转，压过眉毛上方的阳白（胆经），再往外接近太阳穴。额头往上一点，是神庭、上星（督脉）。What it touches as it rolls.At the centre, Yintang; rolling to either side passes over Yangbai above the eyebrows (Gallbladder channel) and towards the temples. A little higher on the forehead lie Shenting and Shangxing (Governing Vessel).
