下焦Xiajiao · 区域 · Area · Lower jiao

用手怎么找How to find it

手掌放在肚脐以下，一直到耻骨。这一片，连同盆腔，是下焦。
Rest your palm below your navel, down to the pubic bone. This area, with the pelvis behind it, is the lower jiao.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。 You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

三焦是什么三焦是六腑里的「孤之腑」：别的腑都和一个脏配对，只有它没有。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：那是一条从无名指到耳后的线。
What the Sanjiao isThe Sanjiao is the "solitary fu": every other fu organ pairs with a zang organ, and it alone has none. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the ring finger to behind the ear.
下焦如渎经文说下焦「别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉」：糟粕下到大肠，水液渗进膀胱。它的样子像渎，一条往下排的水沟。
Like a drainage ditchThe classics describe the lower jiao through the large intestine and the bladder: waste moves down into the large intestine, and fluids seep into the bladder. It works like a drainage ditch, carrying things down and out.
和现代解剖对照大致是下腹和盆腔。肾的位置在腰部，比肚脐还高，后世却把它归在下焦：三焦是按功能分的，不是按高度分的。
Next to modern anatomyRoughly the lower belly and the pelvis. The kidneys sit at the waist, higher than the navel, yet later tradition places them in the lower jiao: the Sanjiao is divided by function, not by height.
和丹田、胯根的关系同一片身体，三张图：在穴位图上是气海、关元，在内丹图上是丹田，在三焦图上是下焦。胯根在它的两侧，是腿接进来的地方。
The dantian and the kua rootOne stretch of body on three maps: Qihai and Guanyuan on the acupoint map, the dantian on the inner-alchemy map, the lower jiao on the Sanjiao map. The kua root lies on either side, where the legs join it.
怎么放注意力用渎的词：通不通、顺不顺、往不往下走。不说「下焦饱满」。日志里的「排便」那一项，就记在这里。
How to rest your attention hereUse the words of a ditch: does it flow, is it smooth, does it move downward. Never call the lower jiao full. The bowel field in the log belongs here.

经络Channel

三焦经 Sanjiao channel

从无名指尖起，经手背（液门），沿手臂外侧往上，到肩、耳后、眉梢。

经典原文Source

下焦管往下排：
The lower jiao carries things down and out:
下焦者，别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：
The other map, the Sanjiao. The lower jiao works like a drainage ditch; it is about flow, not gathering:
上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦是通水道的官：
The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:
三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。
《素问·灵兰秘典论》
三焦和膀胱，在体表对应腠理毫毛：
The Sanjiao and bladder show on the surface, in the skin and fine hair:
肾合三焦膀胱，三焦膀胱者，腠理毫毛其应。
《灵枢·本藏》
