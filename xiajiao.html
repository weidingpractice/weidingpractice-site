这是什么What it is

三焦是什么 《灵枢·本输》叫三焦「孤之腑」。五脏只有五个，六腑却有六个，多出来的那一个就是三焦；后世把心包配给它，凑成表里，但心包不算一个独立的脏。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：一条从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角的线。What the Sanjiao is The Lingshu calls the Sanjiao the "solitary fu". There are five zang organs but six fu, and the Sanjiao is the sixth, the one left over; later tradition paired it with the pericardium, which is not a full zang organ in its own right. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.

下焦如渎 经文说下焦「别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉」：糟粕下到大肠，水液渗进膀胱。它的样子像渎，一条往下排的水沟。《难经》给它定了性：「主出而不内」。上焦出的是气，中焦出的是营气，下焦只管往外排。Like a drainage ditch The classics describe the lower jiao through the large intestine and the bladder: waste moves down into the large intestine, and fluids seep into the bladder. It works like a drainage ditch, carrying things down and out. The Classic of Difficulties sums it up: it "lets out and never takes in". The upper jiao gives qi, the middle jiao the nutritive qi, and the lower jiao only lets go.

和现代解剖对照 大致是下腹和盆腔。上界在肚脐，只是体表标志：注家讲「别回肠」，说糟粕的分别发生在「脐上一寸水分穴处」，功能上是连过去的。下界是骨盆底，躯干的另一道肌性隔板，但它封住的是出口，不是把体腔分成两半：膈是分，盆底是封。肾的位置在腰部，比肚脐还高，后世却把它归在下焦：三焦是按功能分的，不是按高度分的。Next to modern anatomy Roughly the lower belly and the pelvis. Its upper edge, the navel, is only a surface landmark: a commentary places the sorting of waste one cun above the navel, so the function runs across it. Its lower edge is the pelvic floor, the trunk's other muscular sheet; but it seals the outlet rather than dividing the cavity in two. The diaphragm divides; the pelvic floor seals. The kidneys sit at the waist, higher than the navel, yet later tradition places them in the lower jiao: the Sanjiao is divided by function, not by height.

和丹田、胯根的关系 同一片身体，三张图：在穴位图上是气海、关元，在内丹图上是丹田，在三焦图上是下焦。胯根在它的两侧，是腿接进来的地方。The dantian and the kua root One stretch of body on three maps: Qihai and Guanyuan on the acupoint map, the dantian on the inner-alchemy map, the lower jiao on the Sanjiao map. The kua root lies on either side, where the legs join it.

怎么放注意力 用渎的词：通不通、顺不顺、往不往下走。自测一句：能不能排。桥式同时落在两张图上：抬髋时开上焦（胸），也动下焦（腹股沟），注意腹股沟是打开的还是缩着的。不说「下焦饱满」。日志里的「排便」那一项，就记在这里。How to rest your attention here Use the words of a ditch: does it flow, is it smooth, does it move downward. One question to test it: does it empty? Bridge sits on two maps at once: lifting the hips opens the upper jiao (the chest) and works the lower jiao (the groin); notice whether the groin opens or draws in. Never call the lower jiao full. The bowel field in the log belongs here.

原文Classical source

下焦管往下排：The lower jiao carries things down and out:

《灵枢·营卫生会》下焦者，别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉。连着 · Linked to：下焦 Xiajiao Area

注家说：下焦只出不进，一去不回。我把它当作一个提醒，留意排得顺不顺：A commentary, not the text itself: the lower jiao only lets out and never takes in; what leaves does not return. I take it as a reminder to notice whether things move freely:

「下焦如渎」旧注 注 · commentary渎者，水所注泄。言下焦主出而不纳，逝而不返，故曰下焦如渎也。连着 · Linked to：下焦 Xiajiao Area

《难经》的说法：下焦「主出而不内」。后一句「其府在气街」，气街有两种解法，其中一种就在腹股沟、胯根这一带：The Classic of Difficulties: the lower jiao "lets out and never takes in". The next line places the Sanjiao's seat at the qi street, which can be read two ways; one of them lies in the groin, where the kua root is:

《难经·三十一难》下焦者，当膀胱上口，主分别清浊，主出而不内，以传道也。其治在脐下一寸。故名曰三焦，其府在气街。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：「气街」有二义：一指《灵枢·卫气》「四街」的通道（胸、腹、头、胫各有气街）；一指气冲穴，在毛际两旁，也就是腹股沟、胯根这一带。而且「其府在气街」紧接「故名曰三焦」，说的很可能是三焦整体，不专指下焦。连着 · Linked to：下焦 Xiajiao Area · 胯根 Kua root Area

三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：Three images of the Sanjiao: mist, steeping, and a drainage ditch:

《灵枢·营卫生会》上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：三焦讲的是过程：散、泡、排。和丹田「田、鼎」的容器语汇不同。连着 · Linked to：丹田 Dantian Area · 上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

三焦是通水道的官：The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:

《素问·灵兰秘典论》三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

「孤之腑」：三焦没有配对的脏：The "solitary fu": the Sanjiao has no zang organ to pair with:

《灵枢·本输》三焦者，中渎之腑也，水道出焉，属膀胱，是孤之腑也。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

三焦的气一直到肌肉和皮肤。读到这句，我想到的是：这正是体式碰得到的地方：The qi of the Sanjiao reaches the muscles and skin. Reading this, I think of where practice can meet it:

《灵枢·五癃津液别》三焦出气，以温肌肉，充皮肤，为其津。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

这一条讲三焦整体，不是哪一焦：三焦和膀胱，在体表对应腠理毫毛：This line is about the Sanjiao as a whole, not any one jiao: the Sanjiao and bladder show on the surface, in the skin and fine hair:

《灵枢·本藏》肾合三焦膀胱，三焦膀胱者，腠理毫毛其应。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area
