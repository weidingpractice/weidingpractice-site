下焦Xiajiao · Area · Lower jiao

用手怎么找How to find it

手掌放在肚脐以下，一直到耻骨。这一片，连同盆腔，是下焦。
Rest your palm below your navel, down to the pubic bone. This area, with the pelvis behind it, is the lower jiao.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。 You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

三焦是什么《灵枢·本输》叫三焦「孤之腑」。五脏只有五个，六腑却有六个，多出来的那一个就是三焦；后世把心包配给它，凑成表里，但心包不算一个独立的脏。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：一条从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角的线。
What the Sanjiao isThe Lingshu calls the Sanjiao the "solitary fu". There are five zang organs but six fu, and the Sanjiao is the sixth, the one left over; later tradition paired it with the pericardium, which is not a full zang organ in its own right. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.
下焦如渎经文说下焦「别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉」：糟粕下到大肠，水液渗进膀胱。它的样子像渎，一条往下排的水沟。《难经》给它定了性：「主出而不内」。上焦出的是气，中焦出的是营气，下焦只管往外排。
Like a drainage ditchThe classics describe the lower jiao through the large intestine and the bladder: waste moves down into the large intestine, and fluids seep into the bladder. It works like a drainage ditch, carrying things down and out. The Classic of Difficulties sums it up: it "lets out and never takes in". The upper jiao gives qi, the middle jiao the nutritive qi, and the lower jiao only lets go.
和现代解剖对照大致是下腹和盆腔。上界在肚脐，只是体表标志：注家讲「别回肠」，说糟粕的分别发生在「脐上一寸水分穴处」，功能上是连过去的。下界是骨盆底，躯干的另一道肌性隔板，但它封住的是出口，不是把体腔分成两半：膈是分，盆底是封。肾的位置在腰部，比肚脐还高，后世却把它归在下焦：三焦是按功能分的，不是按高度分的。
Next to modern anatomyRoughly the lower belly and the pelvis. Its upper edge, the navel, is only a surface landmark: a commentary places the sorting of waste one cun above the navel, so the function runs across it. Its lower edge is the pelvic floor, the trunk's other muscular sheet; but it seals the outlet rather than dividing the cavity in two. The diaphragm divides; the pelvic floor seals. The kidneys sit at the waist, higher than the navel, yet later tradition places them in the lower jiao: the Sanjiao is divided by function, not by height.
和丹田、胯根的关系同一片身体，三张图：在穴位图上是气海、关元，在内丹图上是丹田，在三焦图上是下焦。胯根在它的两侧，是腿接进来的地方。
The dantian and the kua rootOne stretch of body on three maps: Qihai and Guanyuan on the acupoint map, the dantian on the inner-alchemy map, the lower jiao on the Sanjiao map. The kua root lies on either side, where the legs join it.
怎么放注意力用渎的词：通不通、顺不顺、往不往下走。自测一句：能不能排。桥式同时落在两张图上：抬髋时开上焦（胸），也动下焦（腹股沟），注意腹股沟是打开的还是缩着的。不说「下焦饱满」。日志里的「排便」那一项，就记在这里。
How to rest your attention hereUse the words of a ditch: does it flow, is it smooth, does it move downward. One question to test it: does it empty? Bridge sits on two maps at once: lifting the hips opens the upper jiao (the chest) and works the lower jiao (the groin); notice whether the groin opens or draws in. Never call the lower jiao full. The bowel field in the log belongs here.
没有采用的「通三焦」「利水道」「调气机」这类功效说法，经文没有对应；三焦经病候里的「汗出」，属于三焦经，留给三焦经那一条。
Left out on purposeClaims that practice "unblocks the Sanjiao", "frees the waterways" or "regulates the qi" have no match in the classics. "Sweating" in the Sanjiao channel's disorders belongs to the channel, and waits for its own entry.

经络Channel

三焦（六腑之一） The Sanjiao, one of the six fu

三焦是腑，不是经络。它的经络是三焦经，另作一条：从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角。

The Sanjiao is a fu organ, not a channel. Its channel is the Sanjiao channel, a separate line: from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.

经典原文Source

下焦管往下排：
The lower jiao carries things down and out:
下焦者，别回肠，注于膀胱而渗入焉。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·营卫生会》
注家说：下焦只出不进，一去不回。这就是自测「能不能排」：
A commentary, not the text itself: the lower jiao only lets out and never takes in; what leaves does not return. This is the self-test "does it empty?":
渎者，水所注泄。言下焦主出而不纳，逝而不返，故曰下焦如渎也。
医经 Medical classics · 「下焦如渎」旧注 · 注 commentary
《难经》的说法：下焦「主出而不内」；三焦的府在气街，也就是腹股沟、胯根这一带：
The Classic of Difficulties: the lower jiao "lets out and never takes in", and the Sanjiao gathers at the qi street, in the groin, where the kua root lies:
下焦者，当膀胱上口，主分别清浊，主出而不内，以传道也。其治在脐下一寸。故名曰三焦，其府在气街。
医经 Medical classics · 《难经·三十一难》
三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：
The other map, the Sanjiao. The lower jiao works like a drainage ditch; it is about flow, not gathering:
上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦是通水道的官：
The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:
三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。
医经 Medical classics · 《素问·灵兰秘典论》
「孤之腑」：三焦没有配对的脏：
The "solitary fu": the Sanjiao has no zang organ to pair with:
三焦者，中渎之腑也，水道出焉，属膀胱，是孤之腑也。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·本输》
三焦的气一直到肌肉和皮肤，这是体式够得到的地方：
The qi of the Sanjiao reaches the muscles and skin, where practice can meet it:
三焦出气，以温肌肉，充皮肤，为其津。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·五癃津液别》
这一条讲三焦整体，不是哪一焦：三焦和膀胱，在体表对应腠理毫毛：
This line is about the Sanjiao as a whole, not any one jiao: the Sanjiao and bladder show on the surface, in the skin and fine hair:
肾合三焦膀胱，三焦膀胱者，腠理毫毛其应。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·本藏》
