标准定位Standard location

在手指，食指末节桡侧，指甲根角侧上方0.1寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.2.1 条，第 8 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.2.1, p. 8 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the index finger, radial to the distal phalanx, 0.1 F-cun proximal-lateral to the radial corner of the index fingernail, at the intersection of the vertical line of the radial border of the fingernail and the horizontal line of the base of the index fingernail.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：食指桡侧指甲根角侧上方（即沿角平分线方向）0.1寸。相当于沿爪甲桡侧画一直线与爪甲基底缘水平线交点处取穴。The standard's own note: 0.1 cun from the radial corner of the index fingernail (along the line bisecting the corner). That is, where a line along the radial edge of the nail meets a line along the base of the nail.

用手怎么找
食指伸直，指甲朝上。看指甲靠大拇指那一边、靠近手指根的那个角。同一只手的拇指指甲尖，放在这个角外面紧挨着的地方，不压在指甲上。Straighten your index finger, nail up. Look at the corner of the nail on the thumb side, nearest the base of the finger. Put the tip of the thumbnail of the same hand just outside that corner, right beside it, not on the nail.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

大肠经Large Intestine channel

从食指指尖（商阳）起，沿手臂外侧前缘往上，经肩，过脖子，到鼻翼旁。

From the tip of the index finger (Shangyang), up the front edge of the outer arm, over the shoulder and neck, to beside the nostril.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
