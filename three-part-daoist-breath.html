三段道家呼吸

Three-part Daoist Breath✓ 有体会 · felt0 个穴位 · 0 条经络
一吸一呼，带着手臂、头和背一起动；做 2 分钟或 20 分钟都可以。Each breath in and out moves the arms, head and back together; it works for two minutes or twenty.
口令 · Cue

怎么做Practice note

来源这是 Austin Ince 在阴瑜伽教师培训里教的一段热身小串联。这个名字在阴瑜伽老师之间通用，最早由谁编排，还没查到。下面是我用自己的话写的步骤。SourceA short warm-up sequence Austin Ince teaches in his Yin teacher training. The name is shared among Yin teachers; who first put it together, I have not yet found. The steps below are in my own words.

坐法跪坐在脚跟上（金刚坐 Vajrasana）。也可以盘腿坐、坐在椅子上，或者在脚趾蹲（toe squat）里做。How to sitKneel and sit on your heels (Vajrasana). You can also sit cross-legged, sit on a chair, or do it in a toe squat.

1 呼双手放回身体两侧。1 OutLet the hands rest by your sides.

2 吸头向后，背微微后弯，双臂举过头顶。2 InTake the head back, arch the back a little, and lift the arms overhead.

3 呼下巴收向胸口，屈肘把双手带到头后，紧接着把双手向两侧推开。两个动作在同一口呼气里完成。3 OutChin to chest, bend the elbows to bring the hands behind the head, then straight away push them out to the sides. Both moves happen on the same out-breath.

4 吸头向后，屈肘让指尖碰到肩膀，胸口上提，让肺吸满。4 InTake the head back, bend the elbows so the fingertips touch the shoulders, lift the chest and fill the lungs.

5 呼下巴收向胸口，掌心向前推；双手往前走的时候，背拱圆。5 OutChin to chest, push the palms forward; as the hands travel forward, round the back.

6 吸头向后，双臂向上伸过头顶，背后弯。6 InTake the head back, reach the arms overhead, and arch the back.

7 呼下巴落下，向前折叠进婴儿式，双臂放在身体两侧的地上。7 OutBring the chin down and fold forward into Child's Pose, arms on the floor by your sides.

再吸坐起来，在同一口吸气里直接开始下一轮。In againCome up to sitting and begin the next round on the same in-breath.

几轮重复 3 到 5 轮，每轮都在婴儿式里结束。最后一次婴儿式，双臂向前伸直，像一次深深的拜伏，可以从这里接别的体式。How manyRepeat three to five rounds, ending each in Child's Pose. In the last one, stretch the arms forward like a deep bow; you can flow on into other postures from there.

什么时候用热身，单独练，收尾，冥想之前都可以。When to use itAs a warm-up, as a practice on its own, to close, or before meditation.

我的体会有一天我连下犬式都做不下去，身体一做就紧张。做了这个小串联，神经系统很快就安静下来。What I noticedOne day even Downward Dog felt like too much, and my body tensed at the thought. After this short sequence, my nervous system settled quickly.

一个体式往往连着好几个穴位、好几条经络。下面实线标记的是练习里真的感觉到的；虚线标「可能」的，是按位置推测、还没在垫子上验证的。原文写到篇名，可以追溯。
