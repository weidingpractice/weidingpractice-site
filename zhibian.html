秩邊

秩边 Zhibian

BL54 · Order's Edge · 膀胱经 Bladder channel

标准定位Standard location

在臀部，横平第4骶后孔，骶正中嵴旁开3寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.7.54 条，第 19 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.7.54, p. 19 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the buttock region, at the same level as the fourth posterior sacral foramen, 3 B-cun lateral to the median sacral crest.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：本穴位于骶管裂孔旁开３寸，横平白环俞（BL30）。The standard's own note: This point is 3 cun lateral to the sacral hiatus, level with Baihuanshu (BL30).

用手怎么找
站着，右手像插进裤子后口袋那样，手掌贴在屁股上，手指朝下。中指尖和尾骨一样高，不要更低。手掌盖住的这一片，秩边就在里面。Stand and put your right hand as if into the back pocket of your trousers: palm on the buttock, fingers pointing down. Keep the tip of your middle finger level with your tailbone, no lower. Zhibian lies in the area under your palm.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

膀胱经Bladder channel

从内眼角起，上头顶，沿脊柱两侧往下，经骶骨（八髎），沿腿后面到小脚趾外侧。

From the inner corner of the eye, over the crown, down both sides of the spine, through the sacrum (Baliao), down the back of the leg to the outer edge of the little toe.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
