怎么做Practice note

2026 年 9 月阴瑜伽教师培训，每次上课前有 10 分钟唱诵。我发现，只要把中指放在劳宫，我的声音立马变得柔和、轻，非常奇妙。During the Yin teacher training in September 2026, each class opened with ten minutes of chanting. I found that once I rested my middle finger on Laogong, my voice turned soft and light straight away. It was remarkable.
