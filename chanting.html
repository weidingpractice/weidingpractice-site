唱诵Chanting

中指放在劳宫，声音立刻变得柔和、轻。Rest the middle finger on Laogong, and the voice turns soft and light at once.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

劳宫 Laogong
PC8
有体会 · felt

轻轻握拳，中指尖碰到掌心的地方。

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

怎么做How I practise it

2026 年 9 月阴瑜伽教师培训，每次上课前有 10 分钟唱诵。我发现，只要把中指放在劳宫，我的声音立马变得柔和、轻，非常奇妙。
During the Yin teacher training in September 2026, each class opened with ten minutes of chanting. I found that once I rested my middle finger on Laogong, my voice turned soft and light straight away. It was remarkable.
