Vṛkṣāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →

脚心是水，大腿内侧是土，肩窝是金。The sole is water, the inner thigh is earth, the shoulder hollow is metal.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

涌
涌泉 Yongquan
KI1
有体会 · felt

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

血
血海 Xuehai
SP10
有体会 · felt

坐着弯膝。右手掌盖住左膝盖骨，拇指斜向大腿内侧，拇指尖落下的地方。

Seated, knee bent: cup your right palm over your left kneecap; the tip of your thumb points to it on the inner thigh.

LU2
有体会 · felt

手叉腰，锁骨外端下面会出现一个窝。

Hands on hips: a hollow appears just below the outer end of the collarbone.
