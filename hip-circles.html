把觉知放在两个半球的当中，很深的那个点。Rest your attention deep in the centre of each half of the buttocks.
口令 · Cue

怎么做Practice note

先左右，再画圈。一般先左右摆，pathway 是先左后右；走顺了以后，自然而然就会去画圈。Side to side, then circles.Start by swaying left and right; once the path runs smoothly, you begin to draw circles without trying.

觉知放在哪里。画圈的时候，可以把觉知放在耻骨（pubic bone）。耻骨在正中，画正中，连着它的两个半球自然画得更顺。也可以把觉知放在两个半球的当中那两个点：耻骨是它们中间的那个点，两个半球是从它延伸出去的。Where to rest your attention.As you circle, you can rest your attention on the pubic bone. It sits at the centre, so when you circle from the centre, the two halves connected to it follow more smoothly. Or rest it on the two points at the centre of each half: the pubic bone is the point between them, and the two halves extend out from it.

是一个球，不是一个圆。人是三维的结构。你绕的时候，是一个圆球，不是平面上的圆形；是 3D，不是 2D。所以觉知放在球的当中：两个半球的当中，很深的一个点。不是臀部外侧那块骨头后面的窝，是在内侧。A sphere, not a circle.The body is three-dimensional. As you circle, you trace a sphere, not a flat circle: 3D, not 2D. So rest your attention at the centre of the sphere, the deep point in the middle of each half of the buttocks. Not the hollow behind the bony point on the outside of the hip; it lies further in.

两个点，更能激起腰椎的觉知。我个人的感觉是：把觉知放在两个半球当中的那两个点，更能激起腰椎的觉知。这完全就是 alignment 了。Two points wake up the lumbar spine.My own feeling: resting attention on the two points at the centre of each half wakes up awareness of the lumbar spine more. That is alignment itself.

这是一个意象。更多的其实是个意象，不一定要对到哪个穴位。跟人说"两个半球的当中"，他会明白。It's an image.It works more as an image than as a point. Tell someone "the centre of each half" and they understand.

肠胃也动了。绕臀时感觉最强烈的就是两个半球当中那个很深的点，同时有肠胃的蠕动。The gut moves too.During the circles, the deep point in the middle of each half feels strongest, and the gut starts to move.

这个体式的觉知是一个意象，不是一个穴位。下面两个穴位只是位置在这附近，按位置推测，标「可能」。

一个体式往往连着好几个穴位、好几条经络。下面实线标记的是练习里真的感觉到的；虚线标「可能」的，是按位置推测、还没在垫子上验证的。原文写到篇名，可以追溯。

胞肓

胞肓 Baohuang

BL53 · Bladder Vitals · 膀胱经
○ 可能，待验证 · possible, to test
用手怎么找
站着。先找腰窝：后腰最下面，脊柱两边各有一个小凹窝。右手放到背后，手指朝下，手掌贴在屁股上半部、靠外边。手往上挪，让手腕这一头和腰窝一样高。手掌最上面靠外的这一片，胞肓就在里面。Stand. First find the dimples: at the very bottom of your lower back there is a small hollow on each side of the spine. Put your right hand behind you, fingers pointing down, palm on the upper, outer part of the buttock. Move the hand up until the wrist end is level with the dimples. Baohuang lies in the area at the top outer corner of your palm.

秩邊

秩边 Zhibian

BL54 · Order's Edge · 膀胱经
○ 可能，待验证 · possible, to test
用手怎么找
站着，右手像插进裤子后口袋那样，手掌贴在屁股上，手指朝下。中指尖和尾骨一样高，不要更低。手掌盖住的这一片，秩边就在里面。Stand and put your right hand as if into the back pocket of your trousers: palm on the buttock, fingers pointing down. Keep the tip of your middle finger level with your tailbone, no lower. Zhibian lies in the area under your palm.
