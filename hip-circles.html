怎么做How I practise it

先左右，再画圈。一般先左右摆，pathway 是先左后右；走顺了以后，自然而然就会去画圈。
Side to side, then circles.Start by swaying left and right; once the path runs smoothly, you begin to draw circles without trying.
觉知放在哪里。画圈的时候，可以把觉知放在耻骨（pubic bone）。耻骨在正中，画正中，连着它的两个半球自然画得更顺。也可以把觉知放在两个半球的当中那两个点：耻骨是它们中间的那个点，两个半球是从它延伸出去的。
Where to rest your attention.As you circle, you can rest your attention on the pubic bone. It sits at the centre, so when you circle from the centre, the two halves connected to it follow more smoothly. Or rest it on the two points at the centre of each half: the pubic bone is the point between them, and the two halves extend out from it.
是一个球，不是一个圆。人是三维的结构。你绕的时候，是一个圆球，不是平面上的圆形；是 3D，不是 2D。所以觉知放在球的当中：两个半球的当中，很深的一个点。不是臀部外侧那块骨头后面的窝，是在内侧。
A sphere, not a circle.The body is three-dimensional. As you circle, you trace a sphere, not a flat circle: 3D, not 2D. So rest your attention at the centre of the sphere, the deep point in the middle of each half of the buttocks. Not the hollow behind the bony point on the outside of the hip; it lies further in.
两个点，更能激起腰椎的觉知。我个人的感觉是：把觉知放在两个半球当中的那两个点，更能激起腰椎的觉知。这完全就是 alignment 了。
Two points wake up the lumbar spine.My own feeling: resting attention on the two points at the centre of each half wakes up awareness of the lumbar spine more. That is alignment itself.
这是一个意象。更多的其实是个意象，不一定要对到哪个穴位。跟人说"两个半球的当中"，他会明白。
It's an image.It works more as an image than as a point. Tell someone "the centre of each half" and they understand.
肠胃也动了。绕臀时感觉最强烈的就是两个半球当中那个很深的点，同时有肠胃的蠕动。
The gut moves too.During the circles, the deep point in the middle of each half feels strongest, and the gut starts to move.
