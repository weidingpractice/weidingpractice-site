标准定位Standard location

在前胸部，锁骨下窝凹陷中，肩胛骨喙突内缘，前正中线旁开6寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.1.2 条，第 8 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.1.2, p. 8 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the anterior thoracic region, in the depression of the infraclavicular fossa, medial to the coracoid process of the scapula, 6 B-cun lateral to the anterior median line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：横平内侧的气户（ST13）、俞府（KI27）、璇玑（CV21），4穴略呈一弧形分布，其弧度与锁骨下缘弧度相应。The standard's own note: Level with Qihu (ST13), Shufu (KI27) and Xuanji (CV21) on the inside; the four points lie on a slight arc that follows the curve of the lower edge of the collarbone.

用手怎么找
手叉腰，锁骨外端下面会出现一个窝。Hands on hips: a hollow appears just below the outer end of the collarbone.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
