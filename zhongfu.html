中府

中府 Zhongfu

LU1 · Middle Palace · 肺经 Lung channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

在前胸部，横平第1肋间隙，锁骨下窝外侧，前正中线旁开6寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.1.1 条，第 8 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.1.1, p. 8 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the anterior thoracic region, at the same level as the first intercostal space, lateral to the infraclavicular fossa, 6 B-cun lateral to the anterior median line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：注 1：先确定云门（LU2），中府即在云门（LU2）下1寸。 注 2：横平内侧的库房（ST14）、彧中（KI26）、华盖（CV20），4穴略呈一弧形分布，其弧度与第1肋间隙弧度相应。The standard's own notes: First locate Yunmen (LU2); Zhongfu is 1 cun below Yunmen (LU2). Level with Kufang (ST14), Yuzhong (KI26) and Huagai (CV20) on the inside; the four points lie on a slight arc that follows the curve of the first intercostal space.

用手怎么找
右手中指，放在左边锁骨靠肩膀那一头的下面。往下一个大拇指宽，再往胸口里挪一点。那一片里最凹、最深的地方。Right middle finger under the outer end of the left collarbone, near the shoulder. Move down one thumb-width, then a little in towards the chest. The deepest, most hollow spot there.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

From the upper outer chest (Zhongfu, Yunmen), down the front edge of the inner arm, to the tip of the thumb.
