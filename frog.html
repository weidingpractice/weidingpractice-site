青蛙式Frog

额头或下巴落地，头可以转向一边，不挤压脖子。意放在血海和腹股沟的窝。Rest the forehead or chin down, or turn the head to one side so the neck is not squeezed. Rest your attention on Xuehai and the hollow of the groin.

形 · 2 跪 · 四足 · 膝或胫骨，加手或前臂Shape · 2 Kneeling & all fours · Knees or shins, with hands or forearms

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

血海 Xuehai
SP10
有体会 · felt

坐着弯膝。右手掌盖住左膝盖骨，拇指斜向大腿内侧，拇指尖落下的地方。

Seated, knee bent: cup your right palm over your left kneecap; the tip of your thumb points to it on the inner thigh.

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease. Rest your attention; don't press.

经典原文Sources

原文不翻译。每条前面一句英文说明它讲的是什么。 The classical texts stay in Chinese; the line above each says what it is about.

《灵枢》写的脾经走向：从大脚趾内侧，沿脚内侧、内踝前、胫骨后，上到膝和大腿内侧前缘，入腹：
The Spleen channel as the Lingshu traces it: from the inside of the big toe, along the inner foot, in front of the inner ankle, behind the shin, up the front edge of the inner knee and thigh, into the belly:
脾足太阴之脉，起于大指之端，循指内侧白肉际，过核骨后，上内踝前廉，上踹内，循胫骨后，交出厥阴之前，上膝股内前廉，入腹，属脾络胃。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·经脉》

怎么做How I practise it

我的体会（11-14）有一天，一低头就感觉头部胀，心里慌，做不了倒立。身体自己想做婴儿式，额头压着印堂左右轻轻晃，然后自动进了青蛙式。血海和腹股沟的窝最明显，脚内侧也有感觉，是一整条力线。头转向一边，不挤压脖子，停留 2 到 3 分钟，然后身体往前趴一点，很舒服。
What I noticed (14 Nov)One day, every time I lowered my head, my head felt full and pressured and I felt panicky inside; inversions were out. My body wanted Child's Pose: forehead on Yintang, rolling gently side to side, and then it moved into Frog on its own. Xuehai and the groin hollow were clearest; the inner foot too, one whole line. Head turned to one side so the neck was free, two to three minutes, then a little forward. It felt good.
三条线放在一起我感觉到的线：脚内侧 → 小腿内侧 → 血海 → 大腿内侧 → 腹股沟的窝。《灵枢》的脾经：从大脚趾内侧起，过内踝前，沿胫骨后上行，经膝和大腿内侧前缘，入腹。Myers 的深前线：足底深层、小腿深层、膝内侧、内收肌、骨盆。Austin 的讲义说青蛙式作用于脾经、肝经和肾经。三条线落在同一片区域，这是我身体里感受到的巧合。大腿内侧并排走着脾、肝、肾三条经，我感觉到的是哪一条，还要再分辨。
Three lines side by sideThe line I felt: inner foot, inner calf, Xuehai, inner thigh, the groin hollow. The Spleen channel in the Lingshu: from the inside of the big toe, in front of the inner ankle, up behind the shin, along the front edge of the inner knee and thigh, into the belly. Myers's Deep Front Line: deep sole, deep calf, inner knee, adductors, pelvis. Austin's manual says Frog works the Spleen, Liver and Kidney channels. The three lines meet in one region: a coincidence I felt in my own body. Three channels run side by side up the inner thigh; which one I felt, I still have to tell apart.

两个视角Two views of one line

经络视角

肝经 Liver channel

从大脚趾起，沿脚背、小腿和大腿内侧往上，绕过阴部，到小腹、胁肋，上连目系，到头顶。

From the big toe, up the top of the foot and the inside of the shin and thigh, around the genitals, through the lower belly and the ribs, connecting with the eye, to the crown.

经络视角

肾经 Kidney channel

从小脚趾下面斜走到脚心（涌泉），沿腿内侧往上，经腹部、胸口，止于锁骨下方。

From under the little toe, across to the sole (Yongquan), up the inside of the leg, through the belly and chest, ending below the collarbone.

筋膜视角 · Thomas Myers, Anatomy Trains（第 4 版，2020）

深前线 Deep Front Line

从足底深层起，经小腿深层后侧、膝内侧，沿大腿内收肌群上到骨盆，再经腰大肌、横膈膜往上，一直到颈前深层和颅底。

From the deep sole of the foot, up the deep back of the calf and the inner knee, along the adductors of the inner thigh to the pelvis, then on through the psoas and diaphragm to the deep front of the neck and the base of the skull.

和腿内侧的阴经走在同一片区域：脚内侧、小腿内侧、膝内侧、大腿内收肌、腹股沟。Wei 在青蛙式里感觉到的那条线，正好落在这里。这是一个巧合，不是证明。

It runs through the same region as the yin channels of the inner leg: inner foot, inner calf, inner knee, the adductors, the groin. The line Wei felt in Frog falls right here. A coincidence, not a proof.

和别人对照Compared with

现代老师的说法，只在和我的体会正好一致或正好不同时放进来。 Modern teachers appear only where my own practice agrees or differs.

Austin 的讲义列了三条经：脾、肝、肾。我在身体里最先感觉到的两个点，血海和腹股沟的窝，都在脾经上。
Austin's manual names three channels: Spleen, Liver and Kidney. The two points I felt first, Xuehai and the groin hollow, both sit on the Spleen channel.
青蛙式里，腿内侧受到的压力作用于脾经、肝经和肾经。

The inner leg pressure works the Spleen EARTH, Liver WOOD, and Kidney WATER.

Austin Ince，阴瑜伽教师培训讲义（2026），「体式、经络与五行」一节
