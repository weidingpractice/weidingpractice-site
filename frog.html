青蛙式

Frog✓ 有体会 · felt2 个穴位 · 3 条经络
额头或下巴落地，头可以转向一边，不挤压脖子。意放在膝盖内侧和腹股沟的窝。Rest the forehead or chin down, or turn the head to one side so the neck is not squeezed. Rest your attention on the inner knee and the hollow of the groin.
口令 · Cue

往阴，往阳Toward yin, toward yang

更阴 More yin

  • 头转向一边，不挤压脖子。Turn the head to one side so the neck is not squeezed.
  • 停留两三分钟以后，身体往前趴一点。After two or three minutes, let the body sink a little further forward.

再阴，膝盖收回来，就回到婴儿式。我那天正是从婴儿式自己进的青蛙式。 婴儿式 →More yin: draw the knees in and you are back in Child's Pose. That day, Frog grew out of Child's Pose on its own.

本式 The pose

前臂撑地，膝盖打开，额头或下巴落地。意放在膝盖内侧和腹股沟的窝。Forearms down, knees wide, forehead or chin to the floor. Attention on the inner knee and the groin hollow.

更阳 More yang

  • 用力六七秒，膝盖往里压、前臂往下压，再完全放松六七秒，来回几次（PNF）。Thanks to Austin Ince.Press for six or seven seconds, knees in and forearms down, then let go completely for six or seven seconds; repeat a few times (PNF). Thanks to Austin Ince.

再阳，膝离地、手撑直，就走到下犬式。 下犬式 →More yang: knees lift, arms straighten, and you arrive in Downward Dog.

怎么做Practice note

我的体会（11-14）有一天，一低头就感觉头部胀，心里慌，做不了倒立。身体自己想做婴儿式，额头压着印堂左右轻轻晃，然后自动进了青蛙式。膝盖内侧和腹股沟的窝最明显，脚内侧也有感觉，是一整条力线。头转向一边，不挤压脖子，停留 2 到 3 分钟，然后身体往前趴一点，很舒服。What I noticed (14 Nov)One day, every time I lowered my head, my head felt full and pressured and I felt panicky inside; inversions were out. My body wanted Child's Pose: forehead on Yintang, rolling gently side to side, and then it moved into Frog on its own. The inner knee and the groin hollow were clearest; the inner foot too, one whole line. Head turned to one side so the neck was free, two to three minutes, then a little forward. It felt good.

三条线放在一起我感觉到的线：脚内侧 → 小腿内侧 → 膝盖内侧 → 大腿内侧 → 腹股沟的窝。《灵枢》的脾经：从大脚趾内侧起，过内踝前，沿胫骨后上行，经膝和大腿内侧前缘，入腹。Myers 的深前线：足底深层、小腿深层、膝内侧、内收肌、骨盆。三条线落在同一片区域，这是我身体里感受到的巧合。大腿内侧并排走着脾、肝、肾三条经，我感觉到的是哪一条，还要再分辨。Three lines side by sideThe line I felt: inner foot, inner calf, inner knee, inner thigh, the groin hollow. The Spleen channel in the Lingshu: from the inside of the big toe, in front of the inner ankle, up behind the shin, along the front edge of the inner knee and thigh, into the belly. Myers's Deep Front Line: deep sole, deep calf, inner knee, adductors, pelvis. The three lines meet in one region: a coincidence I felt in my own body. Three channels run side by side up the inner thigh; which one I felt, I still have to tell apart.

胯根

胯根 Kua root

Area · Kua root · inner groin · 脾经
✓ 在这个体式里感觉到 · felt in this pose
用手怎么找
坐着，一条腿屈膝外放。手指放在大腿根和小腹相接的折痕下面一点，往里、往深处，能摸到一个凹下去的窝。先在折痕上找到股动脉的搏动：搏动外侧是冲门，内侧那块软的是耻骨肌；顺着凹槽往膝盖方向，是箕门。整片就是胯根。Sit with one knee bent and dropped out to the side. Put your fingers just below the crease where the thigh meets the belly, and feel inward and deep for a hollow. First find the pulse of the femoral artery in the crease: Chongmen lies just outside it, and the soft muscle just inside it is the pectineus; follow the groove down towards the knee and you reach Jimen. The whole area is the kua root.

脾经Spleen channel
《灵枢·骨度》季胁以下至髀枢，长六寸，髀枢以下至膝中，长一尺九寸。髀枢：大腿和骨盆相接、转动的地方。
《素问·骨空论》侠髋为机。机：枢机、转动之处。
《难经·三十一难》下焦者，当膀胱上口，主分别清浊，主出而不内，以传道也。其治在脐下一寸。故名曰三焦，其府在气街。「气街」有二义：一指《灵枢·卫气》「四街」的通道（胸、腹、头、胫各有气街）；一指气冲穴，在毛际两旁，也就是腹股沟、胯根这一带。而且「其府在气街」紧接「故名曰三焦」，说的很可能是三焦整体，不专指下焦。
肝经循行（《中华传世藏书·子部·中医》所收注文） 注 · commentary 出处待补循股内之阴包、五里、阴廉，上会于足太阴之冲门、府舍，入阴毛中之急脉。转引，待对原书。说明脾经和肝经在这一片会合。

相关经络Channels

脾经Spleen channel

从大脚趾内侧起，沿腿内侧往上，经腹部，到侧胸（大包）。

From the inside of the big toe, up the inside of the leg, through the belly, to the side of the chest (Dabao).
《灵枢·经脉》脾之大络，名曰大包，出渊腋下三寸，布胸胁。

肝经Liver channel

从大脚趾起，沿脚背、小腿和大腿内侧往上，绕过阴部，到小腹、胁肋，上连目系，到头顶。

From the big toe, up the top of the foot and the inside of the shin and thigh, around the genitals, through the lower belly and the ribs, connecting with the eye, to the crown.

肾经Kidney channel

从小脚趾下面斜走到脚心（涌泉），沿腿内侧往上，经腹部、胸口，止于锁骨下方。

From under the little toe, across to the sole (Yongquan), up the inside of the leg, through the belly and chest, ending below the collarbone.
《灵枢·经脉》肾足少阴之脉，起于小指之下，邪走足心。

深前线Deep Front Line

从足底深层起，经小腿深层后侧、膝内侧，沿大腿内收肌群上到骨盆，再经腰大肌、横膈膜往上，一直到颈前深层和颅底。

From the deep sole of the foot, up the deep back of the calf and the inner knee, along the adductors of the inner thigh to the pelvis, then on through the psoas and diaphragm to the deep front of the neck and the base of the skull.

和腿内侧的阴经走在同一片区域：脚内侧、小腿内侧、膝内侧、大腿内收肌、腹股沟。青蛙式里感觉到的那条线，正好落在这里。这是一个巧合，不是证明。It runs through the same region as the yin channels of the inner leg: inner foot, inner calf, inner knee, the adductors, the groin. The line felt in Frog falls right here. A coincidence, not a proof.
