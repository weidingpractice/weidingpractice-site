Acupoints on the face面部的穴位

The Chinese national standard for acupoint locations, GB/T 12346-2021, places 19 of the 362 channel points on the face. Here they are, grouped by where they sit: around the eyes, on the nose, around the mouth, on the cheek and jaw, and in front of the ear. For each: its Chinese name, pinyin and code, the channel it belongs to, and where it is, in our English translation of the standard, with the standard's own Chinese wording and clause number underneath.

国家标准 GB/T 12346-2021 把 362 个经穴中的 19 个定在面部。这里按位置分组：眼周、鼻、口唇、颊与下颌、耳前。每个穴位写中文名、拼音、代码、所属的经、在哪里（我们的英文翻译），下面附国标原文和条文号。

This page says where the points are. It does not say what they treat. You are welcome to use it: Use this freely. To cite: Source: Seeing Through One Body, https://weidingpractice.com/face-points.html

Around the eyes眼周

  • 睛明 Jingming · BL1

    Bladder channel (foot taiyang)

    On the face, in the hollow on the inner wall of the eye socket, just above and inside the inner corner of the eye.

    国标原文：在面部，目内眦内上方眶内侧壁凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.7.1 条）

  • 攒竹 Cuanzhu · BL2

    Bladder channel (foot taiyang)

    On the face, in the hollow at the inner end of the eyebrow, at the frontal notch.

    国标原文：在面部，眉头凹陷中，额切迹处。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.7.2 条）

  • 承泣 Chengqi · ST1

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, between the eyeball and the lower edge of the eye socket, directly below the pupil.

    国标原文：在面部，眼球与眶下缘之间，瞳孔直下。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.1 条）

  • 四白 Sibai · ST2

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, at the infraorbital foramen.

    国标原文：在面部，眶下孔处。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.2 条）

Nose鼻

  • 素髎 Suliao · GV25

    Governing vessel

    On the face, at the very centre of the tip of the nose.

    国标原文：在面部，鼻尖的正中央。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.13.26 条）

  • 迎香 Yingxiang · LI20

    Large Intestine channel (hand yangming)

    On the face, beside the midpoint of the outer edge of the side of the nose, in the nasolabial groove.

    国标原文：在面部，鼻翼外缘中点旁，鼻唇沟中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.2.20 条）

  • 巨髎 Juliao · ST3

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, level with the lower edge of the side of the nose, directly below the pupil.

    国标原文：在面部，横平鼻翼下缘，瞳孔直下。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.3 条）

Mouth and lips口唇

  • 口禾髎 Kouheliao · LI19

    Large Intestine channel (hand yangming)

    On the face, level with the point one third of the way down the philtrum, directly below the outer edge of the nostril.

    国标原文：在面部，横平人中沟上1/3与下2/3交点，鼻孔外缘直下。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.2.19 条）

  • 水沟 Shuigou · GV26

    Governing vessel

    On the face, on the philtrum, one third of the way down.

    国标原文：在面部，人中沟的上1/3与中1/3交点处。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.13.27 条）

  • 兑端 Duiduan · GV27

    Governing vessel

    On the face, at the midpoint of the tubercle of the upper lip.

    国标原文：在面部，上唇结节的中点。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.13.28 条）

  • 地仓 Dicang · ST4

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, 0.4 cun lateral to the corner of the mouth.

    国标原文：在面部，口角旁开0.4寸。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.4 条）

  • 承浆 Chengjiang · CV24

    Conception vessel

    On the face, in the hollow at the centre of the groove between the lower lip and the chin.

    国标原文：在面部，颏唇沟的正中凹陷处。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.14.24 条）

Cheek and jaw颊与下颌

  • 颧髎 Quanliao · SI18

    Small Intestine channel (hand taiyang)

    On the face, at the lower edge of the cheekbone, in the hollow directly below the outer corner of the eye.

    国标原文：在面部，颧骨下缘，目外眦直下凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.6.18 条）

  • 大迎 Daying · ST5

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, in front of the angle of the jaw, in the hollow at the front edge of the masseter's attachment, where the facial artery can be felt.

    国标原文：在面部，下颌角前方，咬肌附着部的前缘凹陷中，面动脉搏动处。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.5 条）

  • 颊车 Jiache · ST6

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, one finger-width in front of and above the angle of the jaw.

    国标原文：在面部，下颌角前上方一横指。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.6 条）

  • 下关 Xiaguan · ST7

    Stomach channel (foot yangming)

    On the face, in the hollow between the midpoint of the lower edge of the cheekbone arch and the notch of the jaw.

    国标原文：在面部，颧弓下缘中央与下颌切迹之间凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.3.7 条）

In front of the ear耳前

  • 听宫 Tinggong · SI19

    Small Intestine channel (hand taiyang)

    On the face, in the hollow between the centre of the tragus and the condyle of the jaw.

    国标原文：在面部，耳屏正中与下颌骨髁突之间的凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.6.19 条）

  • 耳门 Ermen · TE21

    Sanjiao channel (hand shaoyang)

    On the face, in the hollow between the notch above the tragus and the condyle of the jaw.

    国标原文：在面部，耳屏上切迹与下颌骨髁突之间的凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.10.21 条）

  • 听会 Tinghui · GB2

    Gallbladder channel (foot shaoyang)

    On the face, in the hollow between the intertragic notch and the condyle of the jaw.

    国标原文：在面部，耳屏间切迹与下颌骨髁突之间的凹陷中。（GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.11.2 条）

All 362 points are on the forest map: the forest. Words used here: cun is a body-relative unit of length used by the standard; see the glossary.
