用手怎么找
先找到肚脐。从肚脐正对着往后，到后腰脊柱的正中。用中指指肚摸，正对的地方有一个凹陷。Find your navel first. Go straight back from it to the middle of the spine in your lower back. Feel with the pad of your middle finger: right there is a small hollow.
