Skandasana

转臀下去，把骶骨这块后侧区域拉开。

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

胞
胞肓 Baohuang
BL53
可能 · possible

后腰下面那两个小腰窝的高度，往外量四指宽。

Level with the two small dimples at the base of your lower back, four finger-widths out to the side.

秩
秩边 Zhibian
BL54
可能 · possible

摸到骶骨最下端、尾骨开始的地方，从那个高度往外量四指宽。

Find the bottom of the sacrum, where the tailbone begins; from that level, four finger-widths out to the side.

怎么做How I practise it

转臀下去。Skandasana 是转臀下去，把骶骨这块后侧区域拉开。你只有转，才可以不卡着。
Turn the hips and lower.In Skandasana you turn the hips and lower, opening the back of the sacrum. Only when you turn do you stop getting stuck.

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
