劳宫捂眼Palming with Laogong

双手搓热，掌心劳宫轻轻盖在闭着的眼睛上，停一分钟，不压。Rub your palms warm and rest Laogong lightly over your closed eyes for a minute, without pressing.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

劳
劳宫 Laogong
PC8
有体会 · felt

轻轻握拳，中指尖碰到掌心的地方。

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

怎么做How I practise it

什么时候做。练完以后；也可以在下犬式、犁式这类头朝下的体式之后，或者看屏幕很久以后。
When.After practice; also after head-down poses such as Downward Dog and Plough, or after long screen time.
怎么做。手掌微微拱起，掌根落在颧骨上，掌心对着眼睛，不碰眼球。眼睛在掌心的暗处休息。
How.Cup the palms slightly, heels of the hands on the cheekbones, the hollow of each palm over an eye without touching the eyeball. Let the eyes rest in the dark.
试一试。劳宫能让唱诵的声音变柔和；看看它能不能让眼睛也柔和下来：眼睛后面的压力、太阳穴的紧，有没有松一点。没有变化也记下来。
Try it.Laogong softens the voice in chanting; see whether it softens the eyes too: the pressure behind them, the tightness at the temples. If nothing changes, write that down too.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。
