体式 · Pose

高弓步High Lunge

进入时守环跳，后腿守太溪。

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

环
环跳 Huantiao
GB30
有体会 · felt

侧躺，上面那条腿弯起来。摸到屁股外侧最凸的那块骨头，它后面最深的窝。

Lie on your side, top knee bent: the deepest hollow behind the bony point on the outside of your hip.

胯
胯根 Kuagen
区域 · Area
有体会 · felt

坐着，一条腿屈膝外放。手指放在大腿根和小腹相接的折痕下面一点，往里、往深处，能摸到一个凹下去的窝。先在折痕上找到股动脉的搏动：搏动外侧是冲门，内侧那块软的是耻骨肌；顺着凹槽往膝盖方向，是箕门。整片就是胯根。

Sit with one knee bent and dropped out to the side. Put your fingers just below the crease where the thigh meets the belly, and feel inward and deep for a hollow. First find the pulse of the femoral artery in the crease: Chongmen lies just outside it, and the soft muscle just inside it is the pectineus; follow the groove down towards the knee and you reach Jimen. The whole area is the kua root.

太
太溪 Taixi
KI3
有体会 · felt

脚腕内侧有一块凸起的骨头。从它往脚后跟方向摸，碰到那根粗粗的筋之前，中间有个小窝。

From the bump on the inside of your ankle, slide back towards the heel: the hollow before the thick cord.

涌
涌泉 Yongquan
KI1
有体会 · felt

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

怎么做How I practise it

用后腿的力。Austin 一直在强调 use your back leg, both of your back foot。觉知放在涌泉。涌泉一旦对上太溪，你就知道该怎么用后腿的力了，alignment 也有了。
Use the back leg.Austin keeps saying: use your back leg, both of your back foot. Rest your awareness on Yongquan. Once Yongquan lines up with Taixi, you know how to use the back leg, and the alignment arrives with it.
转换时用哪个点。从高弓步到战士三、到站立扭转，用环跳；从高弓步到战士二，是转臀，觉知放在臀部正中心最深的点。
Which point in which transition.From High Lunge to Warrior III, or to a standing twist, use Huantiao. From High Lunge to Warrior II you turn the hips, so rest your awareness at the deepest point in the centre of each buttock.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。
