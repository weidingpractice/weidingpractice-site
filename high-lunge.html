高弓步High Lunge

Aṣṭa Candrāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →

进入时守环跳，后腿守太溪。Going in, hold Huantiao; in the back leg, hold Taixi.

形 · 4 双脚 · 从高到低：站直 → 站桩 → 弓步 → 蹲Shape · 4 Two feet · High to low: standing, zhan zhuang, lunge, squat

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

环
环跳 Huantiao
GB30
有体会 · felt

侧躺，上面那条腿弯起来。摸到屁股外侧最凸的那块骨头，它后面最深的窝。

Lie on your side, top knee bent: the deepest hollow behind the bony point on the outside of your hip.

胯
胯根 Kua root
Area
有体会 · felt

大腿根前内侧、腹股沟折痕下面凹下去的窝。只放注意力，不用力按。

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease. Rest your attention; don't press.

太
太溪 Taixi
KI3
有体会 · felt

脚腕内侧有一块凸起的骨头。从它往脚后跟方向摸，碰到那根粗粗的筋之前，中间有个小窝。

From the bump on the inside of your ankle, slide back towards the heel: the hollow before the thick cord.

涌
涌泉 Yongquan
KI1
有体会 · felt

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

怎么做How I practise it

用后腿的力。Austin 一直在强调 use your back leg, balls of the back foot。觉知放在涌泉。涌泉一旦对上太溪，你就知道该怎么用后腿的力了，alignment 也有了。
Use the back leg.Austin keeps saying, "Use your back leg, the balls of the back foot." Rest your attention on Yongquan. Once Yongquan lines up with Taixi, you know how to use the back leg, and the alignment arrives with it.
转换时用哪个点。从高弓步到战士三、到站立扭转，用环跳；从高弓步到战士二，是转臀，觉知放在臀部正中心最深的点。
Which point in which transition.From High Lunge to Warrior III, or to a standing twist, use Huantiao. From High Lunge to Warrior II you turn the hips, so rest your attention at the deepest point in the centre of each buttock.
