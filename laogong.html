劳宫 Laogong

PC8 · Palace of Toil · 心包经 Pericardium channel

标准定位Standard location

在手掌，横平第 3 掌指关节近端，第 2、3 掌骨之间偏于第 3 掌骨。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

On the palm of the hand, in the depression between the second and third metacarpal bones, proximal to the metacarpophalangeal joints.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

注：中国标准和 WHO 都把「第 3、4 掌骨之间偏于第 3 掌骨」列为另一种定位。标准原注：握拳屈指时，中指尖点到处。Note: Both the Chinese standard and WHO list an alternative location: between the third and fourth metacarpals, nearer the third. The Chinese standard adds: make a fist, and the point is where the tip of the middle finger lands.

用手怎么找
轻轻握拳，中指尖碰到掌心的地方。Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
