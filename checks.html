手部穴位的意象与名称：一份文献出处核查Images and names of the hand acupoints: a source check

《灵枢》为五输穴逐一写了位置，只有鱼际一处带取象说明；「列缺」作闪电解属文学语汇，未见古代医书据此释穴名。

The Ling Shu gives a location for each transport point, and only Yuji carries an image-based gloss; reading "Lieque" as lightning is literary usage, with no classical medical text applying it to the point name.

血压与「脾为生痰之源」：一份文献出处核查Blood pressure and "the spleen is the source of phlegm": a source check

中医典籍里没有「血压」；「脾为生痰之源」出自《本草纲目》半夏条，原本在讲半夏。

Classical texts have no concept of blood pressure; "the spleen is the source of phlegm" comes from the Bencao Gangmu entry on banxia.
