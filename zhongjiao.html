中焦Zhongjiao · 区域 · Area · Middle jiao

用手怎么找How to find it

手掌放在胸骨下端和肚脐之间，也就是上腹。这一片是中焦。
Rest your palm between the bottom of your breastbone and your navel, on the upper belly. This is the middle jiao.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。 You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

三焦是什么三焦是六腑里的「孤之腑」：别的腑都和一个脏配对，只有它没有。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：那是一条从无名指到耳后的线。
What the Sanjiao isThe Sanjiao is the "solitary fu": every other fu organ pairs with a zang organ, and it alone has none. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the ring finger to behind the ear.
中焦如沤经文说中焦「并胃中，出上焦之后」，在膈以下，脾胃在这里，管消化：「泌糟粕，蒸津液，化其精微」。它的样子像沤：泡着、慢慢化开。
Like soakingThe classics place the middle jiao with the stomach, just below the diaphragm. It digests: it separates the waste, steams the fluids and refines what is fine. It works like soaking: things steep and slowly break down.
和现代解剖对照大致是腹腔的上半部，不是整个腹腔。中焦和下焦之间没有隔膜，是一个连通的腔。
Next to modern anatomyRoughly the upper half of the abdomen, not the whole of it. No membrane separates the middle and lower jiao; the cavity is continuous.
怎么放注意力用沤的词：化不化、沉不沉、胀不胀。前屈和扭转时，注意上腹是软的、能让开，还是顶着。
How to rest your attention hereUse the words of soaking: is it breaking down, does it sink, is it bloated. In forward folds and twists, notice whether the upper belly is soft and gives way, or pushes back.

经络Channel

三焦经 Sanjiao channel

从无名指尖起，经手背（液门），沿手臂外侧往上，到肩、耳后、眉梢。

经典原文Source

中焦在膈下，管消化：
The middle jiao lies below the diaphragm and digests:
中焦亦并胃中，出上焦之后，此所受气者，泌糟粕，蒸津液，化其精微，上注于肺脉，乃化而为血。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：
The other map, the Sanjiao. The lower jiao works like a drainage ditch; it is about flow, not gathering:
上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦是通水道的官：
The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:
三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。
《素问·灵兰秘典论》
水谷走的路：
The road that food and drink travel:
三焦者，水谷之道路，气之所终始也。
《难经·三十一难》
