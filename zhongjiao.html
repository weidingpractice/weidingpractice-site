中焦Zhongjiao · Area · Middle jiao

用手怎么找How to find it

手掌放在胸骨下端和肚脐之间，也就是上腹。这一片是中焦。
Rest your palm between the bottom of your breastbone and your navel, on the upper belly. This is the middle jiao.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。 You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

三焦是什么《灵枢·本输》叫三焦「孤之腑」。五脏只有五个，六腑却有六个，多出来的那一个就是三焦；后世把心包配给它，凑成表里，但心包不算一个独立的脏。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：一条从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角的线。
What the Sanjiao isThe Lingshu calls the Sanjiao the "solitary fu". There are five zang organs but six fu, and the Sanjiao is the sixth, the one left over; later tradition paired it with the pericardium, which is not a full zang organ in its own right. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.
中焦如沤经文说中焦「并胃中，出上焦之后」，在膈以下，脾胃在这里，管消化：「泌糟粕，蒸津液，化其精微」，做出来的叫「营气」。它的样子像沤：泡着、慢慢化开。
Like soakingThe classics place the middle jiao with the stomach, just below the diaphragm. It digests: it separates the clear from the turbid, steams the fluids and refines what is fine, and what it makes is called the nutritive qi. It works like soaking: things steep and slowly break down.
和现代解剖对照大致是腹腔的上半部，不是整个腹腔。经文自己就这样划：中焦「泌糟粕」，糟粕往下进「回肠」，回肠归下焦。中焦和下焦之间没有隔膜，是一个连通的腔。
Next to modern anatomyRoughly the upper half of the abdomen, not the whole of it. The classics draw the line themselves: the middle jiao passes the waste down into the large intestine, which belongs to the lower jiao. No membrane separates the middle and lower jiao; the cavity is continuous.
怎么放注意力用沤的词：化不化、沉不沉、胀不胀。自测一句：能不能转。前屈和扭转时，注意上腹是软的、能让开，还是顶着。
How to rest your attention hereUse the words of soaking: is it breaking down, does it sink, is it bloated. One question to test it: does it turn? In forward folds and twists, notice whether the upper belly is soft and gives way, or pushes back.
没有采用的「通三焦」「利水道」「调气机」这类功效说法，经文没有对应；三焦经病候里的「汗出」，属于三焦经，留给三焦经那一条。蝴蝶式不放在这里：它动的是髋，上腹不受压，测不到「能不能转」。
Left out on purposeClaims that practice "unblocks the Sanjiao", "frees the waterways" or "regulates the qi" have no match in the classics. "Sweating" in the Sanjiao channel's disorders belongs to the channel, and waits for its own entry. Butterfly is left out here: it works the hips and doesn't press the upper belly, so it can't answer "does it turn?"

经络Channel

三焦（六腑之一） The Sanjiao, one of the six fu

三焦是腑，不是经络。它的经络是三焦经，另作一条：从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角。

经典原文Source

中焦在膈下，管消化；它做出来的，叫营气：
The middle jiao lies below the diaphragm and digests; what it makes is called the nutritive qi:
中焦亦并胃中，出上焦之后，此所受气者，泌糟粕，蒸津液，化其精微，上注于肺脉，乃化而为血，以奉生身，莫贵于此，故独得行于经隧，命曰营气。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：
The other map, the Sanjiao. The lower jiao works like a drainage ditch; it is about flow, not gathering:
上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。
《灵枢·营卫生会》
三焦是通水道的官：
The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:
三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。
《素问·灵兰秘典论》
「孤之腑」：三焦没有配对的脏：
The "solitary fu": the Sanjiao has no zang organ to pair with:
三焦者，中渎之腑也，水道出焉，属膀胱，是孤之腑也。
《灵枢·本输》
三焦的气一直到肌肉和皮肤，这是体式够得到的地方：
The qi of the Sanjiao reaches the muscles and skin, where practice can meet it:
三焦出气，以温肌肉，充皮肤，为其津。
《灵枢·五癃津液别》
这一条讲三焦整体，不是哪一焦：三焦和膀胱，在体表对应腠理毫毛：
This line is about the Sanjiao as a whole, not any one jiao: the Sanjiao and bladder show on the surface, in the skin and fine hair:
肾合三焦膀胱，三焦膀胱者，腠理毫毛其应。
《灵枢·本藏》
水谷走的路：
The road that food and drink travel:
三焦者，水谷之道路，气之所终始也。
《难经·三十一难》
