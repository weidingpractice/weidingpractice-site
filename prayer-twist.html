合掌扭转Prayer Twist

弓步里：Parivṛtta Añjaneyāsana / Parivṛtta Aṣṭa Candrāsana · 幻椅式里：Parivṛtta Utkaṭāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →

一只手的劳宫贴住另一只手的液门。不用转那么深，呼吸很好。Press the Laogong of one hand against the Yemen of the other. No need to twist so deep; the breath is good.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

中
中焦 Zhongjiao
Area
可能 · possible

手掌放在胸骨下端和肚脐之间，也就是上腹。这一片是中焦。

Rest your palm between the bottom of your breastbone and your navel, on the upper belly. This is the middle jiao.

劳
劳宫 Laogong
PC8
有体会 · felt

轻轻握拳，中指尖碰到掌心的地方。

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

液
液门 Yemen
TE2
有体会 · felt

手背朝上，无名指和小指之间的指缝，往手腕方向一点点的小窝。

Back of the hand: the small hollow just behind the web between ring and little fingers.
