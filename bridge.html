桥式Bridge

Setu Bandha Sarvangasana

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

胯
胯根 Kuagen
区域 · Area
有体会 · felt

坐着，一条腿屈膝外放。手指放在大腿根和小腹相接的折痕下面一点，往里、往深处，能摸到一个凹下去的窝。先在折痕上找到股动脉的搏动：搏动外侧是冲门，内侧那块软的是耻骨肌；顺着凹槽往膝盖方向，是箕门。整片就是胯根。

Sit with one knee bent and dropped out to the side. Put your fingers just below the crease where the thigh meets the belly, and feel inward and deep for a hollow. First find the pulse of the femoral artery in the crease: Chongmen lies just outside it, and the soft muscle just inside it is the pectineus; follow the groove down towards the knee and you reach Jimen. The whole area is the kua root.

丹
丹田 Dantian
区域 · Area
有体会 · felt

手掌横放在肚脐下面，掌心对着小腹。手掌盖住的这一片，就是下丹田。它不是一个点，是一片区域。

Lay your palm across your belly just below the navel. The area under your hand is the lower dantian. It is an area, not a point.

下
下焦 Xiajiao
区域 · Area
可能 · possible

手掌放在肚脐以下，一直到耻骨。这一片，连同盆腔，是下焦。

Rest your palm below your navel, down to the pubic bone. This area, with the pelvis behind it, is the lower jiao.

上
上焦 Shangjiao
区域 · Area
可能 · possible

手掌平放在胸骨上，两乳之间。手掌盖住的胸腔这一片，就是上焦。

Lay your palm flat on your breastbone, between the breasts. The chest under your hand is the upper jiao.

命
命门 Mingmen
GV4
可能 · possible

手放在后腰正中，和肚脐一样高的地方。

Hand on the centre of your lower back, level with your navel.
