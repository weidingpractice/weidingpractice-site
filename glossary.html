词汇 · Glossary

词汇Words used in these notes

这些笔记里反复出现的词，按地图分开，各说一句。Words that keep coming up, grouped by map, one short explanation each.

日常身体The everyday body

窝Hollow

身体上凹进去的地方，多半在关节周围：肩窝、肘窝、胯根、膝盖后面。一个窝里常有好几个穴位。国标找穴用的体表标志里，也有腋窝、肘窝、腘窝（GB/T 12346-2021 第 3.5 节）。

A place where the body dips inwards, usually around a joint: the shoulder hollow, the inside of the elbow, the groin, the back of the knee. One hollow often holds several points. The national standard's own surface landmarks include the armpit, the elbow hollow and the back of the knee (GB/T 12346-2021, section 3.5).

我们自己的话Our own words

胯根Kua root

大腿接进躯干的地方，前面腹股沟一带。练习里的说法，书里还没找到出处。现代解剖地图上，这一带叫股三角（待核）。

Where the thigh joins the trunk, around the front of the groin. A word from practice; no book source found yet. On the modern anatomy map this area is called the femoral triangle (to be checked).

医经The medical classics

穴位Point

身体上有名字、有编号的地方。名字和位置以 GB/T 12346-2021 为准；编号（如 GB20）的字母是经络，数字是在这条经上的第几个。这里只教怎么用手找到它，找到以后怎么做，由你自己。

A named, numbered place on the body. Names and locations follow GB/T 12346-2021; in a code such as GB20, the letters are the channel and the number is its place along it. These pages only show how to find a point with your hand; what you do once you have found it is up to you.

寸Cun

找穴用的长度，用被取穴的人自己的身体来量：拇指的宽约 1 寸，四指并拢约 3 寸。国标以身体上的标志和骨度为主，手指量只是补充（GB/T 12346-2021，4.2.3）。

The unit for locating points, measured on the body of the person being located: the width of your thumb is about 1 cun, four fingers together about 3. The standard relies first on body landmarks and proportional measures; finger measures only supplement them (GB/T 12346-2021, 4.2.3).

经络Channel (meridian)

经典里描述的通路，不只是穴位的连线。每一条都有一段走在身体里面，「属」一个藏或府，「络」另一个；体表的那一段，一站一站经过穴位。走向以《灵枢·经脉》原文为准。十二正经以脏腑命名，另有沿背后正中上行的督脉、沿前面正中上行的任脉。

A pathway the classics describe, not just a line between points. Each runs inside the body for a stretch, where it belongs to one zang or fu organ and links with another; its surface stretch passes from point to point. The course follows the text of the Lingshu, "Jingmai". Twelve main channels are named after organs, plus the Governing Vessel up the back and the Conception Vessel up the front.

经筋Sinew channel

《灵枢·经筋》讲的十二条筋：从手指、脚趾起，往躯干和头走，一路结在关节上。和经脉不是同一套路线，也不等于现代的筋膜链。

The twelve sinew channels of the Lingshu, "Jingjin": they start at the fingers and toes and run towards the trunk and head, binding at the joints along the way. They are not the same routes as the main channels, and not the same thing as modern fascial lines.

藏Zang (our image: a lake)

心、肝、脾、肺、肾，五个藏。《素问·五藏别论》：「所谓五藏者，藏精气而不泻也，故满而不能实。」装满的是精气，不是实实在在的东西，所以满，却不实。我们把它画成一片湖：满满一湖水，却不是实心的。湖是我们的意象，不是原文的词。原文据排印本（底本明顾从德翻宋本），原刻字形待核。心包是心外面的一层，《灵枢·经脉》给它单列一条经，但《五藏别论》的五藏、六府里都没有它，所以它有经，却不在五个湖里。

The five zang: heart, liver, spleen, lungs, kidneys. The Suwen, "Wuzang bielun", says they store essence and qi and do not let it out, so they are full but never solid. We draw each as a lake: full of water, yet not solid. "Lake" is our image, not a word from the classic. The quotation follows a modern typeset edition whose base text is Gu Congde's 1550 reprint of the Song edition; the characters of the original woodblock are still to be checked. The pericardium, the layer around the heart, has a channel of its own in the Lingshu, but it is in neither list of the "Wuzang bielun": it has a channel, but is not one of the five lakes.

府Fu (our image: a valley)

胆、胃、小肠、大肠、膀胱、三焦，六个府。同一篇：「六府者，传化物而不藏，故实而不能满也。」经过的东西是实的，但一直在走，不停留，所以永远不满。我们把它画成一道山谷：东西从中间流过去。谷也是我们的意象。同一篇另有「奇恒之府」六个：脑、髓、骨、脉、胆、女子胞。胆在两处都有，这是原文本来的样子。

The six fu: gallbladder, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, bladder, sanjiao. The same chapter says they pass things on and do not store them, so they are filled but never full. What passes through is solid, but it keeps moving, so a fu is never full. We draw each as a valley: things flow through it. "Valley" is our image too. The same chapter also names six "extraordinary fu": brain, marrow, bone, vessels, gallbladder, uterus. The gallbladder is in both lists; that is the text as it stands.

三焦Sanjiao (the three jiao)

六腑之一，没有自己的实体器官：一套管水、管气的通道，分三段。上焦是胸，中焦是上腹，下焦是下腹和盆腔。手臂外侧的三焦经是另一回事。

One of the six fu organs, with no organ of its own: a set of passages for water and qi in three sections. Upper jiao: the chest. Middle jiao: the upper belly. Lower jiao: the lower belly and pelvis. The Sanjiao channel, on the outside of the arm, is a separate thing.

气qi

「上焦开发，宣五谷味，熏肤充身泽毛，若雾露之溉，是谓气。」《灵枢·决气》。原文据排印本（底本明赵府居敬堂本），原刻字形待核。

Lingshu, "Jueqi": the sentence in the classic that says what is called qi. Classical texts are given in Chinese only. The quotation follows a modern typeset edition based on the Ming Zhao edition; the characters of the original woodblock are still to be checked.

颜色Colour

古书讲十二经的颜色，原文见「原文」页「经络的颜色」，原页在「按身体找」最下面。

The classics give each channel a colour; the passages are on the Sources page, and the original pages are at the foot of By body place.

修炼和功法Cultivation and martial arts

丹田Dantian

这个词在不止一张地图上。《黄帝内经》可靠的本文里没有「丹田」；今本《素问》只在后人补入的遗篇《本病论》里出现一次，指头部的上丹田。道家的《黄庭经》把它和呼吸连在一起：「嘘吸庐外，出入丹田」。宋以后的针灸书又把「丹田」当作关元穴的别名，在脐下二寸或三寸，几种说法并存。这里说的是下丹田：肚脐以下、手掌盖住的那一片。

A word found on more than one map. The reliable text of the Huangdi Neijing does not use it; the received Suwen has it once, in the later-added chapter "Benbing lun", and there it means the upper dantian in the head. The Daoist Huangting jing links it with breathing: "breathing out and in, it passes in and out of the dantian". Later acupuncture books, from the Song on, use "dantian" as another name for the point Guanyuan, placing it two or three cun below the navel; the books do not agree on the number. These notes mean the lower dantian: the area under your palm below the navel.

怎么读这些笔记How to read these notes

哪张图Which map

同一片身体，在不同的地图上有不同的名字。从上海去北京：坐飞机，你只要一张地铁图，把你送到机场；开车，要一张路网图；走路，又要一张街道图。路还是那条路，用的地图不同。这里用六张地图：医经（《灵枢》《素问》《难经》和国家标准）；修炼和功法（道家内丹书、太极拳书）；现代解剖；日常身体（膝盖内侧、肩窝这种谁都知道在哪里的说法）；瑜伽和现代练习；还有我们自己的话（练习里的体会，每次都标明）。每个词只放在一张地图上，一句话只站在一张地图上说。

The same stretch of body goes by different names on different maps. To go from Shanghai to Beijing by plane, you need only a metro map to reach the airport; by car you need a road map; on foot, a street map. The way is the same; the map changes. These notes use six maps: the medical classics (Lingshu, Suwen, Nanjing and the national standards); cultivation and martial arts (Daoist inner-alchemy books, taiji books); modern anatomy; the everyday body (words like inner knee or shoulder hollow, which everyone can place); yoga and modern practice; and our own words (what is felt in practice, always marked as such). Each word sits on one map, and each sentence speaks from one map.

有体会 / 可能Felt / possible

有体会：我在自己身体里试过，在这个体式里感觉到了这个地方。可能：按位置推测，还没试过。

Felt: I have tried it in my own body and felt this place in this pose. Possible: a guess from where it sits, not yet tried.

待核To be checked

还没有对过原书和版本的出处。先放着，标明待核，核过再去掉。

A source not yet checked against the original edition. It stays visible, marked, until it is.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
