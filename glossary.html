词汇Words used in these notes

这些笔记里反复出现的词，各说一句。One short explanation for each word that keeps coming up.

气qi

经典里在身体里运行的「气」。这里用来说呼吸和注意力带动的那种流动感，不当作可以测量的东西。

The vital breath or energy the classics describe moving through the body. Here it is a word for what you feel move with breath and attention, not a measurable substance.

穴位Point (acupoint)

身体上有名字的地方，多半是一个小窝。在这些笔记里，不按，只把注意力放在那里。名字后面的代码（GB20、LU10）是国际标准编号：字母是经络，数字是在这条经上的位置。

A named place on the body, usually a small hollow. In these notes you never press it; you rest your attention there. The code after the name (GB20, LU10) is the international standard code: letters for the channel, a number for its place on it.

区域Area

比一个穴位大的一片，不是一个点，比如丹田、胯根、三焦。用整个手掌盖住，不去找一个点。

A larger stretch of the body that is not a single point, such as the dantian, the kua root or the three jiao. You cover it with your palm instead of looking for a spot.

经络Channel (meridian)

经典里描述的通路，不只是穴位的连线。每一条都有一段走在身体里面，「属」一个藏或府，「络」另一个；体表的那一段，一站一站经过穴位。走向以《灵枢·经脉》原文为准。地图上，体表的一段画实线，走在身体里面的一段画淡线。十二正经以脏腑命名，另有沿背后正中上行的督脉、沿前面正中上行的任脉。

A pathway the classics describe, not just a line between points. Each runs inside the body for a stretch, where it belongs to one zang or fu organ and links with another; its surface stretch passes from point to point. The course follows the text of the Lingshu, "Jingmai". On the map the surface stretch is a solid line and the stretch inside the body a faint one. Twelve main channels are named after organs, plus the Governing Vessel up the back and the Conception Vessel up the front.

颜色Colour

经典里讲经络的颜色，有这两段原文。《素问·经络论》：「黄帝问曰：夫络脉之见也，其五色各异，青黄赤白黑不同，其故何也？岐伯对曰：经有常色而络无常变也。帝曰：经之常色何如？岐伯曰：心赤，肺白，肝青，脾黄，肾黑，皆亦应其经脉之色也。帝曰：络之阴阳，亦应其经乎？岐伯曰：阴络之色应其经，阳络之色变无常，随四时而行也。」《灵枢·五音五味》：「……手少阴，藏心，色赤……足少阴，藏肾，色黑……足太阴，藏脾，色黄……手大阴，藏肺，色白……足厥阴，藏肝，色青……」《素问》据京都大学附属图书馆藏《重广补注黄帝内经素问》（RB00002950，第 243 页）；《灵枢》据元刻本卷十，肺一句元刻本作「手大阴」。两叶原图见下面「原叶」。

Two passages in the classics speak of the colours of the channels. Suwen, "Jingluo lun": The Yellow Emperor asked: "When the network vessels show, their five colours differ, blue-green, yellow, red, white and black. Why?" Qi Bo replied: "The channels have constant colours; the network vessels change without constancy." The Emperor asked: "What are the constant colours of the channels?" Qi Bo said: "Heart red, lungs white, liver blue-green, spleen yellow, kidneys black; each matches the colour of its channel." The Emperor asked: "Do the yin and yang network vessels also match their channels?" Qi Bo said: "The colour of the yin network vessels matches their channel; the colour of the yang network vessels changes without constancy, moving with the four seasons." Lingshu, "Wuyin wuwei": "... hand shaoyin, stores the heart, colour red ... foot shaoyin, stores the kidneys, colour black ... foot taiyin, stores the spleen, colour yellow ... hand taiyin, stores the lungs, colour white ... foot jueyin, stores the liver, colour blue-green ..." The Suwen follows the copy held by Kyoto University Library (RB00002950, image 243); the Lingshu follows the 1339 woodblock, juan 10, which writes da for tai in the lung line. Both pages are below, under "The pages".

藏Zang (our image: a lake)

心、肝、脾、肺、肾，五个藏。《素问·五藏别论》：「所谓五藏者，藏精气而不泻也，故满而不能实。」装满的是精气，不是实实在在的东西，所以满，却不实。我们把它画成一片湖：满满一湖水，却不是实心的。湖是我们的意象，不是原文的词。原文据排印本（底本明顾从德翻宋本），原刻字形待核。

The five zang: heart, liver, spleen, lungs, kidneys. The Suwen, "Wuzang bielun", says they store essence and qi and do not let it out, so they are full but never solid. We draw each as a lake: full of water, yet not solid. "Lake" is our image, not a word from the classic. The quotation follows a modern typeset edition whose base text is Gu Congde's 1550 reprint of the Song edition; the characters of the original woodblock are still to be checked.

府Fu (our image: a valley)

胆、胃、小肠、大肠、膀胱、三焦，六个府。同一篇：「六府者，传化物而不藏，故实而不能满也。」经过的东西是实的，但一直在走，不停留，所以永远不满。我们把它画成一道山谷：东西从中间流过去。谷也是我们的意象。同一篇另有「奇恒之府」六个：脑、髓、骨、脉、胆、女子胞。胆在两处都有，这是原文本来的样子。

The six fu: gallbladder, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, bladder, sanjiao. The same chapter says they pass things on and do not store them, so they are filled but never full. What passes through is solid, but it keeps moving, so a fu is never full. We draw each as a valley: things flow through it. "Valley" is our image too. The same chapter also names six "extraordinary fu": brain, marrow, bone, vessels, gallbladder, uterus. The gallbladder is in both lists; that is the text as it stands.

属 · 络 · 表里Belongs to, links with; paired organs

《灵枢·经脉》写每条经的走向时，都说它「属」一个藏或府、「络」另一个。属，是这条经自己的那一个；络，是和它成对的那一个。心经「出属心系，下膈络小肠」；小肠经「入缺盆络心……抵胃属小肠」，倒过来。一湖一谷就这样成了一对，两条经把它们连在一起，叫表里。

When the Lingshu traces each channel, it says the channel belongs to one organ and links with another. It belongs to its own organ and links with its partner. The Heart channel belongs to the heart system and links with the small intestine; the Small Intestine channel does the reverse. So a lake and a valley become a pair, joined by two channels. This pairing is called biao-li, outside and inside.

心包Pericardium

心外面的一层。《灵枢·经脉》给它单列一条经，名字是「心主手厥阴心包络之脉」，和三焦成对。《五藏别论》讲五藏、六府，心包都不在里面。所以它有经，却不在五个湖里，地图上不给它画湖。

The layer around the heart. The Lingshu gives it a channel of its own, named "the vessel of the heart's master, hand jueyin, the pericardium", paired with the sanjiao. The "Wuzang bielun" lists five zang and six fu, and the pericardium is in neither list. So it has a channel but is not one of the five lakes; the map draws no lake for it.

寸cun

找穴位用的「同身寸」：大约是你自己大拇指的宽度。用自己的身体量，所以合你自己的身体。

A body-relative inch used to locate points: about the width of your own thumb. Because it is measured on your own body, it fits you.

丹田Dantian

道家修炼书里的说法，指肚脐以下的一片，不是医书的概念。这里说的是下丹田，用手掌盖住的那一片。

A field below the navel named in Daoist cultivation texts, not in the medical classics. These notes mean the lower dantian, an area under the palm.

三焦Sanjiao (the three jiao)

六腑之一，没有自己的实体器官：一套管水、管气的通道，分三段。上焦是胸，中焦是上腹，下焦是下腹和盆腔。手臂外侧的三焦经是另一回事。

One of the six fu organs, with no organ of its own: a set of passages for water and qi in three sections. Upper jiao: the chest. Middle jiao: the upper belly. Lower jiao: the lower belly and pelvis. The Sanjiao channel, on the outside of the arm, is a separate thing.

胯根Kua root

大腿根前内侧、腹股沟折痕下面的窝，解剖上叫股三角，是腿接进躯干的地方。

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease; anatomically the femoral triangle. Here the leg joins the trunk.

有体会 / 可能Felt / possible

有体会：我在自己身体里试过，这个体式里这个点有用。可能：按位置推测，还没试过。

Felt: I have tested this point in this pose in my own body. Possible: a guess from where it sits, still to be tested.

经与注Scripture and commentary

经文本身，还是后人的注解。笔记里每次都标明，因为注不是经。

A line from the classic itself, or a later scholar's note on it. The notes always say which, because a commentary is not the classic.

待核To be checked

还没有对过原书和版本的出处。先放着，标明待核，核过再去掉。

A source not yet checked against the original edition. It stays visible, marked, until it is.

导引Daoyin

古代中国的呼吸配合动作。马王堆三号汉墓出土的《导引图》（公元前 2 世纪）绘有四十多个人物姿态，有图无文；张家山汉简《引书》用文字记述具体方法，有文无图，两者正好互补。最早的文字记载在《庄子·刻意》（原文作「道引」）：「吹呴呼吸，吐故纳新，熊经鸟申，为寿而已矣。」不过庄子接着说，更高的是「不道引而寿」：他是拿导引作对照，不是在推荐它。英文的「Taoist Yoga」指两样不同的东西：一是阴瑜伽的来源，阴瑜伽的内容来自 Paulie Zink 的 Taoist Yoga，形式和名称成形于 Paul Grilley 与 Sarah Powers；二是赵避尘《性命法诀明指》的英译书名，讲内丹，和身体动作无关。

Ancient Chinese movement done with the breath. The Daoyin Tu silk painting from Mawangdui Tomb 3 (2nd century BCE) shows more than forty figures, with pictures but no text; the Yinshu bamboo slips from Zhangjiashan describe methods in words, with no pictures, so the two complement each other. The earliest written mention is in the Zhuangzi ("Ke Yi"): "Blowing and breathing, expelling the old and taking in the new, hanging like a bear and stretching like a bird: all this is only for long life." But Zhuangzi goes on to rank higher still the person who lives long without daoyin; he cites it as a contrast, not a recommendation. In English, "Taoist Yoga" means two different things. One is a source of Yin Yoga: its content comes from Paulie Zink's Taoist Yoga, and its form and name took shape with Paul Grilley and Sarah Powers. The other is the English title of Zhao Bichen's Xingming fajue mingzhi, a book on inner alchemy, unrelated to postures.

哪张图Which map

同一片身体，在不同的图上有不同的名字：穴位图、内丹图、三焦图、现代解剖。笔记里每次都说清楚用的是哪张图。

The same stretch of body has different names on different maps: the acupoint map, the inner-alchemy map, the Sanjiao map, modern anatomy. These notes always say which map a word belongs to.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
