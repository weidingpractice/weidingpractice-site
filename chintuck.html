收下巴

Chin tuck✓ 有体会 · felt2 个穴位 · 1 条经络
从大椎沿着一条线，一直到风府。One line from Dazhui all the way up to Fengfu.
口令 · Cue

怎么做Practice note

怎么做。平躺，膝盖弯曲，头下不垫，或只垫一条薄毛巾。轻轻点头，像在说「嗯」：下巴往喉咙方向收，后脑勺贴着地面往上拉长。手指摸着脖子两侧，那两条大肌肉不绷紧。停 5 到 10 秒，放松。做几次。How to do it.Lie on your back, knees bent, no pillow or just a thin towel. Nod gently, as if saying "mm": draw the chin towards the throat and let the back of the head lengthen up along the floor. Keep your fingers on the two big muscles at the sides of the neck; they stay soft. Hold 5 to 10 seconds, then let go. A few times.

幅度很小。是「收」，不是低头。全程不应该痛；手臂发麻、头晕，就停下。A very small movement.It is a tuck, not a bow of the head. It should never hurt; if your arm goes numb or you feel dizzy, stop.

放在哪里。热身里：肩 → 收下巴 → 头部转动 → 侧倾 → 脊柱。融心式（下巴点地）待得久之后：躺下做几次，短，浅。Where it goes.In the warm-up: shoulders → chin tuck → head movements → side bends → spine. After a long Melting Heart with the chin on the floor: lie down and do a few, short and shallow.

2026-11-20，第一次。从大椎沿着一条线一直到风府，很明显。感觉一开始是从很像心脏那块地方，很深沉地升上来。20 November 2026, the first time.From Dazhui, one clear line all the way up to Fengfu. At first it felt as if it rose, deep and slow, from somewhere like the heart.

用手怎么找

GV16 · Wind Palace · 督脉
✓ 在这个体式里感觉到 · felt in this pose 首次记录 · first noted 2026-11-20
用手怎么找
一只手，中指指腹放在脖子后面发际线的正中，往上推。推到后脑勺那块骨头挡住手指的地方，手指下面那个小凹陷就是风府。One hand: put the pad of your middle finger on the hairline at the middle of the back of your neck and push upwards. Where the bone at the back of your skull stops the finger, the small hollow under it is Fengfu.

从尾骨下方起，沿脊柱正中往上，到后颈，进入头部。

From below the tailbone, up the midline of the spine to the back of the neck, and into the head.
