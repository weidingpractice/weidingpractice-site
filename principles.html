练习原则What holds across poses

体式页记的是一个体式里发生了什么；这里记的是在很多体式里都成立的规律。Each pose page records what happens in that pose. This page records what holds across many of them.

注意力点要成对。Attention points come in pairs.

要做好 alignment，就把注意力放在成对的两个点上。左右成对：两条腿，屁股两边，肩胛骨两边，手两边。前后也成对：劳宫放在骶骨上，和前面的肚脐成对，呼吸时前后就对上了。只放一个点，身体还是找不到 alignment。内外也成对（11-12）：同一条腿上，大腿根内侧的窝（胯根，inner groin）和外侧的环跳成对，是窝和内外的对称。

To find alignment, rest your attention on points that come in pairs. Left and right: two legs, two sides of the buttocks, two shoulder blades, two hands. Front and back too: rest Laogong on the sacrum and pair it with the navel, and as you breathe, front and back line up. With one point, the body still can't find its alignment. Inside and outside pair up too (12 Nov): on the same leg, the hollow at the inner groin pairs with Huantiao on the outside, a hollow matched across the hip.

幅度永远不是重点。Range is never the point.

转得深还是浅，跟身体条件、练习时间长短有关。我们练的永远不是幅度，而是觉知和呼吸的深度。

How deep you go depends on your body and how long you have practised. You never practise range; you practise awareness and the depth of the breath.

transition 要转臀，转才不卡。Turn the hips in transitions.

臀部是可以转的，而且一定要转。大多数人的 transition 不转臀部，这是个很大的问题。两个提示：高弓步到战士二；战士二到 Skandasana。

The hips can turn, and they must. Most people keep the hips still in transitions, and that is a big problem. Two cues: High Lunge to Warrior II; Warrior II to Skandasana.

站立力线：什么时候用哪个点。Standing lines: which point, when.

  • 整条站立力线：涌泉 → 太溪 → 血海 → 胯根（inner groin 的窝）→ 环跳。每一站都是一个窝：涌泉、足弓、血海、inner groin
    The whole standing line: Yongquan → Taixi → Xuehai → the kua root (the inner groin hollow) → Huantiao. Every stop is a hollow: Yongquan, the arch, Xuehai, the inner groin
  • 上肢：劳宫 + 肩窝。只看肩窝在不在、有没有被挤压，不用管手指和肩胛骨怎么转。用力的时候，呼吸穿过肩窝，不停：推起来时深吸气，不憋气（11-13）
    The arms: Laogong and the shoulder hollow. Only check that the hollow is there and not squeezed; when you push, let the breath pass through the shoulder hollow and keep it moving, breathing in deeply as you push up, never holding it (13 Nov). You needn't think about the fingers or how the shoulder blades rotate
  • 用后腿的力（balls of the back foot）：涌泉对上太溪
    Using the back leg ("the balls of the back foot"): Yongquan lines up with Taixi
  • 转臀的 transition：高弓步 → 战士二，战士二 → Skandasana：臀部正中心最深的点（意象）
    Hip-turning transitions: High Lunge → Warrior II, Warrior II → Skandasana: The deepest point in the centre of each buttock (an image)
  • 高弓步 → 战士三，高弓步 → 站立扭转：环跳
    High Lunge → Warrior III, High Lunge → standing twist: Huantiao
  • 站立后弯（站立下轮）：涌泉 → 太溪 → 血海 → 腹股沟前面（冲门或气冲，待确认）
    Standing backbend (drop-back to Wheel): Yongquan → Taixi → Xuehai → front of the groin (Chongmen or Qichong, to confirm)

先说用的是哪张图。Say which map you are using.

待验证 · still testing

同一片身体，在不同的图上有不同的名字。讲到一个部位，先说用的是哪张图：穴位图（气海、关元）、内丹图（丹田、小周天）、三焦图（下焦）。两张图的形容词不互借：「聚、充实、气足」是丹田的词；「通、不通、顺、堵、壅」是三焦的词。虚实寒热两边都用，不能拿来分。同一个「满」字，丹田满是练出来的，下焦胀满是病。不说「下焦饱满」，也不说「丹田通畅」。还有一张图要单独标：王唯工的脉波谐波是一个物理模型。从它推出来的话，比如「上焦属胆经」「奇经八脉属三焦经」，只能写成他的推论，不能当中医常识。

One stretch of the body carries different names on different maps. When you name a place, first say which map you mean: the acupoint map (Qihai, Guanyuan), the inner-alchemy map (dantian, Microcosmic Orbit), or the Sanjiao map (lower jiao). The maps don't share adjectives. Gathered, filled and abundant belong to the dantian; flowing, blocked, smooth, stuck and congested belong to the Sanjiao. Both maps use empty and full, cold and hot, so those words can't tell them apart. The same word full means progress in the dantian and illness in the lower jiao. Never call the lower jiao full, and never call the dantian unblocked. One more map needs its own label: Wang Wei-kung's pulse harmonics are a physical model. Claims drawn from it, such as "the upper jiao belongs to the Gallbladder channel" or "the eight extraordinary vessels belong to the Triple Burner channel", are his inferences, never common TCM knowledge.
