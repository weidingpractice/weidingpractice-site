体式 · Pose

鹰式手臂缠绕Eagle arms

Garuḍāsana（手臂） · 名称对照 · Pose names →

不是掌心相对：下方的手按住上方手的鱼际。Instead of pressing palm to palm, let the lower hand press the Yuji point of the upper hand.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

鱼
鱼际 Yuji
LU10
有体会 · felt

手掌朝上，大拇指根部那块鼓起的肉（大鱼际）的中间，靠手背和手掌交界的边上。

Palm up: the middle of the fleshy mound at the base of the thumb, on the edge where the palm meets the back of the hand.

怎么做How I practise it

下方的手按上方手的鱼际。手臂缠好以后，我不把两个掌心相对，而是用下方那只手，去按上方那只手的鱼际。
The lower hand presses the upper hand's Yuji.Once the arms are wrapped, I don't bring the palms together. I use the lower hand to press Yuji on the upper hand.
呼吸到了上后背。按住鱼际，能明显感觉到呼吸到了上后背，肩胛骨打开，非常明显。
The breath reaches the upper back.Pressing Yuji, I clearly feel the breath reach the upper back, and the shoulder blades open.
教课时。我让学生做这个体式、体会这个穴位，再加上按鱼际这句提示，就能听到学生的呼气明显变长。
In class.When I teach this pose and ask students to feel this point, adding the cue to press Yuji, I can hear their exhales grow noticeably longer.
