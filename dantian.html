丹田Dantian · Area · Lower Dantian

用手怎么找How to find it

手掌横放在肚脐下面，掌心对着小腹。手掌盖住的这一片，就是下丹田。它不是一个点，是一片区域。
Lay your palm across your belly just below the navel. The area under your hand is the lower dantian. It is an area, not a point.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。 You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

不是一个点，是一片区域丹田本身不是一个穴位，所以没有编号；它盖住的这一片里，气海、关元各有编号。古人都说它在脐下，但几寸说法不一：有说二寸，有说三寸，也有说二到三寸之间。中医穴位里，脐下一寸半是气海，脐下三寸是关元；丹田就在这一带。所以这里不找一个点，用整个手掌盖住这一片。
An area, not a pointThe dantian is not an acupoint itself, so it has no code; Qihai and Guanyuan, which lie inside the area it covers, each have one. The old texts all place it below the navel but disagree on how far: two cun, three cun, or somewhere between (a cun is a body-relative inch, about the width of your own thumb). On the acupoint map, Qihai sits 1.5 cun below the navel and Guanyuan 3 cun below; the dantian covers this stretch. So you don't look for one point here; you cover the whole area with your palm.
名字从哪里来「丹田」这个名字来自道家修炼的书，不是来自医书。现存较早写下「出入丹田」的是《黄庭经》。后来又分出三个丹田：头、心下、脐下。这里说的是脐下的下丹田。
Where the name comes fromThe name comes from Daoist cultivation texts, not from the medical classics. The Yellow Court Classic is among the earliest surviving texts to say that breath goes out and in through the dantian. Later traditions name three: in the head, below the heart, and below the navel. These notes mean the lower one.
丹田和小周天：两张不同的地图穴位地图上的丹田，和小周天练习里的丹田，名字一样，却不是同一张图。小周天是后来内丹和气功的练习图像：气沿督脉上行，沿任脉下行。幻椅式里「吸气从下丹田沿脊柱往上」，用的是小周天这张图。
The dantian and the Microcosmic Orbit: two different mapsThe dantian on the acupoint map and the dantian in Microcosmic Orbit practice share a name but belong to different maps. The Microcosmic Orbit is a later image from Daoist inner alchemy and qigong: qi rises along the Governing Vessel and comes down along the Conception Vessel. In Chair Pose, "breathe in from the lower dantian and up along the spine" draws on this second map.
气沉丹田：起点，不是停住站桩的口令是「屈膝松胯，气沉丹田，意守涌泉，虚领顶劲」。丹田和涌泉一起用：气从丹田往下沉，落到脚底。不是把气守在丹田不动。
Sinking the qi: a starting point, not a resting placeThe standing cue runs: bend the knees, release the hips, sink the qi to the dantian, rest your attention on Yongquan, lift lightly through the crown. The dantian and Yongquan work together: the qi sinks from the dantian and lands in the soles. You don't hold the qi still in the dantian.
丹田不是下焦下腹这一片，在两张图上有两个名字。在三焦这张图上，肚脐以下叫下焦。下焦是六腑里三焦的一段，古人说「下焦如渎」，像一条水沟，讲的是通不通。丹田来自内丹，是「田」，是「鼎」，讲的是聚和炼。所以这里说「丹田」，用的是内丹这张图；说「下焦」，用的是中医这张图。两边的形容词不互借：不说「下焦饱满」，也不说「丹田通畅」。
Not the lower jiaoThe lower belly carries two names on two maps. On the Sanjiao map, the area below the navel is the lower jiao. The lower jiao is one section of the Sanjiao, one of the six fu organs; the classics say it works like a drainage ditch, so the question there is whether things flow. The dantian comes from Daoist inner alchemy. It is a field, a cauldron, so the question there is gathering and refining. When these notes say dantian, they use the inner-alchemy map; when they say lower jiao, they use the medical map. The two maps don't share adjectives: these notes never call the lower jiao full, and never call the dantian unblocked.
怎么放注意力手盖在这里，不用去找某种特定的感觉。只注意这一片此刻是什么样：清楚、模糊、温暖、凉，还是没有明显感觉。感觉是真实的；感觉意味着什么，仍然是开放的。
How to rest your attention hereRest your hand here and don't go looking for any particular feeling. Just notice what the area is like right now: clear, blurred, warm, cool, or nothing in particular. The sensation is real. The interpretation remains open.

练习框架Practice framework

以现代呼吸机制为底，经典原文作对照，自己在体式里的体会作验证。 Modern breathing mechanics first, the classics as a cross-check, my own practice as the test.

这个框架的立场 Where this framework stands

  • 以现代呼吸机制为底，经典原文作对照，自己在体式里的体会作验证。Modern breathing mechanics come first, the classics serve as a cross-check, and my own practice in the poses is the test.
  • 不采用内丹的「炼精化炁、结丹」之类说法来描述自己的体感。小周天只当作一个意象，不当作事实。I don't describe what I feel in inner-alchemy terms such as "refining essence into qi" or "forming the elixir". The Microcosmic Orbit is an image here, not a fact.
  • 先说用的是哪张图：找位置，用穴位图；讲呼吸，用现代解剖；引原文，标明是经还是注。Say which map you are using: the acupoint map to find a place, modern anatomy to talk about breathing, and for every classical line, whether it is scripture or commentary.
  • 感觉是真实的；感觉意味着什么，仍然是开放的。The sensation is real. The interpretation remains open.

呼吸的机制（现代解剖） How breathing works

  • 吸气的主力是膈肌。膈肌收缩，穹顶下降，胸腔变大，空气进来。安静呼气基本是被动的：膈肌放松、回弹。The diaphragm does most of the work of breathing in. It contracts, its dome lowers, the chest grows, and air comes in. A quiet out-breath is mostly passive: the diaphragm relaxes and springs back.
  • 膈肌下降，把腹腔里的内容往下、往前推。腹腔像一个装满水的罐子，几乎不能压缩，压力要找出口：腹壁往外，盆底往下，下肋往两侧和后背张开。As the diaphragm lowers, it pushes the contents of the belly down and forward. The belly is like a jar full of water and barely compresses, so the pressure has to go somewhere: the belly wall moves out, the pelvic floor moves down, and the lower ribs widen to the sides and back.
  • 盆底和膈肌像上下两层膈：吸气时一起轻轻下降，呼气时一起回升。盆底本来就跟着呼吸起伏。The pelvic floor and the diaphragm work like two diaphragms, one above the other: they lower gently together on the in-breath and rise together on the out-breath. The pelvic floor already moves with every breath.
  • 腹横肌像一条围腰。它适度张着，膈肌下降时腹压升高，腰椎得到支撑。这是现代所说的「核心稳定」。The transverse abdominis works like a wide belt. With a little tone in it, the pressure in the belly rises as the diaphragm lowers, and the lumbar spine is supported. This is what modern training calls core stability.
  • 所以「顺」和「逆」的差别不在膈肌：吸气时膈肌总是下降。差别在腹壁：顺腹式时腹壁让开；逆腹式时腹壁不让，压力转到下肋两侧、后背和腰。So natural and reverse breathing don't differ at the diaphragm, which always lowers on the in-breath. They differ at the belly wall: in natural breathing it gives way; in reverse breathing it holds, and the pressure moves to the sides of the lower ribs, the back and the waist.
  • 用力过头会怎样：腹壁收死，膈肌下不去，呼吸跑到胸口和脖子；憋气用力，腹压和血压骤升；提肛，就和盆底在吸气时自然的下降对着干。What happens when you force it: clench the belly wall and the diaphragm can't lower, so the breath climbs into the chest and neck; hold your breath and push, and pressure in the belly and blood pressure jump; lift the pelvic floor and you work against its natural lowering on the in-breath.

这样练，最贴近身体本来的功能 Practising with the body, not against it

  • 第一步：先有自然的腹式呼吸。躺着，一只手放在下腹。吸气时手被轻轻推起，呼气时落下。这一步稳了，再往下走。Step one: start with natural belly breathing. Lie down with one hand on your lower belly. The hand rises gently as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out. Move on only when this is steady.
  • 第二步：只「逆」一点点。手轻轻托住下腹，吸气时下腹往手里推，手不让它鼓出去，两边平衡，只留一个饱满感。下肋两侧和后背会跟着张开。Step two: reverse only a little. Cradle the lower belly with your hand; as you breathe in, the belly presses into your hand and your hand doesn't let it bulge, so the two balance and only a sense of fullness stays. The lower ribs and the back widen with it.
  • 盆底不提，跟着呼吸走。Leave the pelvic floor alone; let it move with the breath.
  • 鼻吸鼻呼，安静，不憋气，不刻意拉长。Breathe in and out through the nose, quietly. Don't hold the breath, and don't stretch it on purpose.
  • 幅度不是重点。有饱满感就够了，幅度大反而不对。Range is not the point. A sense of fullness is enough; a big movement is a sign you've gone wrong.
  • 先在静态里练熟，再带进体式。Get used to it lying still before you take it into the poses.

停下来的信号 When to stop

  • 头晕、发麻、胸口发紧、肩膀和脖子耸起来、脸憋胀、腰痛：回到自然呼吸。Dizziness, tingling, a tight chest, shoulders and neck creeping up, a flushed face, or back pain: go back to natural breathing.
  • 怀孕、高血压、腹部手术之后、疝气、盆底有问题时，先问医生，不练逆腹式。If you are pregnant, have high blood pressure, have had abdominal surgery, a hernia or pelvic floor problems, ask your doctor first and don't practise reverse breathing.
  • 这是关于注意力和呼吸的练习，不是治疗。This is a practice of attention and breath, not a treatment.

经典原文：只留可靠的 Classical sources kept

  • 《黄庭外景经》「嘘吸庐外，出入丹田」：呼吸是出入、经过丹田，不是停在里面。The Yellow Court Classic: "breath goes out and in through the dantian". The breath passes through the dantian; it doesn't stop there.
  • 《太极拳论》「虚领顶劲，气沉丹田」：站桩口令里「虚领顶劲，气沉丹田」这一句的通行出处；「屈膝松胯，意守涌泉」是自己的口令。通行题王宗岳作，成书与作者有不同意见。气沉丹田要和涌泉一起读：从丹田往下落，不是守住不动。The Taijiquan Lun: "lift lightly through the crown, sink the qi to the dantian". This is the usual source of that one line of the standing cue; "bend the knees, release the hips, rest attention on Yongquan" are my own words. The text is traditionally attributed to Wang Zongyue, though its dating and authorship are disputed. Read "sink the qi" together with Yongquan: the qi drops from the dantian; it isn't held there.
  • 《十三势歌》「刻刻留心在腰间，腹内松净气腾然」（「净」，一系传本作「静」）：腹内要松。作「净」，对得上不收死腹壁；作「静」，对得上不提肛、不躁动。The Song of Thirteen Postures: "keep the waist in mind at every moment; the belly relaxed and clear inside, the qi rises" (one line of copies reads "still" for "clear"). Either way the belly stays relaxed: "clear" matches not clenching the belly wall; "still" matches not lifting the pelvic floor and not straining.
  • 《灵枢·寒热病》「脐下三寸，关元也」；《针灸资生经》「当以脐下二寸为是」：位置古人说法不一，所以用整个手掌盖住这一片。The Lingshu: "three cun below the navel is Guanyuan"; the Zhenjiu Zisheng Jing: "two cun below the navel is right". The old texts disagree on the place, so cover the whole area with your palm.
  • 《难经·六十六难》「脐下肾间动气……故名曰原」：原文用「原」，不用「丹田」。The Classic of Difficulties, chapter 66: "the moving qi between the kidneys below the navel ... is called the source". The text says "source", not "dantian".
  • 《灵枢·营卫生会》「下焦如渎」：这是三焦图，讲通不通，不要和丹田的饱满混在一起。The Lingshu: "the lower jiao is like a drainage ditch". That belongs to the Sanjiao map, which is about flow; don't mix it with the fullness of the dantian.

在体式里的练习方向 Directions to explore in the poses

  • 幻椅式：吸气时下腹饱满，看脊柱是不是跟着变长。对照你的口令「吸气从下丹田沿脊柱往上」。Chair: as you breathe in and the lower belly fills, notice whether the spine lengthens. Compare it with your own cue, "breathe in from the lower dantian and up along the spine".
  • 站立体式（战士二、高弓步、树式）：丹田和涌泉成对。看气往下落时，脚底是不是更稳。Standing poses (Warrior II, High Lunge, Tree): pair the dantian with Yongquan. As the qi drops, notice whether the feet feel steadier.
  • 前后成对：手放在骶骨（八髎）上，和前面的丹田成对呼吸。吸气时，前后两只手是不是都被推开一点。Front and back in a pair: rest a hand on the sacrum (Baliao) and breathe with it and the dantian in front. As you breathe in, notice whether both hands are pressed out a little.
  • 扭转（祈祷扭转、三角扭转）：腹部被挤压时，饱满感去了哪里：下肋、侧腰，还是后背。Twists (Prayer Twist, Revolved Triangle): when the belly is squeezed, where does the fullness go: the lower ribs, the side waist, or the back?
  • 承重体式（四柱支撑、下犬）：只观察，不加逆腹式。看自己是不是在憋气。Weight-bearing poses (Chaturanga, Downward Dog): only watch; don't add reverse breathing. Notice whether you are holding your breath.
  • 鞍式、蝴蝶：前侧被拉开或放松时，下腹还能不能有饱满感。Saddle and Butterfly: as the front of the body opens or softens, notice whether the lower belly can still feel full.
  • 记录时，穴位选「丹田」，「觉知放在哪」写下当时的感觉：清楚、模糊、温暖、凉、饱满、没有感觉。In the log, choose "dantian" as the point and write what you felt under "where attention goes": clear, blurred, warm, cool, full, or nothing.

腹部和别处不一样 Why the belly is different

  • 腿、手臂、肩胛骨的力量可以练出来；腹部的感觉却和内脏、腹腔、肌肉、脂肪、食物、排泄、女性的生理周期都连在一起。所以记丹田，也记这几样。You can build strength in your legs, arms and shoulder blades. Sensation in the belly is tied to the organs, the abdominal cavity, muscle, fat, food, digestion and, for women, the menstrual cycle. So when I log the dantian, I log these too.
  • 「只感觉到肚脐以下一片」是内脏感觉本来的分辨率：内脏的感受器少，定位天生模糊。手掌盖住那一片，是给模糊的内脏感觉配一个清楚的锚。"I only feel a stretch below the navel": that is how sharp sensation from the organs naturally is. The organs have few receptors and place things vaguely. A palm over the area gives that vague sense a clear anchor.
  • 生理期前后肚子胀，感觉不清楚是正常的。那几天不要求自己「感觉清楚」。Around a period the belly is often bloated, and feeling less is normal. On those days, don't ask yourself to feel clearly.
  • 腹肌绷得太硬，逆腹式需要的那一点「不让」就没有余地。腹壁要能动。If the belly muscles are held too hard, there's no room for the small "holding" that reverse breathing needs. The belly wall has to be free to move.
  • 《素问·生气通天论》「谨和五味，骨正筋柔，气血以流，腠理以密」：饮食调和是练习的一部分。日志里每天点一下：今天是原型食物，还是有深加工。The Suwen: "balance the five flavours, and the bones stay true, the sinews supple, qi and blood flow, and the skin's pores close well". Eating is part of the practice. Each day the log asks one question: whole foods today, or something ultra-processed?
  • 这是练习日志，不是实验。不量腰围，不称体重。记下状态、饮食、排便、周期，日子久了，自己会看到规律。This is a practice log, not an experiment. No measuring the waist, no scales. Note how you feel, what you ate, your bowels and your cycle, and over time the patterns show themselves.

没有采用的 Left out on purpose

  • 《实用瘫痪康复》把逆腹式定义为吸气时腹部内陷、同时提肛。这和膈肌、盆底的自然配合相反，也和课上「不要提盆底肌」相反，不采用。One rehabilitation textbook defines reverse breathing as drawing the belly in on the in-breath while lifting the anus. That works against the natural partnership of the diaphragm and pelvic floor, and against the teacher's "don't lift the pelvic floor", so I don't use it.
  • 「逆呼吸是捷径」「先顺后逆才安全」「丹田化炁、三焦输布」：都是二手转述，没核对原书，不作依据。"Reverse breathing is a shortcut", "learn natural breathing first to be safe", "the dantian turns qi and the Sanjiao spreads it": all second-hand, unchecked against the original books, so none of them is a basis here.
  • 「顺则生人，逆则成仙」：这是内丹讲修炼方向的话，不是讲腹壁的方向，不拿来解释呼吸。"Going with the current makes a person; going against it makes an immortal": this is inner alchemy talking about the direction of cultivation, not about the belly wall, so I don't use it to explain breathing.
  • 「丹田」作为一个可以量出寸数的精确点：古人自己说法就不一。The dantian as an exact point measurable in cun: the old texts themselves don't agree.

任脉 Conception Vessel

从耻骨下方起，沿身体前面正中往上，经肚脐、胸口、喉咙，到下巴，再到眼睛下面。

From below the pubic bone, up the midline of the front of the body, through the navel, chest and throat, to the chin and below the eyes.

和别人对照Compared with

同时代老师的话，短引、署名。 Contemporary teachers, quoted briefly and by name.

他教逆腹式时，只要一个饱满感，盆底不另外去提：
When he teaches reverse breathing, he asks only for a sense of fullness, without lifting the pelvic floor on purpose:

The old texts disagree on the distance. The Song-dynasty Zhenjiu Zisheng Jing insists on two cun, which shows that several answers were in circulation:
论丹田穴，当以脐下二寸为是。
