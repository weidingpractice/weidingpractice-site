環跳

环跳 Huantiao

GB30 · Jumping Circle · 胆经 Gallbladder channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

在臀部，股骨大转子最凸点与骶管裂孔连线的外1/3与内2/3交点处。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.11.30 条，第 26 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.11.30, p. 26 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the buttock region, at the junction of the lateral one third and medial two thirds of the line connecting the prominence of the greater trochanter with the sacral hiatus.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：侧卧，伸下腿，上腿屈髋屈膝取穴。The standard's own note: Lie on your side, lower leg straight, upper leg bent at hip and knee.

用手怎么找
左侧躺，下面的腿伸直，上面的右腿屈髋屈膝。右手摸到屁股外侧最凸的那块骨头。右手拇指第一个关节压在这块骨头的最高点上，拇指尖指向尾骨。拇指尖落下的地方，差不多就是环跳。Lie on your left side, lower leg straight, right leg bent at hip and knee. With your right hand, find the most prominent bone on the outside of your hip. Press the first joint of your right thumb onto the highest point of that bone, with your thumb tip pointing towards your tailbone. Where the thumb tip lands is roughly Huantiao.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

胆经Gallbladder channel

从外眼角起，绕头侧到后脑（风池），沿身体侧面往下，经髋（环跳），到第四脚趾。

From the outer corner of the eye, around the side of the head to the back of the skull (Fengchi), down the side of the body, through the hip (Huantiao), to the fourth toe.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.
