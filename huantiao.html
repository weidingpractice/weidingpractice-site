环跳Huantiao · GB30 · Jumping Circle

标准定位Standard location

在臀部，股骨大转子最凸点与骶管裂孔连线的外 1/3 与内 2/3 交点处。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》，已对原文

In the buttock region, at the junction of the lateral one third and medial two thirds of the line connecting the prominence of the greater trochanter with the sacral hiatus.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

注：标准原注：侧卧，伸下腿，上腿屈髋屈膝取穴。Note: The Chinese standard adds: find it lying on your side, lower leg straight, upper leg bent at hip and knee.

用手怎么找How to find it

侧躺，上面那条腿弯起来。摸到屁股外侧最凸的那块骨头，它后面最深的窝。
Lie on your side, top knee bent: the deepest hollow behind the bony point on the outside of your hip.

不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

胆经 Gallbladder channel

从外眼角起，绕头侧到后脑（风池），沿身体侧面往下，经髋（环跳），到第四脚趾。

From the outer corner of the eye, around the side of the head to the back of the skull (Fengchi), down the side of the body, through the hip (Huantiao), to the fourth toe.
