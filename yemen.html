用手怎么找How to find it

手背朝上，无名指和小指之间的指缝，往手腕方向一点点的小窝。
Back of the hand: the small hollow just behind the web between ring and little fingers.

不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

三焦经 Sanjiao channel

从无名指端起，沿手背、手臂外侧往上，到肩，经颈侧、耳后，到眉梢和眼外角。

From the tip of the ring finger, up the back of the hand and the outside of the arm, over the shoulder, along the side of the neck and behind the ear, to the end of the eyebrow and the outer corner of the eye.

经典原文Source

液门的位置。这是五输穴的荥穴，不是循行：
Where Yemen lies. This is about the Spring point among the five transport points, not the route of the channel:
溜于液门，液门，小指次指之间也，为荥。
