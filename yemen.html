液門

液门 Yemen

TE2 · Fluid Gate · 三焦经 Sanjiao channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

在手背，第4、5指间，指蹼缘上方赤白肉际凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.10.2 条，第 22 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.10.2, p. 22 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the dorsum of the hand, in the depression superior to the web margin between the ring and little fingers, at the border between the red and white flesh.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
手背朝上，无名指和小指之间的指缝，往手腕方向一点点的小窝。Back of the hand: the small hollow just behind the web between ring and little fingers.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

三焦经Sanjiao channel

从无名指端起，沿手背、手臂外侧往上，到肩，经颈侧、耳后，到眉梢和眼外角。

From the tip of the ring finger, up the back of the hand and the outside of the arm, over the shoulder, along the side of the neck and behind the ear, to the end of the eyebrow and the outer corner of the eye.
《灵枢·经脉》三焦手少阳之脉，起于小指次指之端，上出两指之间，循手表腕，出臂外两骨之间，上贯肘，循臑外上肩……入缺盆，布膻中，散络心包，下膈，循属三焦。……其支者，从耳后入耳中，出走耳前，过客主人前，交颊，至目锐眦。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：「散络」：多数本子如此；张其成全解本作「散落」，底本待核。
