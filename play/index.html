以身观身Seeing Through One Body

一个人的身体，看见更大的世界。瑜伽是印度的，我练着练着，有了中国的气韵。One body, and through it a wider world. Yoga came from India; as I kept practising, it took on a Chinese spirit.

穴位是身体上一个有名字的地方。练习时，把注意力放在这里，不用按，只是留意。 A point is a named place on the body. In practice you don't press it; you rest your attention there.
