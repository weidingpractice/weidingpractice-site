Use it

Everything written on this site in our own words is yours to use: the names of the points and what they mean, where they are on the body, how to find them with your hand, which channel they belong to, the stories and images behind them. Use it with your clients, in your classes, in your posts, in your products, in your marketing. You don't need to ask us.

Say where it came from

Add a link to the page you took it from, for example: Source: Seeing Through One Body, weidingpractice.com/laogong.html. That link is enough. If anyone asks how you know, the evidence is on that page: the classical text, the original woodblock pages, the national standard.

Three things to leave on our pages

  • the photographs of old books (they belong to the libraries that keep them);
  • the exact wording of the Chinese national standards;
  • anything we quote from other teachers' manuals.

You don't need these. They are our receipts. Link to us and they come with you.

One thing to keep straight

This site describes where things are and what the classics say. It does not say what treats what. Please don't present it as a treatment.
