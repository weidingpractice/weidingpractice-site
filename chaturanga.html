位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease. Rest your attention; don't press.

Find the Yunmen hollow, slide down one thumb-width and slightly inward; it is the deeper spot under your finger.

Back of the hand: the small hollow just behind the web between ring and little fingers.

怎么做How I practise it

注意力在劳宫的力线上。手指放在地上就可以了，但注意力要放在劳宫，放在劳宫的力线上，这样手腕到肩膀的力线就非常顺。
Attention on the Laogong line.Just place your fingers on the floor, and rest your attention on Laogong, on the line that runs from it. The line from wrist to shoulder then runs very smoothly.
肩窝很重要。真的不是方向的问题，而是要把觉知放在肩窝，呼吸要到肩窝。可能不只是云门，还有更深一点的点。
The shoulder hollow matters.It really isn't about direction. Rest your attention in the shoulder hollow and breathe into it. It may be more than Yunmen; there is a deeper spot too.
