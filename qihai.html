标准定位Standard location

在下腹部，脐中下1.5寸，前正中线上。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.14.6 条，第 29–30 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.14.6, p. 29–30 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the lower abdomen, 1.5 B-cun inferior to the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior median line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
食指和中指并拢，放在肚脐正下方，两指的下沿。Two fingers together just below the navel: the point is at their lower edge.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

任脉Conception Vessel

从耻骨下方起，沿身体前面正中往上，经肚脐、胸口、喉咙，到下巴，再到眼睛下面。

From below the pubic bone, up the midline of the front of the body, through the navel, chest and throat, to the chin and below the eyes.
《素问·骨空论》任脉者，起于中极之下，以上毛际，循腹里，上关元，至咽喉，上颐循面入目。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：异文：「上颐循面入目」六字，《医学原始》校记称《难经》《甲乙经》无。以后写任脉和面部、眼睛的关系，要先说明这一处。
