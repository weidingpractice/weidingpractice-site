鱼际 Yuji

LU10 · Fish Border · 肺经 Lung channel

标准定位Standard location

在手掌，第1掌骨桡侧中点赤白肉际处。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.1.10 条，第 8 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.1.10, p. 8 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the palm, radial to the midpoint of the first metacarpal bone, at the border between the red and white flesh.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
手掌朝上。大拇指下面有一根骨头，沿着它找到中间。手指在这里从手心往侧边摸过去：手心的皮肤软，翻到侧边就变硬、变紧了。软变硬的那条线上，就是。Palm up. Under the thumb runs a bone; find the middle of it. There, feel from the palm toward the side of the hand: the palm skin is soft, and as you come round the edge it turns firmer and tighter. The point is on that line where soft turns firm.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

From the upper outer chest (Zhongfu, Yunmen), down the front edge of the inner arm, to the tip of the thumb.
