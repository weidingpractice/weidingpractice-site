怎么读这些笔记How to read these notes

这里的每一条，都是我在垫子上用自己的身体试过、记下来的。每个连接都标了「有体会」或「可能」：有体会是我在身体上验证过的，可能是按位置推测、还要验证的。原文写明出处，不翻译。
Everything here is something I have tested in my own body on the mat and written down. Every link is marked felt or possible. Felt means I have tested it in my own body; possible means a guess from location, still to test. Classical sources are named and left untranslated.
