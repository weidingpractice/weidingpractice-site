练习里的穴位Points for attention, inside the poses

穴位是身体上一个有名字的地方。练习时，把注意力放在这里，不用按，只是留意。这里是我在垫子上一个体式一个体式验证过的记录。A point is a named place on the body. In practice you don't press it; you rest your attention there. These are notes I have tested on the mat, one pose at a time.

体式Poses

下犬式Downward Dog

手指间像夹着一根烟的宽度，力线不断。

Fingers apart by the width of a cigarette; the line of force never breaks.

鞍式Saddle Pose

只要血海没有被挤压、是滋养的，膝盖就是安全的。

As long as Xuehai feels unsqueezed and nourished, your knees are safe.

练习原则Working principles

跨体式都成立的规律，比如：注意力点要成对。

What holds across poses, such as: attention points come in pairs.

怎么读这些笔记How to read these notes

每个连接都标了「有体会」或「可能」：有体会是我在身体上验证过的，可能是按位置推测、还要验证的。原文写明出处，不翻译。
Every link is marked felt or possible. Felt means I have tested it in my own body; possible means a guess from location, still to test. Classical sources are named and left untranslated.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
