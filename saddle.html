鞍式Saddle Pose

Supta Vīrāsana（阴瑜伽叫 Saddle）

感受血海处没有挤压，体会滋养的感受。Feel that Xuehai is not squeezed, and notice the sense of nourishment there.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

血
血海 Xuehai
SP10
有体会 · felt

坐着弯膝。右手掌盖住左膝盖骨，拇指斜向大腿内侧，拇指尖落下的地方。

Seated, knee bent: cup your right palm over your left kneecap; the tip of your thumb points to it on the inner thigh.

怎么做How I practise it

我最 struggle 的体式。我大腿前侧非常紧，所以每天都做。往下躺的时候，躺在抱枕上，减少后弯的幅度。
My hardest pose.The fronts of my thighs are very tight, so I do it every day. When I lie back, I lie on a bolster to make the backbend smaller.
在正常的活动范围里做。很多人觉得这个体式伤膝盖。从膝盖的解剖来看，它其实对膝盖有帮助，前提是在膝盖正常的活动范围（range of movement）里做。
Stay within the normal range.Many people think this pose hurts the knees. Anatomically it helps them, as long as you stay within the knee's normal range of movement.
整条前侧链的 extension。拉伸的是从脚背开始的整个身体前侧：大腿前侧、hips 前侧；手臂打开，作用到肩膀前侧；头往后仰，颈椎、甚至额头都在里面。
An extension of the whole front chain.It stretches the whole front of the body, from the tops of the feet: the front thighs, the front of the hips. Open your arms and it reaches the front of the shoulders; tip your head back and it reaches the neck, even the forehead.
注意力放在血海。大多数人像我现在一样，还停留在膝盖。感受血海处没有挤压，体会滋养的感受。
Rest your attention on Xuehai.Most people, like me now, are still working at the knees. Feel that Xuehai is not squeezed, and notice the sense of nourishment there.
膝盖之间的距离。传统教法是两膝之间一个拳头的距离。下去时特别不舒服，可以加到两个、甚至三个拳头。这仍然在健康的活动范围里，不会影响膝盖、髋或大转子。改变的只是长度：你的身体前侧要 travel 的那段长度，和每个人的身体条件有关。
The space between the knees.Traditionally you keep one fist between the knees. If going down feels very uncomfortable, widen it to two or even three fists. You still stay within a healthy range of movement, and nothing strains the knees, the hips or the greater trochanter. You change only the length: how far the front of your body travels, which depends on each body.
饭后可以做。吃完饭我做的第一个练习就是鞍式。我饭后做，感觉肠胃在动。
After meals is fine.Saddle is the first thing I practise after eating. When I do, I feel my gut moving.

两个视角Two views of one line

经络视角

胃经 Stomach channel

从鼻旁起，经面部，上到额角（头维）；再沿脖子前面、胸口、腹部往下，经大腿前侧、膝盖、小腿前外侧，到脚背，止于第二脚趾。几乎就是身体的整条前侧链。

From beside the nose, over the face to the corner of the forehead; then down the front of the neck, chest and belly, the front thigh, knee and shin, to the top of the foot and the second toe.

筋膜视角 · Thomas Myers, Anatomy Trains（第 4 版，2020）

浅前线 Superficial Front Line

从脚趾背面起，经小腿前侧、膝盖、大腿前侧（股四头肌）、腹直肌、胸骨，过脖子前侧的胸锁乳突肌，止于耳后的乳突和头皮筋膜。

From the tops of the toes, up the front shin, knee and thigh, the rectus abdominis and sternum, over the sternocleidomastoid to the mastoid behind the ear.

和胃经大部分重合：脚背、小腿前侧、大腿前侧、腹部、胸口、脖子前侧。到头部分开：浅前线经胸锁乳突肌止于耳后乳突；胃经经过面部到额角。Wei 说"头往后仰，连额头都在里面"，这一点胃经能解释，浅前线解释不了。

The two lines overlap from the top of the foot to the front of the neck, then part at the head: the fascial line ends behind the ear, while the Stomach channel crosses the face to the forehead. "Tip your head back and even the forehead is in it": the channel explains that; the fascial line does not.

和别人对照Compared with

现代老师的说法，只在和我的体会正好一致或正好不同时放进来。 Modern teachers appear only where my own practice agrees or differs.

Bernie Clark 给膝盖定的边界是「零容忍」：一疼就退。我守的是血海：血海不被挤压、感觉是滋养的，我就留着；一有挤压感就退。他给的是一条线，我给的是一个可以感觉的点。
Bernie Clark sets the knee boundary at zero tolerance: back off at any pain. I watch Xuehai: while it feels open, not squeezed, and nourished, I stay; at the first sense of squeezing, I back off. He gives a line; I give a point you can feel.
“zero tolerance for any pain in the knees”
Bernie Clark, “Yin Yoga for the Knees”（Ekhart Yoga 网站） · 读原文 · Read the source
