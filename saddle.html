口令 · Cue

怎么做Practice note

我最 struggle 的体式。我大腿前侧非常紧，所以每天都做。往下躺的时候，躺在抱枕上，减少后弯的幅度。My hardest pose.The fronts of my thighs are very tight, so I do it every day. When I lie back, I lie on a bolster to make the backbend smaller.

在正常的活动范围里做。很多人觉得这个体式伤膝盖。我的做法是：在膝盖正常的活动范围（range of movement）里做。Stay within the normal range.Many people think this pose hurts the knees. My way is to stay within the knee's normal range of movement.

整条前侧链的 extension。拉伸的是从脚背开始的整个身体前侧：大腿前侧、hips 前侧；手臂打开，作用到肩膀前侧；头往后仰，颈椎、甚至额头都在里面。An extension of the whole front chain.It stretches the whole front of the body, from the tops of the feet: the front thighs, the front of the hips. Open your arms and it reaches the front of the shoulders; tip your head back and it reaches the neck, even the forehead.

注意力放在膝盖内侧。感受膝盖内侧没有挤压，体会滋养的感受。Rest your attention on the inner knee.Feel that the inner knee is not squeezed, and notice the sense of nourishment there.

膝盖之间的距离。传统教法是两膝之间一个拳头的距离。下去时特别不舒服，可以加到两个、甚至三个拳头。改变的只是长度：你的身体前侧要 travel 的那段长度，和每个人的身体条件有关。The space between the knees.Traditionally you keep one fist between the knees. If going down feels very uncomfortable, widen it to two or even three fists. You change only the length: how far the front of your body travels, which depends on each body.

饭后可以做。吃完饭我做的第一个练习就是鞍式。我饭后做，感觉肠胃在动。After meals is fine.Saddle is the first thing I practise after eating. When I do, I feel my gut moving.

和别人对照Compared with

现代老师的说法，只在和练习里的体会正好一致或正好不同时放进来。主角是练习本身。

Bernie Clark 给膝盖定的边界是「零容忍」：一疼就退。我守的是膝盖内侧：那里不被挤压、感觉是滋养的，我就留着；一有挤压感就退。他给的是一条线，我给的是一个可以感觉的地方。Bernie Clark sets the knee boundary at zero tolerance: back off at any pain. I watch the inner knee: while it feels open, not squeezed, and nourished, I stay; at the first sense of squeezing, I back off. He gives a line; I give a point you can feel.

Bernie Clark, “Yin Yoga for the Knees”（Ekhart Yoga 网站）“zero tolerance for any pain in the knees”校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：他两侧内侧半月板都撕裂过、做过手术；现在每天用木棍垫在膝盖后面跪坐，对膝盖疼痛零容忍，一疼就退。

用手怎么找
坐着，翘起二郎腿。用对侧的手盖住上面那条腿的膝盖，掌心正对膝盖骨。手自然地盖上去，大拇指根那块肉下面，膝盖骨内侧边上，会摸到一个窝。Sit and cross one leg over the other. Cover the top knee with the opposite hand, palm right over the kneecap. Let it rest there naturally: under the fleshy pad at the base of your thumb, beside the inner edge of the kneecap, you will feel a hollow.

相关经络Channels

脾经Spleen channel

从大脚趾内侧起，沿腿内侧往上，经腹部，到侧胸（大包）。

From the inside of the big toe, up the inside of the leg, through the belly, to the side of the chest (Dabao).
《灵枢·经脉》脾之大络，名曰大包，出渊腋下三寸，布胸胁。

胃经Stomach channel

从鼻旁起，经面部，上到额角（头维）；再沿脖子前面、胸口、腹部往下，经大腿前侧、膝盖、小腿前外侧，到脚背，止于第二脚趾。几乎就是身体的整条前侧链。

From beside the nose, over the face to the corner of the forehead; then down the front of the neck, chest and belly, the front thigh, knee and shin, to the top of the foot and the second toe.

解剖和筋膜Anatomy & fascia

另一个视角，和经络并排放，不合并。重合的地方和分开的地方都写出来。

浅前线Superficial Front Line

从脚趾背面起，经小腿前侧、膝盖、大腿前侧（股四头肌）、腹直肌、胸骨，过脖子前侧的胸锁乳突肌，止于耳后的乳突和头皮筋膜。

From the tops of the toes, up the front shin, knee and thigh, the rectus abdominis and sternum, over the sternocleidomastoid to the mastoid behind the ear.

和胃经大部分重合：脚背、小腿前侧、大腿前侧、腹部、胸口、脖子前侧。到头部分开：浅前线经胸锁乳突肌止于耳后乳突；胃经经过面部到额角。练习时的感觉是"头往后仰，连额头都在里面"，这一点胃经能解释，浅前线解释不了。The two lines overlap from the top of the foot to the front of the neck, then part at the head: the fascial line ends behind the ear, while the Stomach channel crosses the face to the forehead. "Tip your head back and even the forehead is in it": the channel explains that; the fascial line does not.

Thomas Myers, Anatomy Trains（第 4 版，2020）只写线的名字和走向，不复制书里的图。
