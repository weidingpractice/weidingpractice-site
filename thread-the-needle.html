大包

大包 Dabao

SP21 · Great Embracement · 脾经
✓ 在这个体式里感觉到 · felt in this pose
用手怎么找
坐着，左手臂举过头顶。右手手掌横放，手指并拢，食指一边贴着左腋窝正中。往下量两个手掌宽。用中指指肚在那里摸，会摸到两根骨头之间的一道软缝，就是这一片。Sit and raise your left arm over your head. Lay your right hand flat and sideways, fingers together, index-finger edge against the middle of your left armpit. Measure down two hand-widths. Feel there with the pad of your middle finger: a soft groove between two ribs. That is the place.
