涌泉Yongquan · KI1 · Bubbling Spring

用手怎么找How to find it

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。
Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.
照片待拍 · photo to come

不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

肾经 Kidney channel

从小脚趾下面斜走到脚心（涌泉），沿腿内侧往上，经腹部、胸口，止于锁骨下方。

From under the little toe, across to the sole (Yongquan), up the inside of the leg, through the belly and chest, ending below the collarbone.

经典原文Source

肾出于涌泉，涌泉者，足心也。
医经 Medical classics · 《灵枢·本输》
