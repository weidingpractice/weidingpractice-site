涌泉 Yongquan

KI1 · Bubbling Spring · 肾经 Kidney channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

在足底，屈足卷趾时足心最凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.8.1 条，第 20 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.8.1, p. 20 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the sole of the foot, in the deepest depression of the sole when the toes are flexed.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：卧位或伸腿坐位，卷足，约当足底第2、3趾蹼缘与足跟连线的前1/3与后2/3交点凹陷中。The standard's own note: Lying down or sitting with legs straight, curl the toes: roughly in the hollow at the junction of the front third and back two thirds of the line from the web between the 2nd and 3rd toes to the heel.

用手怎么找
脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

在哪些体式里Poses

肾经Kidney channel

从小脚趾下面斜走到脚心（涌泉），沿腿内侧往上，经腹部、胸口，止于锁骨下方。

From under the little toe, across to the sole (Yongquan), up the inside of the leg, through the belly and chest, ending below the collarbone.
《灵枢·经脉》肾足少阴之脉，起于小指之下，邪走足心。
