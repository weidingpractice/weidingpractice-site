涌泉 Yongquan

KI1 · Bubbling Spring · 肾经 Kidney channel

标准定位Standard location

在足底，屈足卷趾时足心最凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

On the sole of the foot, in the deepest depression of the sole when the toes are flexed.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

注：标准原注：卧位或伸腿坐位，卷足，约在足底第 2、3 趾蹼缘与足跟连线的前 1/3 与后 2/3 交点的凹陷中。Note: The Chinese standard adds: lying down or sitting with legs straight, curl the toes; the point is roughly in the hollow one third of the way from the web between the 2nd and 3rd toes to the heel.

用手怎么找
脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
