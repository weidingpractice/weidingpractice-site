下犬式Downward Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana

手指间像夹着一根烟的宽度，力线不断。Fingers apart by the width of a cigarette; the line of force never breaks.

怎么做How I practise it

指间留缝，但缝不大。
Leave a gap between your fingers, but not a wide one.
掌心微含。不用力压平
Palms slightly hollow.Don't press them flat.
一条路从下面上来。气从涌泉起，经腿、腰、脊，一路走到手指尖和掌心。手不主动往外推，而是被这条路上来的力带着伸开——这就是顺抽。
One path rises from below.It starts at Yongquan (KI1), the hollow behind the ball of the foot, and travels up through the legs, the lower back and pelvis (yao in Chinese), and on up the spine, all the way to the fingertips and the palms. The hands don't push out by themselves; what rises along this path carries them open. In tai chi this is shun chou, reeling the silk outward.
回来也是原路。手指和掌心往回吸，力气经手臂、腰、腿，回到涌泉——这就是逆抽。
The way back is the same path.Fingers and palms draw in, and the force returns through the arms, the lower back and pelvis, and the legs to Yongquan. This is ni chou, reeling the silk inward.
关键在“转”，不在“拉”。手上不是直线往外伸，而是带一点旋转，像拧。但往哪个方向拧，没有定数：有时往上推，有时往下推，力一直在调整，很精微。手其实没有动，只是力一直在调整。太注重方向，就教条了。
It's a spiral, not a pull.The hands don't stretch out in a straight line; there is a slight spiral, like a screw. But which way it turns isn't fixed: sometimes you push up, sometimes down, and the force keeps adjusting, very finely. The hands themselves don't move; only the force keeps adjusting. Hold on to a direction and it becomes a rule.
核心是肩窝。怎么知道没有卡住？首先，力是使得上的；然后到肩胛骨这边，不管是上回旋、内回旋还是怎么样，肩窝这块地方是空的，关节不能卡住。感受到肩窝没有被卡住，就可以了。
The key is the shoulder hollow.How do you know nothing is stuck? First, you feel the force flowing through, unbroken, the way a river runs. Then at the shoulder blade, whether it is rotating upward, inward or any other way, the hollow at the front of the shoulder (around Yunmen, LU2) feels empty. The joint isn't jammed. If you can feel the shoulder hollow isn't stuck, that is enough.
力要连续。抽猛了，力就断；抽得太浅，力又出不来。要做到不猛不断，全程连着。
Keep it continuous.Reel too hard and the silk snaps; too lightly and nothing comes. Not forced, not broken, joined all the way through.
早上 7 到 9 点。我把意识放在食指，也就是大肠经，感觉到肠胃在蠕动。
Mornings, 7 to 9.I rest my awareness in the index finger, on the Large Intestine channel, and I feel my gut start to move.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.
