体式 · Pose

下犬式Downward Dog

Adho Mukha Śvānāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →

手指间像夹着一根烟的宽度，力线不断。Fingers apart by the width of a cigarette; the line of force never breaks.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

怎么做How I practise it

指间留缝，但缝不大。
Leave a gap between your fingers, but not a wide one.
掌心微含。不用力压平
Palms slightly hollow.Don't press them flat.
一条路从下面上来。气从涌泉起，经腿、腰、脊，一路走到手指尖和掌心。手不主动往外推，而是被这条路上来的力带着伸开——这就是顺抽。
One path rises from below.It starts at Yongquan (KI1), the hollow behind the ball of the foot, and travels up through the legs, the lower back and pelvis (yao in Chinese), and on up the spine, all the way to the fingertips and the palms. The hands don't push out by themselves; what rises along this path carries them open. In tai chi this is shun chou, reeling the silk outward.
回来也是原路。手指和掌心往回吸，力气经手臂、腰、腿，回到涌泉——这就是逆抽。
The way back is the same path.Fingers and palms draw in, and the force returns through the arms, the lower back and pelvis, and the legs to Yongquan. This is ni chou, reeling the silk inward.
关键在“转”，不在“拉”。手上不是直线往外伸，而是带一点旋转，像拧。但往哪个方向拧，没有定数：有时往上推，有时往下推，力一直在调整，很精微。手其实没有动，只是力一直在调整。太注重方向，就教条了。
It's a spiral, not a pull.The hands don't stretch out in a straight line; there is a slight spiral, like a screw. But which way it turns isn't fixed: sometimes you push up, sometimes down, and the force keeps adjusting, very finely. The hands themselves don't move; only the force keeps adjusting. Hold on to a direction and it becomes a rule.
核心是肩窝。怎么知道没有卡住？首先，力是使得上的；然后到肩胛骨这边，不管是上回旋、内回旋还是怎么样，肩窝这块地方是空的，关节不能卡住。感受到肩窝没有被卡住，就可以了。
The key is the shoulder hollow.How do you know nothing is stuck? First, you feel the force flowing through, unbroken, the way a river runs. Then at the shoulder blade, whether it is rotating upward, inward or any other way, the hollow at the front of the shoulder (around Yunmen, LU2) feels empty. The joint isn't jammed. If you can feel the shoulder hollow isn't stuck, that is enough.
力要连续。抽猛了，力就断；抽得太浅，力又出不来。要做到不猛不断，全程连着。
Keep it continuous.Reel too hard and the silk snaps; too lightly and nothing comes. Not forced, not broken, joined all the way through.
头朝下的时候。视线放在双手之间或垫子中间，不往下看；停留短一点，不憋气，下颌放松：牙齿分开，嘴唇轻合。
With the head down.Look between your hands or at the middle of the mat, not further down. Stay briefly, keep breathing, and soften the jaw: teeth apart, lips lightly closed.
早上 7 到 9 点。我把意识放在食指，也就是大肠经，感觉到肠胃在蠕动。
Mornings, 7 to 9.I rest my attention in the index finger, on the Large Intestine channel, and I feel my gut start to move.

和别人对照Compared with

现代老师的说法，只在和我的体会正好一致或正好不同时放进来。 Modern teachers appear only where my own practice agrees or differs.

研究：头朝下时眼压会升高，下犬式升得最多。（我们的提醒，不是研究的结论：有青光眼或眼压高的人，停留要短。）
Research: eye pressure rises with the head down, most of all in Downward Dog. (Our note, not the study's: if you have glaucoma or high eye pressure, keep the hold short.)
10 位青光眼患者和 10 位健康人，下犬式、站立前屈、犁式、靠墙抬腿各停 2 分钟。四个体式里，眼压都在 1 分钟内明显升高；下犬式升得最多（健康人平均从 17 升到 29 mmHg，青光眼患者从 17 升到 28 mmHg）。坐起来后 2 分钟内回到原来的水平。
Jasien, Jonas, de Moraes, Ritch（纽约西奈山医院）· PLOS ONE 2015;10(12):e0144505 · 读原文 · Read the source
田兆麟讲方向，最关键这一句：
Tian Zhaolin on direction, the key line:
按抽丝二字，非仅指缓徐直拉而出……其实抽丝二字，乃指旋转出入，如来复线之螺旋形也，出为顺抽，入为逆抽，一切运劲，概不外乎此顺逆二抽。

"Reeling silk" doesn't only mean pulling straight out, slowly ... it means turning in and out, in a spiral like the rifling of a gun barrel: out is reeling outward, in is reeling inward.

田兆麟 · 出处待核 · source to be checked
田兆麟讲路线：
Tian Zhaolin on the path:
其顺抽乃由足至于手指之抽，其逆抽乃由手指回归足之抽。

Reeling outward runs from the feet to the fingers; reeling inward runs from the fingers back to the feet.

田兆麟 · 出处待核 · source to be checked
吴图南讲分寸：
Wu Tunan on how much:
太极拳运劲就同抽丝一样，如抽猛了，丝就断了；抽得不合适，丝又抽不出来……不能够过强，也不能过弱，要恰如其分。

Moving force in tai chi is just like reeling silk: reel too hard and the silk snaps; reel wrongly and it won't come out ... not too strong, not too weak, just right.

吴图南 · 出处待核 · source to be checked
黄震寰讲手上的具体状态：
Huang Zhenhuan on what the hands do:
两上肢沉肩坠肘，竖掌，劳宫朝前……两手随气之鼓荡旋转而伸开，表现为吐、为放、为开、为阳，此为顺抽丝。

Both arms: sink the shoulders, drop the elbows, palms upright, Laogong facing forward ... the hands turn and open with the surge of qi: this is breathing out, releasing, opening, yang. This is reeling outward.

黄震寰 · 出处待核 · source to be checked
收回来的时候：
And on the way back:
两手随气之鼓荡而回吸，表现为吞、为收、为合、为阴，此为逆抽丝。

The hands draw back with the surge of qi: this is taking in, gathering, closing, yin. This is reeling inward.
