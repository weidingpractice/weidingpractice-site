丁玮（Wei Ding），住在伦敦的瑜伽练习者。在 Austin Ince 的阴瑜伽师资培训里讲中医入门。这个网站是她的练习地图：练习时注意力放在哪个穴位，先用手找，再到垫子上感觉，不用按。Wei Ding (丁玮) is a yoga practitioner in London. She teaches the Introduction to Traditional Chinese Medicine on Austin Ince's Yin Yoga Teacher Training. This site is her practice map: where attention rests in each pose, found first by hand and then felt on the mat, never pressed.

她不是医生，也不是执业中医师，不提供诊断或治疗。She is not a doctor or a licensed practitioner of Chinese medicine, and offers no diagnosis or treatment.
