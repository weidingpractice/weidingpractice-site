風池

风池 Fengchi

GB20 · Wind Pool · 胆经 Gallbladder channel

标准定位Standard location

在项部，枕骨之下，胸锁乳突肌上端与斜方肌上端之间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

In the posterior region of the neck, inferior to the occipital bone, in the depression between the origins of the sternocleidomastoid and the trapezius muscles.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

注：中文是 GB/T 12346-2021 第 3.6.20 条（基准穴点）的逐字原文，写「在项部」（A，11/18 核对原文）。第 5 章的正式条目 5.11.20 还没读到，电子版正文在 5.7.39 截断，要看纸本。项专指脖子后面（《说文》：「项，头后也。」）。英文是 WHO 2008 标准的 posterior region of the neck，原文页码待核。Note: The Chinese is verbatim from GB/T 12346-2021, clause 3.6.20 (reference points): "on the nape". The formal entry in chapter 5 (5.11.20) has not been read yet; the available electronic text stops at 5.7.39. The nape (项) means the back of the neck only. The English is from the WHO 2008 standard; page reference still to be checked.

用手怎么找
双手十指交叉抱住后脑勺，两个拇指放在后脑勺最下沿，从中间往两边慢慢滑。大约滑到耳朵和脖子正中的一半，拇指落进一个凹窝，左右各一个。Interlace your fingers and cradle the back of your head, thumbs on the lower edge of the skull. Slide them slowly outwards from the middle. About halfway between the ear and the middle of the neck, each thumb drops into a hollow, one on each side.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

原文Classical source

最早写出风池位置的条文。没有提任何肌肉，只说在发际的凹陷里；还给了古书里唯一的手感：按下去，感觉往耳朵里走。The earliest text to locate Fengchi. It names no muscles, only a hollow at the hairline, and gives the one tactile sign in the old texts: press it and the feeling draws into the ear.

《针灸甲乙经》卷三·头自发际中央旁行凡五穴第六风池，在颞颥后发际陷者中，足少阳、阳维之会。……《气府论》注云：在后陷者中，按之引耳，手、足少阳脉之会。连着 · Linked to：风池 GB20

From the outer corner of the eye, around the side of the head to the back of the skull (Fengchi), down the side of the body, through the hip (Huantiao), to the fourth toe.
