风池Fengchi · GB20 · Wind Pool

标准定位Standard location

在项部，枕骨之下，胸锁乳突肌上端与斜方肌上端之间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》，已对原文

In the anterior region of the neck, inferior to the occipital bone, in the depression between the origins of sternocleidomastoid and the trapezius muscles.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

注：GB/T 12346-2021 写「在项部」，就是脖子后面；WHO 英文写 anterior region of the neck（颈前区），因为 WHO 按斜方肌前缘分前后，风池在斜方肌前缘之前。两份标准用的分区说法不同，指的是同一个点。标准原注：项部枕骨下两侧，横平风府（GV16），胸锁乳突肌与斜方肌两肌之间凹陷中。Note: GB/T 12346-2021 says "on the nape" (the back of the neck). The WHO English says "in the anterior region of the neck", because WHO divides front from back at the front border of trapezius, and Fengchi lies in front of that border. The two standards name the region differently; the point is the same. The Chinese standard adds: on both sides below the occipital bone, level with Fengfu (GV16), in the hollow between sternocleidomastoid and trapezius.

用手怎么找How to find it

双手十指交叉抱住后脑勺，两个拇指放在后脑勺最下沿，从中间往两边慢慢滑。大约滑到耳朵和脖子正中的一半，拇指落进一个凹窝，左右各一个。
Interlace your fingers and cradle the back of your head, thumbs on the lower edge of the skull. Slide them slowly outwards from the middle. About halfway between the ear and the middle of the neck, each thumb drops into a hollow, one on each side.

不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 You don't press; you rest your attention there.

胆经 Gallbladder channel

从外眼角起，绕头侧到后脑（风池），沿身体侧面往下，经髋（环跳），到第四脚趾。

From the outer corner of the eye, around the side of the head to the back of the skull (Fengchi), down the side of the body, through the hip (Huantiao), to the fourth toe.
