SP10 · Sea of Blood · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

在股前内侧，髌底内侧端上 2 寸，股内侧肌隆起处。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

On the anteromedial aspect of the thigh, on the bulge of the vastus medialis muscle, 2 B-cun superior to the medial end of the base of the patella.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text

用手怎么找
坐着弯膝。右手掌盖住左膝盖骨，拇指斜向大腿内侧，拇指尖落下的地方。Seated, knee bent: cup your right palm over your left kneecap; the tip of your thumb points to it on the inner thigh.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

名字里的意The image in the name

草稿，待 Wei 确认措辞

血海 血，和海。海是百川归去的地方：深，满，容得下。血海，就是血汇成海的地方。Sea of Blood Blood, and the sea. The sea is where all rivers go: deep, full, able to hold. Xuehai is where the blood gathers into a sea.

怎么用 练的时候，让大腿内侧这一片饱满、开阔，像潮水慢慢涨上来，不被挤压。鞍式里那句「感受血海处没有挤压，体会滋养的感受」，用的就是这个画面。In practice In practice, let this stretch of the inner thigh feel full and open, like a tide slowly rising, never squeezed. The Saddle cue "feel that Xuehai is not squeezed, and notice the sense of nourishment there" works from this picture.
