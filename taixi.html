太溪 Taixi

KI3 · Great Stream · 肾经 Kidney channel

标准定位Standard location

在踝后内侧，内踝尖与跟腱之间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

On the posteromedial aspect of the ankle, in the depression between the prominence of the medial malleolus and the calcaneal tendon.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text
