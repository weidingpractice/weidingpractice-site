Utkatasana

吸气从下丹田沿脊柱往上。Breathe in from the lower dantian and up along the spine.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

丹
丹田 Dantian
Area
有体会 · felt

手掌横放在肚脐下面，掌心对着小腹。手掌盖住的这一片，就是下丹田。它不是一个点，是一片区域。

Lay your palm across your belly just below the navel. The area under your hand is the lower dantian. It is an area, not a point.

气
气海 Qihai
CV6
有体会 · felt

食指和中指并拢，放在肚脐正下方，两指的下沿。

Two fingers together just below the navel: the point is at their lower edge.

经典原文Sources

原文不翻译。每条前面一句英文说明它讲的是什么。 The classical texts stay in Chinese; the line above each says what it is about.

起点：脐下肾间动气，就是后世所说的下丹田一带。《难经》说它是十二经的根本：
The starting point: the moving qi between the kidneys below the navel, the area later called the lower dantian. The Classic of Difficulties calls it the root of the twelve channels:
脐下肾间动气者，人之生命也，十二经之根本也，故名曰原。
医经 Medical classics · 《难经·六十六难》
路线：督脉从下极出发，沿脊柱里面往上：
The route: the Governing Vessel starts at the base and rises inside the spine:
督脉者，起于下极之俞，并于脊里，上至风府，入属于脑。
医经 Medical classics · 《难经·二十八难》
太极拳的说法：力从脊柱发出：
In tai chi: force issues from the spine:
力由脊发，步随身换。
拳经 Taiji classics · 武禹襄《十三势行功心解》
