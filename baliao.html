八髎

骶部 · 八髎 Baliao

BL31–34 · 膀胱经 Bladder channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

上髎：在骶部，正对第1骶后孔中。次髎：在骶部，第2骶后孔中。中髎：在骶部，正对第3骶后孔中。下髎：在骶部，正对第4骶后孔中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.7.31–5.7.34 条，第 18 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.7.31–5.7.34, p. 18 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

Shangliao, Ciliao, Zhongliao and Xialiao (BL31–BL34), one on each side: in the sacral region, in the first, second, third and fourth posterior sacral foramen respectively.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

我们的说明：分区词「骶部」是国标自己的用词，见附录 B 表 B.1（第 32 页）。四个穴左右各一，共八穴。每个穴另有一条取穴的注，见下面「这一片里的标准穴位」。Our note: "Sacral region" (骶部) is the standard's own regional term (Annex B, table B.1, p. 32). Each of the four points is on both sides, eight in all. Each point also has a short note on how to find it; see the list of standard points below.

这是什么What it is

是一片区域，练的时候也按一片来感受 八髎是左右四对骶后孔，但孔藏在骨头里，体表摸不到孔口，只能摸到骨面上的凹陷。练习里感受和动作都是一整片，不是一个孔一个孔。下面的标准穴位只作参照。An area, and felt as one The eight crevices are four pairs of posterior sacral foramina, but the openings lie inside the bone and cannot be felt from the surface. In practice the sensation and the movement come as one area, not hole by hole. The standard points below are for reference only.

能摸到的骨 髂后上棘：骨盆后面最突出的两个骨尖，左右各一。骶正中嵴：骶骨背面正中一条纵向骨嵴。骶角：最下面两个像小豆子的突起，中间的凹陷是骶管裂孔。国标的说法是：「骶管裂孔：取尾骨上方左右的骶角，与左右骶角平齐的后正中线上。」（GB/T 12346-2021 4.2.1，第 5 页，A）骶管裂孔中心与两侧髂后上棘连线，大致是一个等边三角形（《实用疼痛学》等转述，B，原书待核）。骶管裂孔的形状因人而异，摸不到不等于找错了。Bones you can feel Posterior superior iliac spines: the two most prominent bony points at the back of the pelvis. Median sacral crest: the vertical ridge down the middle of the sacrum. Sacral cornua: the two small bumps at the bottom, with the sacral hiatus in the hollow between them. The Chinese national standard puts the sacral hiatus on the midline, level with the two sacral cornua above the tailbone (GB/T 12346-2021, 4.2.1, p. 5). The hiatus and the two iliac spines roughly make an equilateral triangle. Its shape varies from person to person, so not finding it does not mean you are in the wrong place.

《素问·骨空论》 「……八髎在腰尻分间。」（节引，三个整理本一致，A）腰尻就是骶骨；「分间」是骨间的孔隙。经文说的是一片骨间孔隙；把它讲成上、次、中、下左右八个穴，是后世注文的说法，不是经文。Suwen, chapter 60 The classical line (quoted in part) places the eight crevices in the gaps of the sacrum: one stretch of gaps between the bones. Reading it as eight separate points comes from later commentary, not from the classic itself.

「尾闾」有三种指法 一、长强穴的别名（尾骨端）；二、骶骨加尾骨整段（武术常用）；三、只指能动的尾骨（讲「转尾闾」时）。用这个词时先想清楚说的是哪一种。Three meanings of weilü 1. Another name for Changqiang, at the tip of the tailbone; 2. the whole of sacrum and tailbone, as martial arts usually mean it; 3. only the movable tailbone, when people speak of turning the weilü.

用手怎么找
站着，双手叉腰，拇指在后。拇指往后、往中间滑，碰到脊柱。拇指不动，手掌往下翻，贴着后腰。中指往下的这一片就是骶部，八髎都在里面。Stand with hands on hips, thumbs behind. Slide the thumbs back and in until they meet the spine. Keep the thumbs there and turn the palms down onto the lower back. The area under your middle fingers and below is the sacrum; the eight Baliao points are all in it.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
