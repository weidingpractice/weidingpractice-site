八髎Baliao · BL31–34 · Eight Crevices

用手怎么找How to find it

手放在后腰最下面、屁股正上方那块平平的三角形骨头上，左右各有四个小窝，一共八个。
Hand on the flat triangular bone just above the buttocks: four small hollows on each side, eight in all.

不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

膀胱经 Bladder channel

从内眼角起，上头顶，沿脊柱两侧往下，经骶骨（八髎），沿腿后面到小脚趾外侧。

From the inner corner of the eye, over the crown, down both sides of the spine, through the sacrum (Baliao), down the back of the leg to the outer edge of the little toe.
