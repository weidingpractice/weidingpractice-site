体式名称对照（梵文 · 英文 · 中文）→Pose names in Sanskrit, English and Chinese

按跟地面的关系排，从下往上读：着地越多越阴，着地越少越阳。一个人也是这样，从躺着、坐着、爬着，长到站起来。Arranged by how the body meets the floor; read from the bottom up. The more of you touches the floor, the more yin; the less, the more yang. It is also how a person grows: lying, sitting, crawling, then standing.

同一个形，练法不同，就是阴或阳。亮着的是已经写了的；灰色的还在长。穴位标签实线是有体会，虚线是可能。The same shape becomes yin or yang by how you practise it. Lit poses are written; grey ones are still growing. Solid point tags are felt; dashed ones are possible.
