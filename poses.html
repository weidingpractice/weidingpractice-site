同一个体式，往阴，往阳One pose, toward yin or toward yang

上面的梯子是换体式。这里是不换体式，只改一处，它就变阴或变阳。往阳是多一个要求，往阴是少一个。 · The ladder above changes the pose. Here the pose stays the same; change one thing and it turns more yin or more yang. Toward yang adds a demand; toward yin takes one away.

着地Contact

往阴 · Yin树式：抬起的脚，脚尖点地Tree: the lifted foot rests its toes on the floor

往阳 · Yang树式：抬起的脚完全离地Tree: the lifted foot leaves the floor

手臂Arms

往阴 · Yin树式：双手合掌在胸前Tree: palms together at the chest

往阳 · Yang树式：双手举过头顶Tree: arms raised overhead

多一个动作One more action

往阴 · Yin战士三：回到树式Warrior 3: come back to Tree

往阳 · Yang树式：身体前倾、后腿往后伸，成了战士三Tree: tip forward and reach the back leg out; now it is Warrior 3

脊柱Spine

往阴 · Yin猫牛式：拱背，是猫Cat-cow: round the back, the cat

往阳 · Yang猫牛式：塌腰抬头，是牛Cat-cow: drop the belly and lift the head, the cow

辅具Props

往阴 · Yin婴儿式：额头放在砖或抱枕上Child's pose: forehead on a block or bolster

往阳 · Yang婴儿式：拿掉砖Child's pose: take the block away

意Intention

往阴 · Yin高弓步：停在那里，放松High lunge: stay, and let go

往阳 · Yang高弓步：停在那里，后腿用力；或者动起来High lunge: stay and firm the back leg; or keep moving

往阳，路会分叉；往阴，路会收拢，回到地面。Toward yang the paths branch; toward yin they gather and return to the floor.
