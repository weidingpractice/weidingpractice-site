体式Poses

每个体式里，注意力放在哪几个穴位。Which points to rest your attention on, pose by pose.

唱诵Chanting

中指放在劳宫，声音立刻变得柔和、轻。

Rest the middle finger on Laogong, and the voice turns soft and light at once.

劳宫

三段道家呼吸Three-part Daoist Breath

一吸一呼，带着手臂、头和背一起动；做 2 分钟或 20 分钟都可以。

Each breath in and out moves the arms, head and back together; it works for two minutes or twenty.

手腕绕环Wrist circles

注意力放在掌心，不放在十个手指上。

Rest your attention in the palm, not on all ten fingers.

劳宫

猫牛式Cat–Cow

命门

四足摆髋 → 绕臀Hip sways → circles

把觉知放在两个半球的当中，很深的那个点。

Rest your attention deep in the centre of each half of the buttocks.

胞肓、秩边

穿针式Thread the Needle

让侧肋那一片在动作里铺开。

Let the side ribs spread out as you move.

大包

狮身人面式 → 海豹式（转头变体）Sphinx → Seal, with head turns

把觉知放在风池穴，转头不害怕了，而且幅度自然，更 smooth。

Rest your attention on Fengchi: turning the head stops being frightening, and the range comes naturally and more smoothly.

风池、曲骨、八髎

下犬式Downward Dog

手指间像夹着一根烟的宽度，力线不断。

Fingers apart by the width of a cigarette; the line of force never breaks.

劳宫、涌泉、云门、胯根、商阳

四柱支撑Chaturanga

手指放在地上就可以，注意力放在劳宫的力线上。

Just place your fingers on the floor and rest your attention on the line from Laogong.

劳宫、云门、胯根、中府、液门

高弓步High Lunge

进入时守环跳，后腿守太溪。

Going in, hold Huantiao; in the back leg, hold Taixi.

环跳、胯根、太溪、涌泉

合掌扭转Prayer Twist

一只手的劳宫贴住另一只手的液门。不用转那么深，呼吸很好。

Press the Laogong of one hand against the Yemen of the other. No need to twist so deep; the breath is good.

中焦、劳宫、液门

战士二、三角式Warrior II, Triangle

留意臀部外后方的那个窝。

Notice the hollow at the back and outside of the hip.

环跳

三角扭转式Revolved Triangle

劳宫放在骶骨上，和前面的肚脐成对。

Rest Laogong on the sacrum and pair it with the navel in front.

劳宫、八髎、神阙、命门

侧蹲转换Warrior II → Skandasana

转臀下去，把骶骨这块后侧区域拉开。

Turn the hips as you lower, spreading the back of the sacrum.

Yongquan, Taixi, Xuehai, then the front of the groin.

涌泉、太溪、血海、胯根、上焦、冲门、气冲

幻椅式Chair

吸气从下丹田沿脊柱往上。

Breathe in from the lower dantian and up along the spine.

丹田、气海

鹰式手臂缠绕Eagle arms

不是掌心相对：下方的手按住上方手的鱼际。

Instead of pressing palm to palm, let the lower hand press the Yuji point of the upper hand.

鱼际

战士三、扭转半月Warrior III, Revolved Half Moon

支撑脚守涌泉，后腿守太溪，两个点同时在。

Hold Yongquan in the standing foot and Taixi in the back leg, both at once.

树式Tree

The sole is water, the inner thigh is earth, the shoulder hollow is metal.

Feel that Xuehai is not squeezed, and notice the sense of nourishment there.

双手搓热，掌心劳宫轻轻盖在闭着的眼睛上，停一分钟，不压。

Rub your palms warm and rest Laogong lightly over your closed eyes for a minute, without pressing.
