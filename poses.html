练习原则Working principles

先说用的是哪张图。Say which map you are using. ○ 待验证

同一片身体，在不同的图上有不同的名字。讲到一个部位，先说用的是哪张图：穴位图（气海、关元）、内丹图（丹田、小周天）、三焦图（下焦）。两张图的形容词不互借：「聚、充实、气足」是丹田的词；「通、不通、顺、堵、壅」是三焦的词。虚实寒热两边都用，不能拿来分。同一个「满」字，丹田满是练出来的，下焦胀满是病。不说「下焦饱满」，也不说「丹田通畅」。还有一张图要单独标：王唯工的脉波谐波是一个物理模型。从它推出来的话，比如「上焦属胆经」「奇经八脉属三焦经」，只能写成他的推论，不能当中医常识。

One stretch of the body carries different names on different maps. When you name a place, first say which map you mean: the acupoint map (Qihai, Guanyuan), the inner-alchemy map (dantian, Microcosmic Orbit), or the Sanjiao map (lower jiao). The maps don't share adjectives. Gathered, filled and abundant belong to the dantian; flowing, blocked, smooth, stuck and congested belong to the Sanjiao. Both maps use empty and full, cold and hot, so those words can't tell them apart. The same word full means progress in the dantian and illness in the lower jiao. Never call the lower jiao full, and never call the dantian unblocked. One more map needs its own label: Wang Wei-kung's pulse harmonics are a physical model. Claims drawn from it, such as "the upper jiao belongs to the Gallbladder channel" or "the eight extraordinary vessels belong to the Triple Burner channel", are his inferences, never common TCM knowledge.

穴位的名字就是口令。The point's name is the cue. ✓ 有体会

穴位名字是意象，不是编号。中国人读到「血海」两个字，身体就知道那里该是饱满、开阔、不被挤压的，练习就能做对；换成 SP10，什么都不会发生。就像「枯藤老树昏鸦，小桥流水人家」，几个意象并排放，意境就出来了。树式里「脚心是水，大腿内侧是土，肩窝是金」也是这样：意象并置，身体自己找对。这是这套系统对懂中文的人格外奇妙的地方。

A point name is an image, not a number. Reading the two characters of Xuehai, "Sea of Blood", a Chinese speaker's body knows that place should feel full, open and unsqueezed, and the practice comes out right; SP10 does nothing. It works like classical Chinese poetry, where images set side by side create the mood. The Tree cue does the same: the sole is water, the inner thigh is earth, the shoulder hollow is metal. For readers of Chinese, this is what makes the system uncanny. In English, the translated names (Sea of Blood, Gushing Spring) carry part of the image, so the site keeps them.

练和教用意象，记录用解剖语言。Practise and teach with images; record in anatomical terms. ✓ 有体会

意和形分开。意是你做的：觉知放在哪个点，加一个意向（转、沉、延伸，或者只是放着）。形是身体实际怎么动的：用 Austin 讲义的词描述（平面、方向、动作）。告诉身体往哪个方向转，对我无效：髋可以前倾、后倾、旋转、平移，真实的转动是几个平面一起动一点，脑子算不出来。把意放在臀部正中那个点，想着转，身体自己就会配好，跟幅度、身体条件和腿的位置都有关。所以解剖语言用来事后描述和画图，不用来指挥身体。

Keep intent and form apart. Intent is what you do: where your attention rests, plus an intention (turn, sink, lengthen, or simply rest there). Form is how the body actually moved, described afterwards in the terms of Austin's anatomy handbook: plane, direction, movement. Telling my body which direction to turn does not work for me: the hip can tilt forward or back, rotate and shift, and a real turn mixes several planes at once, more than the mind can compute. Rest the intent on the centre point of the buttock, think "turn", and the body sorts it out by itself, depending on range, build and where the legs are. So anatomical language describes and maps; it does not command.

转得深还是浅，跟身体条件、练习时间长短有关。我们练的永远不是幅度，而是觉知和呼吸的深度。

How deep you go depends on your body and how long you have practised. You never practise range; you practise awareness and the depth of the breath.

注意力点要成对。Attention points come in pairs. ✓ 有体会

要做好 alignment，就把注意力放在成对的两个点上。左右成对：两条腿，屁股两边，肩胛骨两边，手两边。前后也成对：劳宫放在骶骨上，和前面的肚脐成对，呼吸时前后就对上了。只放一个点，身体还是找不到 alignment。内外也成对（11-12）：同一条腿上，大腿根内侧的窝（胯根，inner groin）和外侧的环跳成对，是窝和内外的对称。

To find alignment, rest your attention on points that come in pairs. Left and right: two legs, two sides of the buttocks, two shoulder blades, two hands. Front and back too: rest Laogong on the sacrum and pair it with the navel, and as you breathe, front and back line up. With one point, the body still can't find its alignment. Inside and outside pair up too (12 Nov): on the same leg, the hollow at the inner groin pairs with Huantiao on the outside, a hollow matched across the hip.

臀部是可以转的，而且一定要转。大多数人的 transition 不转臀部，这是个很大的问题。两个提示：高弓步到战士二；战士二到 Skandasana。

The hips can turn, and they must. Most people keep the hips still in transitions, and that is a big problem. Two cues: High Lunge to Warrior II; Warrior II to Skandasana.

上肢力线：劳宫 + 肩窝。只看肩窝在不在、有没有被挤压，不用管手指和肩胛骨怎么转。用力的时候，呼吸穿过肩窝，不停：推起来时深吸气，不憋气（11-13）。The arm line: Laogong and the shoulder hollow. Only check that the hollow is there and not squeezed; you needn't think about the fingers or how the shoulder blades rotate. When you push, let the breath pass through the shoulder hollow and keep it moving: breathe in deeply as you push up, never hold it (13 Nov).
