大包 Dabao

SP21 · Great Embracement · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

在侧胸部，第6肋间隙，腋中线上。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.4.21 条，第 14 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.4.21, p. 14 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the lateral thoracic region, in the sixth intercostal space, on the midaxillary line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：侧卧举臂，在第6肋间隙与腋中线的交点处。The standard's own note: Lying on the side with the arm raised: where the sixth intercostal space meets the midaxillary line.

用手怎么找
坐着，左手臂举过头顶。右手手掌横放，手指并拢，食指一边贴着左腋窝正中。往下量两个手掌宽。用中指指肚在那里摸，会摸到两根骨头之间的一道软缝，就是这一片。Sit and raise your left arm over your head. Lay your right hand flat and sideways, fingers together, index-finger edge against the middle of your left armpit. Measure down two hand-widths. Feel there with the pad of your middle finger: a soft groove between two ribs. That is the place.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.找到了吗？Did you find it?
