太白

太白 Taibai

SP3 · Supreme White · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

在足内侧，第 1 跖趾关节近端赤白肉际凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

On the medial aspect of the foot, in the depression proximal to the first metatarsophalangeal joint, at the border between the red and white flesh.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008)

注：GB/T 12346-2021 第 5.4.3 条，原文逐字核对（A 级）。足太阴脾经输穴、原穴。Note: GB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.4.3, checked word for word (grade A). Stream point and source point of the spleen channel.

用手怎么找
脚内侧，大脚趾根部往后摸到一块突出的骨头（第 1 跖骨头），就在它后面一点，脚底和脚背皮肤颜色交界的凹陷里。On the inside of the foot: feel back from the base of the big toe to the bony bump (the head of the first metatarsal); just behind it, in the hollow where the skin of the sole meets the skin of the top of the foot.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
