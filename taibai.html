太白

太白 Taibai

SP3 · Supreme White · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

在足内侧，第1跖趾关节近端赤白肉际凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.4.3 条，第 13 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.4.3, p. 13 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the medial aspect of the foot, in the depression proximal to the first metatarsophalangeal joint, at the border between the red and white flesh.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
坐着，看脚内侧。大脚趾根部后面有一块鼓起的骨头。从这块骨头往脚跟方向摸，刚过骨头就有一个小窝，在脚底和脚背颜色交界的那条边上。Sit and look at the inside of your foot. Behind the base of the big toe is a bony bump. Feel from it toward the heel: just past the bone is a small hollow, on the line where the colour of the sole meets the colour of the top of the foot.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

脾经Spleen channel

从大脚趾内侧起，沿腿内侧往上，经腹部，到侧胸（大包）。

From the inside of the big toe, up the inside of the leg, through the belly, to the side of the chest (Dabao).
《灵枢·经脉》脾之大络，名曰大包，出渊腋下三寸，布胸胁。
