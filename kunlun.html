昆仑 Kunlun

BL60 · 膀胱经 Bladder channel地图 · Map：医经 Medical classics

标准定位Standard location

在踝后外侧，外踝尖与跟腱之间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.7.60 条，第 20 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.7.60, p. 20 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the back and outer side of the ankle, in the hollow between the tip of the outer ankle bone and the Achilles tendon.Our own translation of the Chinese standard above.

用手怎么找
坐着，一只脚踩地。用同侧手的食指，放在脚踝外侧凸出来的那块骨头上，找到它最鼓的那一点，往脚后跟的方向滑。滑过骨头以后，指头会掉进骨头和后面那根粗筋中间的小坑。差不多就是昆仑。Sit with one foot on the floor. With the index finger of the hand on the same side, find the bone that sticks out on the outside of the ankle, and its most prominent point. Slide towards the heel. Once past the bone, your finger drops into a small hollow between the bone and the thick cord behind it. That is roughly Kunlun.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

膀胱经Bladder channel

从内眼角起，上头顶，沿脊柱两侧往下，经骶骨（八髎），沿腿后面到小脚趾外侧。

From the inner corner of the eye, over the crown, down both sides of the spine, through the sacrum (Baliao), down the back of the leg to the outer edge of the little toe.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.

这里记录的是练习时注意力放在哪里，不是诊断，也不是治疗。有伤病或疼痛，请先咨询医生。

These notes are about where attention goes in practice. They are not diagnosis or treatment. If you have an injury or pain, see a doctor first.
