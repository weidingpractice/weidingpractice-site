四足四向手位Four hand positions on all fours

四足，手指朝前、朝外、朝膝盖、两手指尖相对。手放好不动，转的是肩膀和骨盆。On all fours, set the fingers forward, then out, then toward the knees, then fingertips facing each other. The hands stay put; what circles is the shoulders and the pelvis.

形 · 2 跪 · 四足 · 膝或胫骨，加手或前臂Shape · 2 Kneeling & all fours · Knees or shins, with hands or forearms

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

劳宫 Laogong
PC8
有体会 · felt

轻轻握拳，中指尖碰到掌心的地方。

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

怎么做How I practise it

不是手腕在绕环。手放好就不动。每换一个手的方向，肩膀和骨盆在上面转圈，手腕只是被带着换了角度。
It isn't the wrists circling.Once the hands are placed, they stay. With each new hand direction, the shoulders and pelvis circle above them; the wrists only change angle because of it.
注意力放在掌心。放在劳宫，不放在十个手指上。
Attention in the palm.On Laogong, not on all ten fingers.
