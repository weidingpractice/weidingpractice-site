战士三、扭转半月Warrior III, Revolved Half Moon

Vīrabhadrāsana III · Parivṛtta Ardha Candrāsana

支撑脚守涌泉，后腿守太溪，两个点同时在。Hold Yongquan in the standing foot and Taixi in the back leg, both at once.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

胯
胯根 Kua root
Area
有体会 · felt

大腿根前内侧、腹股沟折痕下面凹下去的窝。只放注意力，不用力按。

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease. Rest your attention; don't press.

涌
涌泉 Yongquan
KI1
有体会 · felt

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

太
太溪 Taixi
KI3
有体会 · felt

脚腕内侧有一块凸起的骨头。从它往脚后跟方向摸，碰到那根粗粗的筋之前，中间有个小窝。

From the bump on the inside of your ankle, slide back towards the heel: the hollow before the thick cord.
