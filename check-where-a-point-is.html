穴位在哪里：国标怎么说

Where a point is: what the national standard says · 2026-11-23 · v1

本文为出处核查，不评价疗效，不提供诊疗建议。 This is a source check. It makes no claim about effects and gives no medical advice.

这份标准 · The standard

GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》，中国推荐性国家标准。2021 年 11 月 26 日发布并实施，由中国中医科学院针灸研究所起草，代替 2006 年版。标准自述适用于针灸教学、科研、医疗、出版和学术交流。引言说，这次修订是为了与世界卫生组织西太区标准《针灸经穴定位》「相协调」。

GB/T 12346-2021, Nomenclature and location of meridian points, a recommended national standard of China. Issued and in force on 26 November 2021, drafted by the Institute of Acupuncture and Moxibustion, China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences, replacing the 2006 edition. It states it is for teaching, research, medical practice, publishing and academic exchange. Its introduction says the revision was made to be harmonised with the WHO Western Pacific standard.

标准原文 · What the standard says

页码照正式发布本。英文是我们的翻译。Page numbers follow the official text. English is our translation.

1. 不能用固定长度，只能按比例。（引言，第 IV 页）

「人体高矮胖瘦的差异很大，无法采用绝对的标准值描述针灸腧穴部位，只有通过等分折量的方法——骨度折量法描述腧穴部位，才能适用于所有人群和所有个体。」

People differ so much in height and build that point locations cannot be given in absolute values; only proportional division can apply to all people and every individual.

2. 指寸只作配合，用的是本人的手指。（4.2.3，第 6 页；3.3.2，第 1 页）

「主要采用体表解剖标志定位法、"骨度"折量定位法，而对少量难以完全采用上述两种方法定位的腧穴，则配合使用"指寸"定位法。」指寸依据「被取穴者本人」的手指。

Landmarks and proportional measurement are the main methods; finger-widths are added only for the few points those two cannot fully locate. The finger used is the person's own.

3. 方法冲突时，标志优先。（4.1，第 5 页；适用于古书描述不明确时）

「体表解剖标志定位法与"指寸"定位法不吻合时，优先考虑体表解剖标志定位法。」

Where the classical description is unclear and landmarks disagree with finger-widths, landmarks take precedence.

4. 标志是人人看得见、摸得到的。（4.2.1，第 5 页）

固定标志：「由骨节和肌肉所形成的突起或凹陷、五官轮郭、发际、指（趾）甲、乳头、脐窝等」；活动标志：关节、肌肉、肌腱、皮肤随着活动而出现的空隙、凹陷、皱纹、尖端。

Fixed landmarks are bony and muscular prominences or hollows, the outline of the features, hairline, nails, nipples and navel; moving landmarks are the gaps, hollows, creases and tips that appear when joints, muscles, tendons and skin move.

5. 个体差异写进了标准。（4.4.4，第 8 页）

每个穴位的「注」要说明「性别体表标志与个体差异情况」。

The notes to each point are to explain landmarks that differ by sex, and individual variation.

6. 定位和怎么找，分开写。（4.4.3，第 7 页）

「腧穴定位的表述中不涉及"取穴法"内容」，取穴法放在注文里说明。

The location text does not include how to find the point; that goes in the notes.

这份标准没有说的 · What it does not say

  • 它没有说穴位是一个范围：定义里写的是「特定部位」（3.1），但表述要求「两线相交定一点」（4.4.1）。
  • 它没有用手摸着找穴的条文。
  • 它和 WHO 标准是「相协调」，不是每个定位都一样。

It does not call a point an area (it defines a point as a "specific site" but asks for two crossing lines to fix one point); it has no clause on finding points by touch; and it is harmonised with the WHO standard, not identical to it.

研究怎么说 · The research

一篇系统综述（Godson & Wardle，2019，doi:10.1016/j.jams.2018.10.009）发现：有资格的医生针灸师找同一个穴位，位置差别相当大，和受训时长、年资、流派都无关；单用手指量寸最不可靠。

A systematic review (Godson & Wardle, 2019) found that qualified medical acupuncturists vary considerably in where they place the same point, regardless of training, experience or style; finger-widths used alone were the least reliable.

本站怎么用这份标准 · How this site uses it

每个穴位页先给国标定位，再给「先用手找」：从标志出发，指宽只作从标志出发的一小步。

Each point page gives the standard location first, then "find it with your hand": starting from landmarks, with a finger-width only as a short step from one.
