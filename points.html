穴位 · Points

穴位Points

穴位是身体上一个有名字的地方。练习时，把注意力放在这里，不用按，只是留意。按身体的位置找一个穴位，点进去，看它在哪些体式里用得上。A point is a named place on the body. In practice you don't press it; you rest your attention there. Find a point by where it sits on the body, then open it to see the poses where it helps.
