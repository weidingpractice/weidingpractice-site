两张地图，两种语言，走的是同一片林子。地图不是身体本身；灰字是还没走到的地方。Two maps in two languages, one forest. The map is not the territory; grey marks places not yet walked.

三腔The three cavities

三腔是装着内脏的腔：胸腔里主要是心和肺，腹腔里主要是脾胃、肝胆和肠，盆腔里主要是膀胱、生殖器官和直肠。穴位地图里的上焦、中焦、下焦和它们大致对应，但说的是两套语言。The three cavities hold the organs: chiefly heart and lungs in the chest, stomach, spleen, liver, gallbladder and intestines in the abdomen, bladder, reproductive organs and rectum in the pelvis. The upper, middle and lower jiao on the acupoint map roughly match them, in another language.

中医的区域TCM areas

没有编号的中医区域：丹田、三焦、八髎。

丹田Dantian · Area 区域 · area · 下腹 lower belly · 任脉 Conception Vessel✓ 有体会 · felt 手掌横放在肚脐下面，掌心对着小腹。手掌盖住的这一片，就是下丹田。它不是一个点，是一片区域。Lay your palm across your belly just below the navel. The area under your hand is the lower dantian. It is an area, not a point.上焦Shangjiao · Area 区域 · area · 胸 chest · 三焦（六腑之一） Sanjiao✓ 有体会 · felt 手掌平放在胸骨上，两乳之间。手掌盖住的胸腔这一片，就是上焦。Lay your palm flat on your breastbone, between the breasts. The chest under your hand is the upper jiao.中焦Zhongjiao · Area 区域 · area · 腹 belly · 三焦（六腑之一） Sanjiao✓ 有体会 · felt 手掌放在胸骨下端和肚脐之间，也就是上腹。这一片是中焦。Rest your palm between the bottom of your breastbone and your navel, on the upper belly. This is the middle jiao.下焦Xiajiao · Area 区域 · area · 下腹 lower belly · 三焦（六腑之一） Sanjiao✓ 有体会 · felt 手掌放在肚脐以下，一直到耻骨。这一片，连同盆腔，是下焦。Rest your palm below your navel, down to the pubic bone. This area, with the pelvis behind it, is the lower jiao.八髎Baliao · BL31–34 · 骶骨 sacrum · 膀胱经 Bladder channel○ 待试 · to try 站着，双手叉腰，拇指在后。拇指往后、往中间滑，碰到脊柱。拇指不动，手掌往下翻，贴着后腰。中指往下的这一片就是骶部，八髎都在里面。Stand with hands on hips, thumbs behind. Slide the thumbs back and in until they meet the spine. Keep the thumbs there and turn the palms down onto the lower back. The area under your middle fingers and below is the sacrum; the eight Baliao points are all in it.

肺经Lung channel

中府Zhongfu · LU1 · 肩前 front of the shoulder · 肺经 Lung channel○ 待试 · to try 右手中指，放在左边锁骨靠肩膀那一头的下面。往下一个大拇指宽，再往胸口里挪一点。那一片里最凹、最深的地方。Right middle finger under the outer end of the left collarbone, near the shoulder. Move down one thumb-width, then a little in towards the chest. The deepest, most hollow spot there.云门Yunmen · LU2 · 肩前 front of the shoulder · 肺经 Lung channel✓ 有体会 · felt 手叉腰，锁骨外端下面会出现一个窝。Hands on hips: a hollow appears just below the outer end of the collarbone.鱼际Yuji · LU10 · 掌心 palm · 肺经 Lung channel✓ 有体会 · felt 手掌朝上。大拇指下面有一根骨头，沿着它找到中间。手指在这里从手心往侧边摸过去：手心的皮肤软，翻到侧边就变硬、变紧了。软变硬的那条线上，就是。Palm up. Under the thumb runs a bone; find the middle of it. There, feel from the palm toward the side of the hand: the palm skin is soft, and as you come round the edge it turns firmer and tighter. The point is on that line where soft turns firm.

大肠经Large Intestine channel

商阳Shangyang · LI1 · 食指 index finger · 大肠经 Large Intestine channel✓ 有体会 · felt 食指伸直，指甲朝上。看指甲靠大拇指那一边、靠近手指根的那个角。同一只手的拇指指甲尖，放在这个角外面紧挨着的地方，不压在指甲上。Straighten your index finger, nail up. Look at the corner of the nail on the thumb side, nearest the base of the finger. Put the tip of the thumbnail of the same hand just outside that corner, right beside it, not on the nail.

胃经Stomach channel

气冲Qichong · ST30 · 腹股沟 groin · 胃经 Stomach channel○ 待试 · to try 平躺。中指从肚脐沿中线往下滑，滑到下腹最下面那块横着的硬骨，停在它上边。食指、中指、无名指并拢竖放，从这里往外量三个手指宽。那里能摸到一跳一跳的，就是。Lie on your back. Slide your middle finger down the midline from the navel to the hard crosswise bone at the bottom of the belly, and stop on its upper edge. Put the index, middle and ring fingers together, pointing up and down, and measure three finger-widths out from there. Where you feel a beat is the point.

脾经Spleen channel

太白Taibai · SP3 · 足内侧 inner foot · 脾经 Spleen channel✓ 有体会 · felt 坐着，看脚内侧。大脚趾根部后面有一块鼓起的骨头。从这块骨头往脚跟方向摸，刚过骨头就有一个小窝，在脚底和脚背颜色交界的那条边上。Sit and look at the inside of your foot. Behind the base of the big toe is a bony bump. Feel from it toward the heel: just past the bone is a small hollow, on the line where the colour of the sole meets the colour of the top of the foot.

冲门Chongmen · SP12 · 腹股沟 groin · 脾经 Spleen channel○ 待试 · to try 半躺或平躺，腿伸直。右手食指和中指并拢，放在右边大腿根的折痕上，从外侧骨盆那块骨头旁边开始。沿折痕慢慢往里、往下移，找到一跳一跳的地方。从跳的地方往外挪一个手指宽，还在折痕里。Lie back, legs straight. 膀胱经Bladder channel

胞肓Baohuang · BL53 · 臀 buttock · 膀胱经 Bladder channel○ 待试 · to try 站着。先找腰窝：后腰最下面，脊柱两边各有一个小凹窝。右手放到背后，手指朝下，手掌贴在屁股上半部、靠外边。手往上挪，让手腕这一头和腰窝一样高。手掌最上面靠外的这一片，胞肓就在里面。Stand. First find the dimples: at the very bottom of your lower back there is a small hollow on each side of the spine. Put your right hand behind you, fingers pointing down, palm on the upper, outer part of the buttock. Move the hand up until the wrist end is level with the dimples. Baohuang lies in the area at the top outer corner of your palm.秩边Zhibian · BL54 · 臀 buttock · 膀胱经 Bladder channel○ 待试 · to try 站着，右手像插进裤子后口袋那样，手掌贴在屁股上，手指朝下。中指尖和尾骨一样高，不要更低。手掌盖住的这一片，秩边就在里面。Stand and put your right hand as if into the back pocket of your trousers: palm on the buttock, fingers pointing down. Keep the tip of your middle finger level with your tailbone, no lower. Zhibian lies in the area under your palm.
