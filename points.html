按身体找By body place

两张地图，两种语言，走的是同一片林子。地图不是身体本身；灰字是还没走到的地方。Two maps in two languages, one forest. The map is not the territory; grey marks places not yet walked.

三腔The three cavities

三腔是装着内脏的腔：胸腔里主要是心和肺，腹腔里主要是脾胃、肝胆和肠，盆腔里主要是膀胱、生殖器官和直肠。穴位地图里的上焦、中焦、下焦和它们大致对应，但说的是两套语言。The three cavities hold the organs: chiefly heart and lungs in the chest, stomach, spleen, liver, gallbladder and intestines in the abdomen, bladder, reproductive organs and rectum in the pelvis. The upper, middle and lower jiao on the acupoint map roughly match them, in another language.

湖与谷Lakes and valleys

藏是湖，府是谷。湖和谷是我们的说法，原文里没有这两个字。每一行是一湖一谷，两条经把它们连起来：一条属这个、络那个，另一条倒过来。原文据《灵枢·经脉》：肺经、大肠经、胃经、脾经、心经、小肠经据元刻本原页，其余据排印本与《类经》卷七对读，元刻本待核。A zang organ is a lake, a fu organ a valley: our images, not words from the classic. Each row is a lake and its valley, joined by two channels, each belonging to one and linking with the other. Text from the Lingshu, "Jingmai": the Lung, Large Intestine, Stomach, Spleen, Heart and Small Intestine channels read on the Yuan woodblock page, the rest from modern editions, still to be checked against it.

地方Places

经络原文里走过的地方，用原文的词，旁边写今天的说法。标「里」的在身体里面，后面写着是哪条经经过。现在有肺经、大肠经、胃经、脾经、心经、小肠经，以后每画一条经，就把它的地方加进来。The places the channels pass, in the classic's own words, with today's words beside them. Those marked "inside" lie within the body; each names the channels that pass it. So far the Lung, Large Intestine, Stomach, Spleen, Heart and Small Intestine channels; each new channel will add its own.
