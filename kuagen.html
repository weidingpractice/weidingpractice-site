胯根

胯根 Kua root

Area · Kua root · inner groin · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

这是一个区域，不是标准穴位，国家标准和 WHO 标准里都没有它的定位。下面的找法是这套笔记自己的写法。This is an area, not a standard acupoint; neither the Chinese national standard nor the WHO standard defines its location. The directions below are these notes' own.

用手怎么找
坐着，一条腿屈膝外放。手指放在大腿根和小腹相接的折痕下面一点，往里、往深处，能摸到一个凹下去的窝。先在折痕上找到股动脉的搏动：搏动外侧是冲门，内侧那块软的是耻骨肌；顺着凹槽往膝盖方向，是箕门。整片就是胯根。Sit with one knee bent and dropped out to the side. Put your fingers just below the crease where the thigh meets the belly, and feel inward and deep for a hollow. First find the pulse of the femoral artery in the crease: Chongmen lies just outside it, and the soft muscle just inside it is the pectineus; follow the groove down towards the knee and you reach Jimen. The whole area is the kua root.不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.找到了吗？Did you find it?

这是什么What it is

是一片区域，不是一个穴位 这个窝就是你在《胯根》里写的胯根：大腿根前内侧，腹股沟下面凹下去的那一片，每条腿各一个。穴位是点，这里是一片面。冲门、箕门、急脉这些穴位都落在这一片里，但不能说「这个窝就是某个穴」。An area, not a point This hollow is the kua root: the dip at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease, one on each leg. An acupoint is a point; this is an area. Chongmen, Jimen and Jimai all sit inside it, but the hollow itself is not any one of them.

解剖上叫股三角 上边是腹股沟韧带，外边是缝匠肌，里边是长收肌，中间凹下去。底下由外往内是髂肌、腰大肌、耻骨肌，像一把扇子。髂腰肌从这里的深处穿过腹股沟韧带下面，经过髋关节前内侧，止于股骨小转子；它的上端连着腰椎。所以「这一块把整个身体连起来了」，在解剖上有对应：这里是腿和脊柱接在一起的地方。Anatomically: the femoral triangle The groin ligament forms the top edge, the sartorius the outer edge, the adductor longus the inner edge, and the area between them dips in. Its floor, from outside in, is the iliacus, the psoas and the pectineus, spread like a fan. The iliopsoas passes deep through here, under the groin ligament and across the front of the hip joint, to the lesser trochanter of the femur; its upper end attaches to the lumbar spine. So the sense that this area joins the whole body has an anatomical match: here the leg and the spine meet.

和环跳隔着髋关节相对 胯根在髋关节的前内侧，环跳在髋关节的后外侧，两点隔着股骨头相对。所以「注意力点要成对」在一条腿上也成立：不是左右成对，是内外成对。Facing Huantiao across the hip joint The kua root sits at the front and inside of the hip joint; Huantiao sits at the back and outside. The two face each other across the head of the femur. So "attention points come in pairs" holds on a single leg too: not left and right, but inside and outside.

在力线里 站立力线是一串窝：涌泉 → 太白（足弓）→ 膝盖内侧 → 胯根 → 环跳。这条线不是直的，是从腿的内侧往上，绕过髋关节，到外后方。In the line of force The standing line is a chain of hollows: Yongquan → Taibai (the arch) → the inner knee → the kua root → Huantiao. It isn't straight: it rises along the inside of the leg, wraps around the hip joint and ends at the back and outside.

怎么放注意力 这里有股动脉和股神经经过。只放注意力，不用力按。How to rest your attention here The femoral artery and nerve pass through here. Rest your attention; don't press.

原文Classical source

《内经》叫这里「髀枢」：大腿从骨盆这里转动：The Neijing calls this place the bi shu, the pivot where the thigh turns from the pelvis:

《灵枢·骨度》季胁以下至髀枢，长六寸，髀枢以下至膝中，长一尺九寸。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：髀枢：大腿和骨盆相接、转动的地方。连着 · Linked to：胯根 Area

髋的两旁是「机」，转动的关键：Beside the hips lies the ji, the hinge on which movement turns:

《素问·骨空论》侠髋为机。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：机：枢机、转动之处。连着 · Linked to：胯根 Area

《难经》的说法：下焦「主出而不内」。后一句「其府在气街」，气街有两种解法，其中一种就在腹股沟、胯根这一带：The Classic of Difficulties: the lower jiao "lets out and never takes in". The next line places the Sanjiao's seat at the qi street, which can be read two ways; one of them lies in the groin, where the kua root is:

《难经·三十一难》下焦者，当膀胱上口，主分别清浊，主出而不内，以传道也。其治在脐下一寸。故名曰三焦，其府在气街。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：「气街」有二义：一指《灵枢·卫气》「四街」的通道（胸、腹、头、胫各有气街）；一指气冲穴，在毛际两旁，也就是腹股沟、胯根这一带。而且「其府在气街」紧接「故名曰三焦」，说的很可能是三焦整体，不专指下焦。连着 · Linked to：下焦 Area · 胯根 Area
