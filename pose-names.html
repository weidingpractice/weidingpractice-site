体式名称对照Pose names: Chinese, English, Sanskrit


中文、英文、梵文对齐，梵文用标准转写（IAST）并附字面词义，词义依 Monier-Williams 梵英词典。阴瑜伽的名字（鞍式、蝴蝶式、蜗牛式）大多是英文俗名，不是另一套梵文。Chinese, English and Sanskrit side by side. Sanskrit is in standard transliteration (IAST) with a literal gloss, checked against the Monier-Williams Sanskrit–English Dictionary. Yin names (Saddle, Butterfly, Snail) are mostly English common names, not a second set of Sanskrit.

猫牛式Cat–Cow Mārjaryāsana – Bitilāsana猫在梵文作 mārjāra（Monier-Williams 词典），体式名里的 marjarya 和它词形不完全一致。bitila 不见于 Monier-Williams 梵英词典，词义待考。Cat in Sanskrit is mārjāra (Monier-Williams); the marjarya in the pose name does not match it exactly. Bitila does not appear in the Monier-Williams dictionary; its meaning is unknown.猫牛式的梵文名在现代用法里相当混乱，各家对应不一，词源请以存疑看待。The Sanskrit names for Cat–Cow are muddled in modern use and sources disagree; treat any etymology with caution.穿针式Thread the Needle Pārśva Bālāsanapārśva 侧；bāla 孩子pārśva side; bāla child字面是「侧婴儿式」。穿针式常被当作它的变体或异名，各流派用法不一。Literally "side child's pose". Thread the Needle is often treated as a variant or another name for it; usage varies by school.
