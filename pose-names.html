体式名称 · Pose names

体式名称对照Pose names: Chinese, English, Sanskrit

中文、英文、梵文对齐。梵文用标准转写（IAST），附字面词义，词义依 Monier-Williams 梵英词典。Chinese, English and Sanskrit side by side. Sanskrit is in standard transliteration (IAST) with a literal gloss, checked against the Monier-Williams Sanskrit–English Dictionary.

阴瑜伽的名字（鞍式、蝴蝶式、蜗牛式）大多是英文俗名，不是另一套梵文。名字下面有链接的，是这些笔记里有详细页面的体式。Yin names (Saddle, Butterfly, Snail) are mostly English common names, not a second set of Sanskrit. A linked name has its own page in these notes.


猫牛式Cat–Cow

Mārjaryāsana – Bitilāsana

猫在梵文作 mārjāra（Monier-Williams 词典），体式名里的 marjarya 和它词形不完全一致。bitila 不见于 Monier-Williams 梵英词典，词义待考。

Cat in Sanskrit is mārjāra (Monier-Williams); the marjarya in the pose name does not match it exactly. Bitila does not appear in the Monier-Williams dictionary; its meaning is unknown.

猫牛式的梵文名在现代用法里相当混乱，各家对应不一，词源请以存疑看待。The Sanskrit names for Cat–Cow are muddled in modern use and sources disagree; treat any etymology with caution.

穿针式Thread the Needle

Pārśva Bālāsana

pārśva 侧；bāla 孩子

pārśva side; bāla child

字面是「侧婴儿式」。穿针式常被当作它的变体或异名，各流派用法不一。Literally "side child's pose". Thread the Needle is often treated as a variant or another name for it; usage varies by school.

下犬式Downward-Facing Dog

Adho Mukha Śvānāsana

adho（adhas）向下；mukha 脸；śvāna 狗

adho (adhas) downward; mukha face; śvāna dog

常见错写成 Śvanāsana（短 a），应为长音 ā。Often misspelt Śvanāsana with a short a; the vowel is long: ā.

侧蹲Skandasana · Side Lunge

skanda, the god of war (Kārttikeya); another reading derives it from skandha (shoulder). The two words differ by one h

现代名字，来源无定论。同一个名字在别的体系里可能指别的动作。A modern name with no settled origin. The same name may mean a different pose in other systems.

阴瑜伽名 · Yin name：鞍式 · Saddle

名称对应，但阴瑜伽的鞍式常允许膝盖分开、用支撑物，做法因老师而异。The names correspond, but Yin Saddle often allows the knees apart and props; practice varies by teacher.

束角式Bound Angle

Baddha Koṇāsana

baddha 束缚的；koṇa 角

baddha bound; koṇa angle

阴瑜伽名 · Yin name：蝴蝶式 · Butterfly

脚位相近。阴瑜伽的蝴蝶式脚跟离身体更远，重点在长时间前屈。The foot position is similar. In Yin Butterfly the heels sit further from the body and the point is a long forward fold.
