膝蓋內側

膝盖内侧 Inner knee

Area · Inner knee · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

这是一个区域，不是标准穴位，国家标准和 WHO 标准里都没有它的定位。下面的找法是这套笔记自己的写法。This is an area, not a standard acupoint; neither the Chinese national standard nor the WHO standard defines its location. The directions below are these notes' own.

用手怎么找
坐着，翘起二郎腿。用对侧的手盖住上面那条腿的膝盖，掌心正对膝盖骨。手自然地盖上去，大拇指根那块肉下面，膝盖骨内侧边上，会摸到一个窝。Sit and cross one leg over the other. Cover the top knee with the opposite hand, palm right over the kneecap. Let it rest there naturally: under the fleshy pad at the base of your thumb, beside the inner edge of the kneecap, you will feel a hollow.不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

是一片地方，不是穴位 膝盖骨内侧边上的这个窝，国标里没有穴位。往上大约三指宽、大腿内侧的肉上，是血海。A place, not a point This hollow beside the inner edge of the kneecap is not a point in the national standard. About three finger-widths higher, on the inner thigh, is Xuehai.

经络Channel

脾经Spleen channel

从大脚趾内侧起，沿腿内侧往上，经腹部，到侧胸（大包）。

From the inside of the big toe, up the inside of the leg, through the belly, to the side of the chest (Dabao).
《灵枢·经脉》脾之大络，名曰大包，出渊腋下三寸，布胸胁。

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.
