膝盖内侧 Inner knee

一片 · Inner knee · 脾经 Spleen channel地图 · Map：日常身体 Everyday body

在别的地图上On other maps

你知道它在哪里。同一片身体，在别的地图上叫这些名字。You know where it is. On other maps, the same stretch of body goes by these names.

医经 脾、肝、肾三条经，在膝盖和大腿内侧并排往上走（《灵枢·经脉》）。膝盖附近有名字的穴位：膝上是血海 SP10；膝下是阴陵泉 SP9；膝内侧、腘横纹的内端是曲泉 LR8；膝后内侧是阴谷 KI10。定位照 GB/T 12346-2021。Medical classics The Spleen, Liver and Kidney channels run side by side up the inner knee and thigh (Lingshu, "Jingmai"). Named points near the knee: Xuehai SP10 above it; Yinlingquan SP9 below it; Ququan LR8 at the inner end of the knee crease; Yingu KI10 at the back of the inner knee. Locations as in GB/T 12346-2021.
