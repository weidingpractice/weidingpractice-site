胞肓 Baohuang

BL53 · Bladder Vitals · 膀胱经 Bladder channel

标准定位Standard location

在臀部，横平第 2 骶后孔，骶正中嵴旁开 3 寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》

In the buttock region, at the same level as the second posterior sacral foramen, 3 B-cun lateral to the median sacral crest.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · checked against the WHO text
