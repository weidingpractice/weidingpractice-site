胞肓

胞肓 Baohuang

BL53 · Bladder Vitals · 膀胱经 Bladder channel

标准定位Standard location

在臀部，横平第2骶后孔，骶正中嵴旁开3寸。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.7.53 条，第 19 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.7.53, p. 19 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the buttock region, at the same level as the second posterior sacral foramen, 3 B-cun lateral to the median sacral crest.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：本穴与内侧的膀胱俞（BL28）、次髎（BL32）均位于第2骶后孔水平。The standard's own note: This point, Pangguangshu (BL28) and Ciliao (BL32) on the inside all lie level with the second posterior sacral foramen.

用手怎么找
站着。先找腰窝：后腰最下面，脊柱两边各有一个小凹窝。右手放到背后，手指朝下，手掌贴在屁股上半部、靠外边。手往上挪，让手腕这一头和腰窝一样高。手掌最上面靠外的这一片，胞肓就在里面。Stand. First find the dimples: at the very bottom of your lower back there is a small hollow on each side of the spine. Put your right hand behind you, fingers pointing down, palm on the upper, outer part of the buttock. Move the hand up until the wrist end is level with the dimples. Baohuang lies in the area at the top outer corner of your palm.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

经络Channel

膀胱经Bladder channel

从内眼角起，上头顶，沿脊柱两侧往下，经骶骨（八髎），沿腿后面到小脚趾外侧。

From the inner corner of the eye, over the crown, down both sides of the spine, through the sacrum (Baliao), down the back of the leg to the outer edge of the little toe.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.
