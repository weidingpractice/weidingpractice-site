三角扭转式Revolved Triangle

Parivṛtta Trikoṇāsana · 名称对照 · Pose names →

劳宫放在骶骨上，和前面的肚脐成对。Rest Laogong on the sacrum and pair it with the navel in front.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

劳宫 Laogong
PC8
有体会 · felt

Make a loose fist: where the tip of your middle finger meets the palm.

Hand on the flat triangular bone just above the buttocks: four small hollows on each side, eight in all.

怎么做How I practise it

劳宫放在骶骨上，不是命门。我把手的劳宫放在骶骨上。昨天也试了命门，放命门太高了，会让我的肩膀不舒服。
Laogong on the sacrum, not Mingmen.I rest the Laogong of my hand on the sacrum. Yesterday I tried Mingmen too; it sits too high and makes my shoulder uncomfortable.
其实是一个呼吸练习。放在骶骨上，感觉做的其实是一个呼吸练习，你很平静，你会知道自己的臀部是 aligned，两条腿也在对的方向。
It is really a breathing practice.With the hand on the sacrum, it becomes a breathing practice. You grow calm, and you know your hips are aligned and both legs point the right way.
前后成对。劳宫放在骶骨上面，再和前面的肚脐成对，这样呼吸的时候你就会觉得前后对上了。除了左右，身体还有前后。
Front and back, in a pair.Rest Laogong on the sacrum and pair it with the navel in front; as you breathe, front and back line up. The body has front and back, not only left and right.
幅度永远不是重点。你可以转得深，也可以转得浅，这跟你的身体条件、练习的时间长度有关。我们练的永远不是幅度，而是觉知、呼吸的深度，呼吸到骶骨。
Range is never the point.You can twist deep or shallow; that depends on your body and how long you have practised. You never practise range. You practise awareness and the depth of the breath, breathing down into the sacrum.
