上焦 Shangjiao

Area · Upper jiao · 三焦（六腑之一） The Sanjiao, one of the six fu

标准定位Standard location

这是一个区域，不是标准穴位，国家标准和 WHO 标准里都没有它的定位。下面的找法是这套笔记自己的写法。This is an area, not a standard acupoint; neither the Chinese national standard nor the WHO standard defines its location. The directions below are these notes' own.

用手怎么找
手掌平放在胸骨上，两乳之间。手掌盖住的胸腔这一片，就是上焦。Lay your palm flat on your breastbone, between the breasts. The chest under your hand is the upper jiao.不用按，只是把注意力放在这一片。You don't press; you rest your attention on the whole area.

这是什么What it is

三焦是什么 《灵枢·本输》叫三焦「孤之腑」。五脏只有五个，六腑却有六个，多出来的那一个就是三焦；后世把心包配给它，凑成表里，但心包不算一个独立的脏。它是一套通道，管水、管气，分上中下三段。经文用「膈」分开上焦和中焦；中焦和下焦之间，经文没有给结构上的边界，是按功能分的。三焦经是另一回事：一条从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角的线。What the Sanjiao is The Lingshu calls the Sanjiao the "solitary fu". There are five zang organs but six fu, and the Sanjiao is the sixth, the one left over; later tradition paired it with the pericardium, which is not a full zang organ in its own right. It is a set of passages for water and qi, in three sections. The classics divide the upper from the middle jiao at the diaphragm; between the middle and lower jiao they give no structural boundary, only a functional one. The Sanjiao channel is something else: a line from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.

上焦如雾 经文说上焦「贯膈而布胸中」，在膈以上，心和肺在这里。它的样子像雾：「上焦开发……若雾露之溉」，气往上、往外散开。Like mist The classics place the upper jiao above the diaphragm, spread through the chest, where the heart and lungs sit. It works like mist: it opens and spreads outward and upward, "like a mist of dew that waters everything".

和现代解剖对照 大致是胸腔。膈肌是躯干里唯一一道横跨整个体腔、把它分成上下两部分的肌性隔板，也正是经文分上焦和中焦的地方。手掌盖到的只是胸前这一片；上焦比手掌深，也比手掌大，手只是一个入口。Next to modern anatomy Roughly the chest cavity. The diaphragm is the one muscular sheet that spans the whole trunk and divides it into upper and lower parts, and it is exactly where the classics divide the upper jiao from the middle. Your palm covers only the front of the chest; the upper jiao is deeper and wider than your hand, which is only a way in.

怎么放注意力 用雾的词：开不开、散不散。不用「饱满」「聚」，那是丹田的词。自测一句：能不能开。后弯时注意胸这一片能不能打开，有没有哪里卡住。How to rest your attention here Use the words of mist: does it open, does it spread. Leave "full" and "gathered" to the dantian. One question to test it: does it open? In backbends, notice whether the chest opens and where it catches.

原文Classical source

上焦的位置：穿过膈，布满胸中：Where the upper jiao lies: through the diaphragm and across the chest:

《灵枢·营卫生会》上焦出于胃上口，并咽以上，贯膈而布胸中。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area

上焦的气和营气一起走，一天一夜五十周：它是一个过程，不只是一片地方：The qi of the upper jiao travels with the nutritive qi, fifty circuits in a day and a night: a process, not just a place:

《灵枢·营卫生会》常与营俱行于阳二十五度，行于阴亦二十五度，一周也。故五十度而复大会于手太阴矣。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area

上焦像雾露一样散开：The upper jiao spreads like mist and dew:

《灵枢·决气》上焦开发，宣五谷味，熏肤、充身、泽毛，若雾露之溉，是谓气。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area

三焦的三个样子：雾、沤、渎：Three images of the Sanjiao: mist, steeping, and a drainage ditch:

《灵枢·营卫生会》上焦如雾，中焦如沤，下焦如渎。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：三焦讲的是过程：散、泡、排。和丹田「田、鼎」的容器语汇不同。连着 · Linked to：丹田 Dantian Area · 上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

三焦是通水道的官：The Sanjiao is the official who keeps the waterways open:

《素问·灵兰秘典论》三焦者，决渎之官，水道出焉。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

「孤之腑」：三焦没有配对的脏：The "solitary fu": the Sanjiao has no zang organ to pair with:

《灵枢·本输》三焦者，中渎之腑也，水道出焉，属膀胱，是孤之腑也。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

三焦的气一直到肌肉和皮肤。读到这句，我想到的是：这正是体式碰得到的地方：The qi of the Sanjiao reaches the muscles and skin. Reading this, I think of where practice can meet it:

《灵枢·五癃津液别》三焦出气，以温肌肉，充皮肤，为其津。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

这一条讲三焦整体，不是哪一焦：三焦和膀胱，在体表对应腠理毫毛：This line is about the Sanjiao as a whole, not any one jiao: the Sanjiao and bladder show on the surface, in the skin and fine hair:

《灵枢·本藏》肾合三焦膀胱，三焦膀胱者，腠理毫毛其应。连着 · Linked to：上焦 Shangjiao Area · 中焦 Zhongjiao Area · 下焦 Xiajiao Area

经络Channel

三焦（六腑之一）The Sanjiao, one of the six fu

三焦是腑，不是经络。它的经络是三焦经，另作一条：从无名指端，经手臂外侧、肩、颈侧、耳后，到眼外角。

The Sanjiao is a fu organ, not a channel. Its channel is the Sanjiao channel, a separate line: from the tip of the ring finger, up the outside of the arm, over the shoulder and side of the neck, behind the ear, to the outer corner of the eye.

这条路还在修。This path is still being built.
