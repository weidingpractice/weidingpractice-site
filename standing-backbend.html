站立下轮Standing drop-back to Wheel

Urdhva Dhanurasana（从站立下）

涌泉、太溪、血海，再到腹股沟前面。

怎么做How I practise it

后弯的力线。站立下轮的力线是从涌泉到太溪到血海，再到腹股沟前面的那个穴位。这个力线又对了，而且都是成对出现的。
The backbend line.In a standing drop-back, the line runs from Yongquan to Taixi to Xuehai, then to the point at the front of the groin. The line works again, and every point comes in a pair.

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

胯
胯根 Kuagen
区域 · Area
有体会 · felt

坐着，一条腿屈膝外放。手指放在大腿根和小腹相接的折痕下面一点，往里、往深处，能摸到一个凹下去的窝。先在折痕上找到股动脉的搏动：搏动外侧是冲门，内侧那块软的是耻骨肌；顺着凹槽往膝盖方向，是箕门。整片就是胯根。

Sit with one knee bent and dropped out to the side. Put your fingers just below the crease where the thigh meets the belly, and feel inward and deep for a hollow. First find the pulse of the femoral artery in the crease: Chongmen lies just outside it, and the soft muscle just inside it is the pectineus; follow the groove down towards the knee and you reach Jimen. The whole area is the kua root.

ST30
可能 · possible

摸到耻骨上缘的正中，从那里往外量三指宽，正好在动脉跳动的地方。

Find the top edge of the pubic bone at the centre, then measure three finger-widths out to the side, where you feel the pulse.
