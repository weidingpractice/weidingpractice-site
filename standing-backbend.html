体式 · Pose

站立下轮Standing drop-back to Wheel

Ūrdhva Dhanurāsana（从站立下） · 名称对照 · Pose names →

涌泉、太溪、血海，再到腹股沟前面。Yongquan, Taixi, Xuehai, then the front of the groin.

形 · 4 双脚 · 从高到低：站直 → 站桩 → 弓步 → 蹲Shape · 4 Two feet · High to low: standing, zhan zhuang, lunge, squat

这个体式里的穴位Points in this pose

位置都写成手的动作，跟着做就能找到。不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。 Every location is a hand action you can follow. You don't press; you rest your attention there.

涌
涌泉 Yongquan
KI1
有体会 · felt

脚趾往下蜷，脚底前面那块肉的正后方会凹下去一个小窝。

Curl your toes: the small hollow just behind the ball of the foot.

太
太溪 Taixi
KI3
有体会 · felt

脚腕内侧有一块凸起的骨头。从它往脚后跟方向摸，碰到那根粗粗的筋之前，中间有个小窝。

From the bump on the inside of your ankle, slide back towards the heel: the hollow before the thick cord.

血
血海 Xuehai
SP10
有体会 · felt

坐着弯膝。右手掌盖住左膝盖骨，拇指斜向大腿内侧，拇指尖落下的地方。

Seated, knee bent: cup your right palm over your left kneecap; the tip of your thumb points to it on the inner thigh.

胯
胯根 Kua root
Area
有体会 · felt

大腿根前内侧、腹股沟折痕下面凹下去的窝。只放注意力，不用力按。

The hollow at the front of the inner thigh, just below the groin crease. Rest your attention; don't press.

上
上焦 Shangjiao
Area
有体会 · felt

手掌平放在胸骨上，两乳之间。手掌盖住的胸腔这一片，就是上焦。

Lay your palm flat on your breastbone, between the breasts. The chest under your hand is the upper jiao.

冲
冲门 Chongmen
SP12
可能 · possible

手指放在大腿根前面的折痕上，先摸到股动脉在跳的地方，在它的外侧。

Rest your fingers in the crease at the front of the hip, find where the femoral artery beats, and move just to its outer side.

气
气冲 Qichong
ST30
可能 · possible

摸到耻骨上缘的正中，从那里往外量三指宽，正好在动脉跳动的地方。

Find the top edge of the pubic bone at the centre, then measure three finger-widths out to the side, where you feel the pulse.

怎么做How I practise it

后弯的力线。站立下轮的力线是从涌泉到太溪到血海，再到腹股沟前面的那个穴位。这个力线又对了，而且都是成对出现的。
The backbend line.In a standing drop-back, the line runs from Yongquan to Taixi to Xuehai, then to the point at the front of the groin. The line works again, and every point comes in a pair.
