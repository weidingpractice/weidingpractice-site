風府

风府 Fengfu

GV16 · Wind Palace · 督脉 Governing Vessel

标准定位Standard location

在颈后部，枕外隆凸直下，两侧斜方肌之间凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.13.16 条，第 28 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.13.16, p. 28 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the posterior region of the neck, directly inferior to the external occipital protuberance, in the depression between the trapezius muscles.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：注：正坐，头稍仰，使项部斜方肌松弛，从项后发际正中上推至枕骨而止即是本穴。The standard's own note: Note: sit upright with the head tilted slightly back so the trapezius relaxes; push up from the middle of the hairline at the back of the neck until the occipital bone stops you: that is the point.

用手怎么找
一只手，中指指腹放在脖子后面发际线的正中，往上推。推到后脑勺那块骨头挡住手指的地方，手指下面那个小凹陷就是风府。One hand: put the pad of your middle finger on the hairline at the middle of the back of your neck and push upwards. Where the bone at the back of your skull stops the finger, the small hollow under it is Fengfu.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.

从尾骨下方起，沿脊柱正中往上，到后颈，进入头部。

From below the tailbone, up the midline of the spine to the back of the neck, and into the head.
《难经·二十八难》督脉者，起于下极之俞，并于脊里，上至风府，入属于脑。
