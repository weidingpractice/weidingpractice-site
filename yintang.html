印堂Yintang · GV24+ · Hall of Impression

标准定位Standard location

在头部，两眉毛内侧端中间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》，已对原文

On the head, in the depression between the medial ends of the two eyebrows.Not in the WHO 2008 standard; the English is our own translation.

注：印堂原为经外奇穴，2006 年版国标归入督脉，编号 GV29；2021 年版改为 GV24+（神庭之后新增）。WHO 2008 标准只收 361 个经穴，没有印堂，所以这里的英文是自译。标准原注：左右攒竹（BL2）连线的中点。Note: Yintang was originally an extra point. The 2006 Chinese standard placed it on the Governing Vessel as GV29; the 2021 edition renumbered it GV24+ (added after Shenting, GV24). The WHO 2008 standard covers 361 channel points and does not include Yintang, so the English here is our own translation. The Chinese standard adds: the midpoint of the line between left and right Cuanzhu (BL2).
