印堂

印堂 Yintang

GV24+ · Hall of Impression · 督脉 Governing Vessel

标准定位Standard location

在头部，两眉毛内侧端中间的凹陷中。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.13.25 条，第 29 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.13.25, p. 29 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the head, in the depression between the medial ends of the two eyebrows.Not in the WHO 2008 standard; the English is our own translation.

国标原注：左右攒竹（BL2）连线的中点。The standard's own note: The midpoint of the line between left and right Cuanzhu (BL2).

我们的说明：印堂原为经外奇穴，2006 年版国标归入督脉，编号 GV29；2021 年版改为 GV24+（神庭之后新增）。WHO 2008 标准只收 361 个经穴，没有印堂，所以这里的英文是自译。Our note: Yintang was originally an extra point. The 2006 Chinese standard placed it on the Governing Vessel as GV29; the 2021 edition renumbered it GV24+ (added after Shenting, GV24). The WHO 2008 standard covers 361 channel points and does not include Yintang, so the English here is our own translation.

用手怎么找
两眉头连线的正中。Midway between the inner ends of the eyebrows.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
