命門

命门 Mingmen

GV4 · Gate of Life · 督脉 Governing Vessel

标准定位Standard location

在腰部，第2腰椎棘突下凹陷中，后正中线上。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.13.4 条，第 28 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.13.4, p. 28 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the lumbar region, in the depression inferior to the spinous process of the second lumbar vertebra (L2), on the posterior median line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
先找到肚脐。从肚脐正对着往后，到后腰脊柱的正中。用中指指肚摸，正对的地方有一个凹陷。Find your navel first. Go straight back from it to the middle of the spine in your lower back. Feel with the pad of your middle finger: right there is a small hollow.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.
