肩窝 Shoulder hollow

一片 · Shoulder hollow · 肺经 Lung channel地图 · Map：日常身体 Everyday body

在别的地图上On other maps

你知道它在哪里。同一片身体，在别的地图上叫这些名字。You know where it is. On other maps, the same stretch of body goes by these names.

医经 云门 LU2 在锁骨下窝的凹陷里；中府 LU1 在它外侧，横平第 1 肋间隙。定位照 GB/T 12346-2021。Medical classics Yunmen LU2 lies in the hollow below the collarbone; Zhongfu LU1 lies just outside it, level with the first intercostal space. Locations as in GB/T 12346-2021.

这是什么What it is

是一片区域，不是一个穴位 云门、中府都落在这一片里，但练习里感受到的是整个窝，不是其中某一个点。An area, not a point Yunmen and Zhongfu both lie inside it, but in practice what you feel is the whole hollow, not one point in it.

在哪些体式里Poses

下犬式Downward Dog怎么知道没有卡住？首先，力是使得上的；然后到肩胛骨这边，不管是上回旋、内回旋还是怎么样，肩窝这块地方是空的，关节不能卡住。感受到肩窝没有被卡住，就可以了。How do you know nothing is stuck? First, you feel the force flowing through, unbroken, the way a river runs. Then at the shoulder blade, whether it is rotating upward, inward or any other way, the hollow at the front of the shoulder (around Yunmen, LU2) feels empty. The joint isn't jammed. If you can feel the shoulder hollow isn't stuck, that is enough.✓ 有体会 · felt四柱支撑Chaturanga真的不是方向的问题，而是要把觉知放在肩窝，呼吸要到肩窝。可能不只是云门，还有更深一点的点。It really isn't about direction. Rest your attention in the shoulder hollow and breathe into it. It may be more than Yunmen; there is a deeper spot too.✓ 有体会 · felt
