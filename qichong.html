气冲 Qichong

ST30 · Surging Qi · 胃经 Stomach channel

标准定位Standard location

在腹股沟，耻骨联合上缘，前正中线旁开2寸，动脉搏动处。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.3.30 条，第 11 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.3.30, p. 11 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the groin region, at the same level as the superior border of the pubic symphysis, 2 B-cun lateral to the anterior median line, over the femoral artery.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：天枢（ST25）下5寸，曲骨（CV2）旁开2寸。The standard's own note: 5 cun below Tianshu (ST25), 2 cun lateral to Qugu (CV2).

我们的说明：中文标准只写「动脉搏动处」，WHO 写明是股动脉（femoral artery）。Our note: The Chinese standard says only "where the artery pulses"; WHO names it as the femoral artery.

用手怎么找
平躺。中指从肚脐沿中线往下滑，滑到下腹最下面那块横着的硬骨，停在它上边。食指、中指、无名指并拢竖放，从这里往外量三个手指宽。那里能摸到一跳一跳的，就是。Lie on your back. Slide your middle finger down the midline from the navel to the hard crosswise bone at the bottom of the belly, and stop on its upper edge. Put the index, middle and ring fingers together, pointing up and down, and measure three finger-widths out from there. Where you feel a beat is the point.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.找到了吗？Did you find it?
