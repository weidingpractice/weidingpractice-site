冲门 Chongmen

SP12 · Surging Gate · 脾经 Spleen channel

标准定位Standard location

在腹股沟，腹股沟斜纹中，髂外动脉搏动处的外侧。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.4.12 条，第 13 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.4.12, p. 13 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

In the groin region, at the inguinal crease, lateral to the femoral artery.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

国标原注：横平曲骨（CV2），府舍（SP13）稍内下方。The standard's own note: Level with Qugu (CV2), slightly medial and inferior to Fushe (SP13).

我们的说明：中文标准写「髂外动脉」，WHO 写 femoral artery（股动脉）。是同一条动脉：髂外动脉过了腹股沟韧带就叫股动脉。Our note: The Chinese standard names the external iliac artery; WHO names the femoral artery. They are one vessel: the external iliac artery becomes the femoral artery below the inguinal ligament.

用手怎么找
半躺或平躺，腿伸直。右手食指和中指并拢，放在右边大腿根的折痕上，从外侧骨盆那块骨头旁边开始。沿折痕慢慢往里、往下移，找到一跳一跳的地方。从跳的地方往外挪一个手指宽，还在折痕里。Lie back, legs straight. Put your right index and middle fingers together on the crease at the top of your right thigh, beside the hip bone. Slide along the crease, inward and down, until you feel a beat. Then move one finger-width outward, still in the crease.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.找到了吗？Did you find it?
