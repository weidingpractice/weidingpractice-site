标准定位Standard location

在上腹部，脐中央。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.14.8 条，第 30 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.14.8, p. 30 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the upper abdomen, in the centre of the umbilicus.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

我们的说明：中国标准和 WHO 都把肚脐算在「上腹部」。Our note: Both the Chinese standard and WHO place the navel in the upper abdomen.

任脉Conception Vessel

从耻骨下方起，沿身体前面正中往上，经肚脐、胸口、喉咙，到下巴，再到眼睛下面。

From below the pubic bone, up the midline of the front of the body, through the navel, chest and throat, to the chin and below the eyes.
《素问·骨空论》任脉者，起于中极之下，以上毛际，循腹里，上关元，至咽喉，上颐循面入目。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：异文：「上颐循面入目」六字，《医学原始》校记称《难经》《甲乙经》无。以后写任脉和面部、眼睛的关系，要先说明这一处。
