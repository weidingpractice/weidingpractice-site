曲骨 Qugu

CV2 · Curved Bone · 任脉 Conception Vessel

标准定位Standard location

在下腹部，耻骨联合上缘，前正中线上。出处：GB/T 12346-2021《经穴名称与定位》 第 5.14.2 条，第 29 页 · AGB/T 12346-2021, clause 5.14.2, p. 29 · grade A: read word for word in the standard

On the lower abdomen, superior to the pubic symphysis, on the anterior median line.Source: WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region (2008) · page in the WHO text not yet checked

用手怎么找
中指从肚脐沿着中线往下滑。滑到下腹最下面那块横着的硬骨。它上边缘的正中间。Slide your middle finger down the midline from the navel. Stop at the hard bone at the bottom of the belly. The centre of its upper edge.不用按，只是把注意力放在那里。You don't press; you rest your attention there.找到了吗？Did you find it?

任脉Conception Vessel

从耻骨下方起，沿身体前面正中往上，经肚脐、胸口、喉咙，到下巴，再到眼睛下面。

From below the pubic bone, up the midline of the front of the body, through the navel, chest and throat, to the chin and below the eyes.
《素问·骨空论》任脉者，起于中极之下，以上毛际，循腹里，上关元，至咽喉，上颐循面入目。校注 · Textual note (Chinese)：异文：「上颐循面入目」六字，《医学原始》校记称《难经》《甲乙经》无。以后写任脉和面部、眼睛的关系，要先说明这一处。
